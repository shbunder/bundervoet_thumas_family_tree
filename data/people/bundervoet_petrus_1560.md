The earliest documented ancestor of the Bundervoet line (Evergem, ~1560); married twice and was the father of Livinus.

CORRECTION (July 2026) to the note recorded a pass earlier. The full-text find was written
up from search snippets as "a Catherine Bundervoet widowed at Meirelbeke in 1587" and a fief
chain of 1687-1700. Reading the actual page ([[bundervoet-fiefs-alost-1687-1701]]) shows the
snippets had merged two adjacent entries: the Meirelbeke 1587 one belongs to the surname VAN
DEN BUNDERE, not Bundervoet.

What the page does show is a Bundervoet family at **Saint-Pierre-Alost**, holding fiefs from
**1687 to 1701** — Catherine as widow and guardian in 1687, then Liévin son of François and
Liévin son of Pasquier in 1700, and Pasquier son of Liévin in 1701. Descent stated, in the
right century, some 30 km from Evergem. A lead for objective 3, and still not a link.

Daughter of [[lucien_vincke]] and [[ludovica_vanald]], and [[emma_vincke]]'s sister. Her
birth — Diksmuide act nr. 206, 7 October 1873 — is one of seven Diksmuide rows in the
vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index (`rab-bs-geboorten`) naming both parents as Vincke Lucien
Julianus x Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria. Index only, not the register image, hence
`sup`.

**One capture of this act renders the father as "Lucien Julien" rather than "Lucien
Julianus"** — every other act in this cluster, and his own record, give him the latter.
Accepted here as a French/Latin variant of the same forename, not a different father:
the mother's rarer full name, the commune and the chronology (she was 27.9) all agree,
there is no evidence of Ludovica having any other husband, and the same province-wide
sweep that closes off a rival couple for her siblings found none here either. The variant
is flagged, not concealed.

Formerly a frontier on [[lucien_vincke]]'s own record ("a probable second daughter, not
grafted"); resolved here (research-log §64).

NEW FRONTIER, NOT LINKED (2026-07-27). [[emma_vincke]]'s own 1907 marriage act, read as a
register image ([[fs-oostende-1907-marriage-vaniseghem-vincke]]), carries a witness
**Constantinus Degraeve**, 36, *aardewerker*, of Diksmuide, stated in the register's own
words as *"zwager der echtgenoote"* — brother-in-law of the bride — implying an
**unrecorded Vincke sister married a Degraeve**. No person record, label or prior search
anywhere in this tree mentions "Degraeve." This record is the only Vincke daughter of
[[lucien_vincke]] × [[ludovica_vanald]] known to have survived to adulthood with no
further record of her own (no marriage, no death) — but that is **pure surname-coincidence
speculation, not an identifier**, and it is recorded here as exactly that: not a link, not
an inference, a bare coincidence noted so a later pass does not rediscover it and
overclaim. What would settle it: Constantinus Degraeve's own marriage act, or a Diksmuide
or Oostende marriage pairing a Vincke bride to a Degraeve groom. See [[lucien_vincke]]'s
own record for the five Vincke children still unaccounted for, any of whom this witness
line could equally point to.

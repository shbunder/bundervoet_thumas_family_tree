Wife of [[bundervoet_lieven_gent]] and mother of the four children named in the Gent
notarial act of 1739: *"Livine Bundervoet filia Lieven, **verweckt bij Joanne de Smet syne
huijsvrauwe**"* — see [[bundervoet-1739-gent-notarial-lieven-children]].

**She exists here only because the register image was read.** FamilySearch's machine
transcription of this page did not produce her name at any point; the phrase naming her is
the clearest gain from opening the image, and it is the reason this act was worth the
trouble of reassembling from deep-zoom tiles.

Nothing else about her is stated — no dates, no origin, no parents — and nothing is guessed.

The act's counterparty is *vrauwe Susanne Marie de Smet, douariere van wijlent Remij du
Laurij*, a Grote Raad councillor's widow. **The shared surname is noted and no relationship
is drawn**: the act states none, and de Smet is among the commonest surnames in East
Flanders.

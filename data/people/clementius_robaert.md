Husband of [[francisca_vanhoutte]], who is recorded as his widow — so he died
before she did (17 April 1668, Oostkamp). No dates, parents or children of his own
are in isavdw.

Her other partner, [[petrus_vandewalle]], is recorded as a partnership rather than a
marriage, and their son [[carolus_vandewalle]] was born about 1638. Which of the two
came first is not stated anywhere reachable; the order below is the order the
records were written in, and should not be read as a chronology.

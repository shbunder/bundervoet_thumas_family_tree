Daughter of [[eduardus_vi]] and [[emma_vincke]], and [[joannes_vi]]'s sister. Her own
marriage act — Stene nr. 21, 22 May 1920 (vrijwilligersrab Huwelijken index,
`rab-bs-huwelijken`) — names her parents in full as Eduardus Van Iseghem x Emma Celesta
Vincke, matching this couple exactly; one of five such acts spanning 1920-1935 that all
converge on the identical parent-name pair. Index only, not the register image, hence
`sup`. The act does not give her own birth date, so none is recorded here (not in the
reachable pages).

FRONTIER — her husband. The same index names him **Desire Camille Gunst**, but he has no
record of his own here: this pass is naming [[eduardus_vi]] and [[emma_vincke]]'s children,
not their in-laws, so his own record is left for objective 2's next pass rather than
entered now.

FRONTIER — a birth before the parents' marriage, NOT PROVEN, not grafted (2026-07-27). A
birth-index sweep for rows naming [[emma_vincke]] as mother, incidental to this family's
1866-1870 blind-window pass, turned up "VINCKE Flavie Eulalie", b. Diksmuide 26 March 1898
act nr. 21, father NN NN, mother VINCKE Emma Celesta — nine years before this couple's
1907 marriage. The rare forename pair matches this record exactly, and a second such row
exists: "VINCKE Albert Joseph", b. Diksmuide 9 December 1899 act nr. 84, d. Diksmuide 26
March 1900 (`rab-bs-overlijdens`; a first read gave 27 March, corrected to 26 for the
same Date-vs-Certificate-Date reason as [[irma_vincke]] and [[jerome_vincke]]). Both
children pre-date the marriage and are registered under Vincke, not Van Iseghem, with no
father named. Legitimation by the 1907 marriage would explain both, and would also
explain [[joannes_vi]]'s own birth at Lens in 1903, four years before the same marriage
(his own record's open question). **NOT ASSERTED HERE.** It needs the marriage act's own

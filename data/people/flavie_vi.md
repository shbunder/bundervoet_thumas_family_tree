Daughter of [[eduardus_vi]] and [[emma_vincke]], and [[joannes_vi]]'s sister. Her own
marriage act — Stene nr. 21, 22 May 1920 (vrijwilligersrab Huwelijken index,
`rab-bs-huwelijken`) — names her parents in full as Eduardus Van Iseghem x Emma Celesta
Vincke, matching this couple exactly; one of five such acts spanning 1920-1935 that all
converge on the identical parent-name pair. Index only, not the register image, hence
`sup`. The act does not give her own birth date, so none is recorded here (not in the
reachable pages).

FRONTIER — her husband. The same index names him **Desire Camille Gunst**, but he has no
record of his own here: this pass is naming [[eduardus_vi]] and [[emma_vincke]]'s children,
not their in-laws, so his own record is left for objective 2's next pass rather than
entered now.

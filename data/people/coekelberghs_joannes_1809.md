Father of the bride in his daughter [[coekelberghs_maria_1848]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe
nr. 6, 24 April 1873. A *landbouwer* of Bertem, present at the wedding and consenting to
it, and recorded as unable to write or sign, *ongeletterd zijnde*.

Named a second time, thirty years later, in the same daughter's remarriage act — Kraainem
nr. 16, 6 November 1903 — which is where his death comes from: Bertem, 19 November 1880.
Two acts three decades apart naming the same man as her father is what carries this link;
the 1873 act alone would be a surname and a commune.

`sup` rather than `doc`: both are AGATHA act analyses — the Rijksarchief's own Demogen
transcriptions — and neither act has a scan on AGATHA or Open Archives.

GRAFTED (2026-07-26) — his own marriage act, **Bertem nr. 8, 30 October 1838, read as the
register image** (`fs-bertem-1838-marriage-register-nr8`, ark `3:1:9392-HG83-X`). It gives
his birth, **Bertem 29 July 1809**, his trade at twenty-nine, *slagter*, and his parents
[[coekelberghs_henricus]] and [[elseviers_petronella]] — both there in person, both
*present en Consenteerende*, both unable to sign, as were the bridegroom, the bride and her
parents. Banns 14 and 21 October; four witnesses, none stated as kin to either party. The
image also corrects AGATHA's spelling: the ink reads **Coekelberghs**, with no *c* before
the *k*.

**The trade change was the objection, and it is now answered rather than waved away.**
The previous note here withheld the graft because he is a *slagter* in 1838, 1853 and 1867
but a *landbouwer* in 1858, 1861 and 1873. Two Bertem birth acts settle it: at his
daughter's birth on 27 June 1853 he declares himself in person, aged 44, born at Bertem,
*slagter*; at his son's on 15 January 1858 he declares himself again, aged 48, born at
Bertem, *landbouwer*. The same man, self-declaring, on both sides of the change, five years
apart, in independently drawn acts — and both ages agree with a birth in July 1809. It is
one life, not two men, and an occupation was never a field that vetoes a match.

The rival reading was checked and refuted too. The only other Coekelberghs marriage
indexed at Bertem in these years is *Joanna Catharina Coeckelberghs*, 1 February 1837 —
a woman born in 1797, daughter of an unrelated *Petrus* Coekelberghs who died in 1798. A
coincidence of surname, not a sibling and not a rival groom.

**Still `sup`, and deliberately so.** The image proves what the 1838 act says. It does not
prove that its bridegroom is the man the 1873 and 1903 acts name as
[[coekelberghs_maria_1848]]'s father — that bridge runs entirely through two AGATHA transcriptions
with no scan behind them. A chain is as strong as its weakest link, and after
docs/research-log.md §52 this project says so rather than rounding up.

FRONTIER — his own birth act would prove the 1809 date instead of reporting it, and his
death act (Bertem, 19 November 1880) would give his age and confirm his parents. Both are
on FamilySearch film **DGS 004471767** (*Geboorten 1796–1870, Huwelijken 1796–1832,
Overlijdens 1796–1842*), which is unindexed and bound in blocks by record type, so it is a
browse. Better still: **Maria Theresia's own Bertem birth act of 22 November 1848** — that
one act would tie this 1838 couple directly to the 1848 daughter and move the whole cluster
off the transcriptions.

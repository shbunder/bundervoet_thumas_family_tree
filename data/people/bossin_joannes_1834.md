Father of the groom in his son [[bossin_ludovicus_1879]]'s marriage act — Kraainem nr. 3, 10
April 1900 (S28), which gives his death as **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 19 March 1887**. His son was
seven.

**RESOLVED (2026-07-27) — not [[bossin_antonia_1849]]'s family.** His own marriage act — Sint-Stevens-
Woluwe nr. 4, **17 June 1858** (S33) — was the open question this record carried: whether
he descended from the same Bossins as his son's mother-in-law, [[bossin_antonia_1849]] (Antonia
Bossin, daughter of [[bossin_guilielmus_1824]] × [[peremans_joanna_1824]]). It answers no. The act names
his own parents as **Judocus Bossin** and **Maria Desmedt**, a dagloonster — a wholly
different couple from [[bossin_guilielmus_1824]] × [[peremans_joanna_1824]]. Same surname, same commune,
two unconnected families a generation apart; that is now settled rather than left as a
question. It also supplies his exact birth for the first time — **2 January 1834,
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe** — and his trade there, *daglooner*, and his bride's exact
birth, **4 April 1836, Brussel**, where she is recorded as a *verlaten kind* (abandoned
child) — see [[wolf_maria_1836]].

**Five more children (2026-07-27), all from acts naming this couple as the child's
parents:** [[bossin_joannes_1857]] (b. SSW 1857, unmarried on the act — see the flag
below), [[bossin_philippus_1859]] (b. SSW 1859), [[bossin_jan_1876]] (b. SSW 1876),
[[bossin_frans_1882]] (b. SSW 1882) and [[bossin_guillielmus_1869]] (b. Kraainem 1869, known only
from his own 1896 marriage act). [[bossin_ludovicus_1879]] was already the only child this
tree held for this couple; these six make seven.

**The mother's exact age is the identifier that carries all five.** Against the
1836-04-04 birthdate the 1858 marriage act gives her, her stated age is an *exact* match
in five separate acts — 21 (1857), 22 (1859), 39 (1876), 46 (1882) and 43 on
[[bossin_ludovicus_1879]]'s own 1879 birth act — five for five, which is what defeats the
"two couples" reading the seventeen-year gap between the 1859 and 1876 births might
otherwise invite. The father's own age matches exactly in two of those acts and is a year
short in two others — ordinary rounding, not a conflict, given the mother's precision on
the same documents.

**His own death is restated twice more, independently.** [[bossin_jan_1876]]'s 1897
marriage act and [[bossin_guillielmus_1869]]'s 1896 marriage act both give his death as
**Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 19 March 1887** — an exact match, to the day, to what this record
already held from S28. Two of his children's own later marriages, nine years apart,
independently agree with each other and with the record.

**His trade moves with him across seven documents, 1857-1887:** *papiermaekersgast* at 24
(1857) → *daglooner* at his own 1858 marriage and again at 25 (1859) → *landbouwer* at 41
(1876) and 45 (1879, [[bossin_ludovicus_1879]]'s own birth act) → *herbergier* at 48 (1882),
the trade he is still keeping, and the one [[wolf_maria_1836]] continues alone, when he dies in
1887.

**FLAG, not resolved — a child born before this marriage.** [[bossin_joannes_1857]] was
born **28 June 1857**, about eleven months before this couple's marriage on 17 June 1858.
Both dates stand as their own acts give them; neither is altered to make them agree. See
his own record for the reasoning, which follows the same shape already documented on
[[vaniseghem_joannes_1903]].

The mother's surname is spelled across these acts as *Dewolf*, *Wolf*, *De Wolf* and *Anna
Maria*/*Maria Anna Dewolf* — one woman, several clerks; see [[wolf_maria_1836]].

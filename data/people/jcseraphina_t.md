Eldest known child of [[georges_cj]] and [[bossin]] — born at **Kraainem on 21 March 1873**,
nine months after her parents married there in June 1872, and so the first of the Kraainem
generation.

Her marriage act (Kraainem nr. 4, 27 July 1892, S26) gives her *zonder beroep* and names both
parents: her father a *fabriekbediende*, her mother [[bossin]] still living and keeping house.
Her mother would be dead two years later.

She married **Jan Baptist Coppens**, a mason of Sint-Stevens-Woluwe.

been pulled.

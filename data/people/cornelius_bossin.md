Eldest known child of [[guilielmus_bossin]] and [[peremans]], and an elder brother of
[[bossin]]. His birth was declared at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on **13 September 1847**, eighteen
months after his parents married at Zaventem.

The birth entry is a `raw` note rather than a date because the index gives the **declaration**,
not the birth. They are usually a day or two apart, and this project does not turn one into
the other.

`sup`: an index row, no register image read. What makes it safe rather than a guess is the
sibling in the middle of the same series — his sister's declaration of 11 January 1849 sits
against a birth of 10 January 1849 already read from her marriage act, with the same two
parents and the same commune (S24).

FRONTIER: his own birth act, for the exact date; and any marriage or death — nothing is known
of him after 1847.

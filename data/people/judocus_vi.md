CHECKED AND REJECTED (July 2026). The corpus's best candidate for this record scores 33
bits and is not him: an **Aalst** death act of 11 March 1869 for Josephus Hippolytus Van
Iseghem, whose father is *Constantinus Franciscus* Van Iseghem.

Three things kill it. Our Judocus died at Oostende in **1832**, so he cannot be a named
father in an 1869 act. The forename is Constantinus Franciscus, not Judocus Franciscus —
the *Franciscus* they share is the commonest middle name in these registers. And Aalst is
in Oost-Vlaanderen, sixty miles from the Oostende family this record belongs to.

What produced the score was the surname plus that shared *Franciscus* plus a child's name.
Van Iseghem is not rare enough in Oost-Vlaanderen for that to mean anything. Left ungrafted
and uncited.

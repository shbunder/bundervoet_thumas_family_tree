Great-grandfather of Maria Rups (1642). Churchwarden and poor-relief master (dischmeester) of Oostkamp, buried in the church, a Bruges citizen who bought the Lettenburgh farmstead (Moerbrugge). Rich property records 1612-1632. Parents Guillielmus Van Renterghem (~1530) x Barbara Wittenheyns (~1535).

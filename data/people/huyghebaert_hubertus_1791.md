Father of [[huyghebaert_appolonia_1830]], named on her 1851 marriage act (Oostende act nr.
090) as "HUYGHEBAERT Hubertus, *schoenmaker*, living". His own marriage act, Oudenburg 29
April 1821, act nr. 24 (RAB ID 253572, microfilm 1358641, remark "SCAN 122 GSU"), gives
age 30 (so b. ~1791), *schoenmaker*, residence Oudenburg.

His daughter [[huyghebaert_appolonia_1830]]'s 1868 remarriage act (Oostende act nr. 106)
independently gives his death: **15 March 1857, Oudenburg** — read off the "Decease Date
Father" / "Location of Decease Father" fields on the bride's side of the same act already
accepted for corroborating her first husband's death.

Ten children with [[derudder_joanna_1793]] at Oudenburg, all sup from the RAB birth index:
[[huyghebaert_carolus_1822]] (13/03/1822, apparently died in infancy — see
[[huyghebaert_carolus_1823]]), [[huyghebaert_carolus_1823]] (the name reused, 01/02/1823, m.
Oudenburg 1846 [[goes_nn]]), [[huyghebaert_augustinus_1824]] (12/05/1824, m. Zandvoorde 1859
[[vantyghem_nn]]), [[huyghebaert_adelia_1826]] (13/07/1826), [[huyghebaert_petrus_1827]]
(05/08/1827), [[huyghebaert_adelaide_1829]] (04/03/1829), [[huyghebaert_appolonia_1830]]
(26/09/1830 — own birth act not found, see her record), [[huyghebaert_marie_1832]]
(04/03/1832), [[huyghebaert_magdalena_1834]] (21/06/1834) and [[huyghebaert_petrus_1837]]
(19/02/1837).

**FRONTIER, refused despite being right there.** His own parents, per the 1821 act, are
**Ferdinandus Huyghebaert × Anna Theresia Termote** — Ferdinandus d. Oudenburg 06/07/1803
(17 Messidor XI), Anna Theresia d. Oudenburg 05/05/1804 (15 Floréal XII); both
republican-calendar dates convert correctly to their Gregorian equivalents. **Not
linked**: this identical name pair recurs as parents in at least four other West-Flemish
communes (Eernegem, Leffinge, Westkerke, Oostkerke-bij-Damme) — exactly the
surname-and-region trap CLAUDE.md rule 1 exists for, and this single 1821 index row is
the only document naming them for *this* family. The document that would settle it: the
Detail page of **Oudenburg 1806, RAB 253462** — same name pair as parents, a different
child — which could not be pulled this pass. Re-openable there, or at the register image.

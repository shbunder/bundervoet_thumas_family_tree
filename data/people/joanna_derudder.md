Mother of [[appolonia_huyghebaert]], named on her 1851 marriage act (Oostende act nr.
090) as "DERUDDER Johanna, 59, *zonder beroep*". Her own marriage act, Oudenburg 29 April
1821, act nr. 24 (RAB ID 253572), gives age 28 (so b. ~1793), *dienstmeid*, birthplace
**Poperinge**.

Still living in 1868: [[appolonia_huyghebaert]]'s second marriage act (Oostende act nr.
106) gives her age as 76 at that date, occupation *zonder beroep*, with no death date
recorded — broadly consistent with the ~1793 birth computed from the 1821 act.

Ten children with [[hubertus_huyghebaert]] — see his record for the full sibship.

FRONTIER, not pursued. Her own parents, per the 1821 act, are **Derudder Pieter Josephus
× Vandenbrande Anna Maria** — Pieter Josephus d. Poperinge 09/04/1810; Anna Maria age 69
and still living in 1821, *werkvrouw*. Rests on that single index row alone, with no
independent document — and, unlike the Huyghebaert pair on [[hubertus_huyghebaert]]'s
record, no rival-community check has yet been run against it either. Left unlinked.

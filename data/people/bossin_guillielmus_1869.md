Not found as a birth act in this pass — no Kraainem birth register for 1869 has been
searched. He is placed here entirely from his own **marriage act, Zaventem, 18 April
1896**, which states his birth directly: **1869-09-30, Kraainem**, and gives him as a
*daglooner*, born and raised, per the residence given, into a family that had moved
between Kraainem and Sint-Stevens-Woluwe (his own residence at marriage is given as
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe). His father is named *Jan Baptiste Bossin*, **deceased
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 19 March 1887** — an exact match, to the day, to [[bossin_joannes_1834]]'s
already-held death, independently of the same corroboration on [[bossin_jan_1876]]'s
1897 marriage act. His mother is named *Maria Anna Dewolf*, a *landbouwster*, present and
consenting.

A fifth child for [[bossin_joannes_1834]] × [[wolf_maria_1836]], born three years before
[[bossin_jan_1876]] and at a different commune (Kraainem, not Sint-Stevens-Woluwe) —
the family's residence moved between the two at some point in this span, which is noted
as a fact and not otherwise explained.

`sup`: an act analysis, not the register image, and not itself a birth act — his own
birth act has not been located.

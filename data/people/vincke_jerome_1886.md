Son of [[vincke_lucien_1840]] and [[vanalderweireldt_ludovica_1845]], and [[vincke_emma_1880]]'s brother — the
youngest known child of the couple. His birth — Diksmuide act nr. 167, 22 September 1886
— is one of seven Diksmuide rows in the vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index
(`rab-bs-geboorten`) naming both parents in full as Vincke Lucien Julianus x
Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria. His mother was 40.8 at his birth — the upper end of
childbearing but not implausible, and the 6.6-year gap since [[vincke_emma_1880]] (1880) is
consistent with unrecorded infant losses in the couple's 1866-1870 blind window (see
[[vincke_lucien_1840]]'s record). A province-wide sweep of the same index, filtered separately
by father's forename and by mother's surname, returns the identical seven rows — no rival
couple. Index only, not the register image, hence `sup`.

Died at Diksmuide, act nr. 5, 13 January 1889, aged two (`rab-bs-overlijdens`), naming
both parents again. **CORRECTED on entry**: the death index's "Certificate Date" column
reads 14 January; the "Date" column, which is the death itself, reads 13 January — the
same Date-vs-Certificate-Date conflation already documented on `rab-bs-geboorten`'s note
for the births index, now confirmed to affect the deaths index too. 13 January is
recorded here, not 14.

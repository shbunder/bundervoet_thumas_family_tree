Named as the bride in her own marriage act, Kraainem, 8 February 1897, to [[felix_bemden76]] —
born at Kraainem, 23 April 1876, *zonder beroep* (without occupation) at the time of the
marriage. The act names her parents as Franciscus Coenraets, *winkelier* of Kraainem, and Anna
Maria Deridder, *winkelierster* of Kraainem — a shopkeeping couple, neither yet in this tree.

The act is held in the harvested corpus and reached through Open Archives:
https://www.openarchieven.nl/abl:c122cfac-929f-f4a8-ff56-704f485d6171

**Not the same person as [[amelia_coenraets03]]** (b. 1903, Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, daughter of
[[coenraets_f]] and [[henrica_thumas]]) — a different, later Amelia Coenraets in the same rare
surname's Kraainem/Sint-Stevens-Woluwe cluster. Noted explicitly so a future pass does not merge
the two by name alone.

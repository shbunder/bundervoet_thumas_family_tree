Named in his daughter Octavia's 1906 Oostende marriage act (nr. 258, S20), read as an
image, as father of the bride: "Ludovicus Schalandrijn", werkman, resident at Oostende
("alhier wonende"), present and consenting. The act signs him "L. Schalandryn"; his
daughter's own record spells the surname Schalandrijn, so the family name appears both
ways in the same generation and this record follows the act's own spelling of him.

FRONTIER — leads from the vrijwilligersrab civil-registration indexes (Huwelijken and
Geboorten), none read at image level, so none of the following is recorded as fact
here:

- birth: Oudenburg, 29 January 1842 (act of 31 January 1842, nr. 14), naming his
  parents as Ferdinandus Faustinus Schalandryn x Isabella Wallecan;
- marriage to Mathilde Standaert: Oudenburg, 17 February 1871, act 17;
- death: Oostende, 15 November 1916, per an Open Archives memorial card (fwk:a60b0372);
- one generation further back, also index-only: Ferdinandus Faustinus Schalandryn's
  parents Jacobus Schalandryn x Anna Devisschere, and Isabella Wallecan's parents
  Henricus Wallecan x Joanna Vantornhoudt;
- at least seven siblings of Octavia appear in the Geboorten index (rab-bs-geboorten) —
  none are entered as records here.

Each needs the underlying act image read before it becomes a fact; none is graftable
on the index alone (verification pass, 2026-07-26).

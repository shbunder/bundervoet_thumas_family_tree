Son of [[lucien_vincke]] and [[ludovica_vanald]], and [[emma_vincke]]'s brother — one of
four children recovered from the couple's **1866-1870 blind window**, where the Diksmuide
birth index gives only the bare father surname VINCKE, no forename and no mother (his own
birth row is Diksmuide 1867 nr. 245). His parentage instead comes from his death, not his
birth: he died at Diksmuide 5 September 1872, act nr. 188 (`rab-bs-overlijdens`, indexed
as *Bellarmin Eugenius*), which names both parents in full — Vincke Lucianus Julianus ×
Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria — an infant of about five, dead before he could ever have
married and so unreachable through any marriage-act route. Index only, not the register
image, hence `sup`.

The death index is what broke this wall: it reaches children who never lived to marry,
which a marriage-act sweep by construction cannot.

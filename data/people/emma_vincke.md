Lived to 94 — the longest-lived in this cluster — outliving her husband by nearly 30 years. Daughter of Lucien Vincke & Ludovica Vanalderweireldt.

CORROBORATED (July 2026). A Familiekunde Vlaanderen Westkust memorial-card record gives
her birth at Diksmuide on 23 January 1880, her death at Oostende on 6 October 1974, and
her partner as Eduard Van Iseghem — every field as held here, and the marriage to
[[eduardus_vi]] with it. She had rested on the gverdievel tree alone.

FRONTIER — her birth act is indexed and has been seen, but not read. AGATHA's index for
the West-Vlaanderen civil birth registers carries it as Diksmuide, **akte nr. 14, act
date 24 January 1880**, child *Emma Celesta Vincke*, born Diksmuide — one day after the
birth date held here, which is the ordinary gap between a birth and its declaration.
Pulling the act itself would move her birth, and her parents [[lucien_vincke]] ×
[[ludovica_vanald]], off a memorial card and a member tree and onto civil registration.

Father of the bride in his daughter [[peremans]]'s marriage act — Zaventem, act nr. 1,
24 February 1846 (S19), read as an image. He was dead by then, and the act gives the date
and place exactly: Zaventem, 6 March 1837. His wife [[joanna_verelst]] outlived him by six
years.

The act gives him no age and no occupation. His daughter was 21 in February 1846, which
places his own birth loosely in the 1780s or 1790s; nothing narrower is recorded.

FRONTIER — his 1837 Zaventem death act, which would give his age, his occupation and
very probably his parents, and his marriage to Joanna Theresia Ver Elst, which the
Zaventem registers should hold for roughly 1815–1824. His daughter told the 1846 act that
she had never known her grandparents nor where they died, so his parents are not reachable
through her.

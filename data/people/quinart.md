Wife of the carpenter Georges Lambert Thumas (m. 1801); mother of ten, including Georges (1804).

CORROBORATED (July 2026), and it turned up a child. The Grez-Doiceau death act of
18 October 1812 for a boy named **Etienne Thumas** gives his parents as *Lambert Thumas* and
*Marie Catherine Quinart* — this couple, which had stood on the m2155 tree alone.

FRONTIER: Etienne himself. He is a son of [[georgeslambert_t]] and this record, and so a
brother of [[georges2_t]], and he is not in the tree. The act has not been opened, so his
birth date and his age at death are unread; opening it would place him.

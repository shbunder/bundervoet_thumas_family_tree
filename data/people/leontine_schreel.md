Second wife of [[edouard_dk]], married at Oostende on 9 May 1946. `doc`, because
the act itself was read as an image and states her birth date, her birthplace and
her own earlier marriage.

She had been married before, to [[benoni_rossel]], and was divorced from him when she
married Édouard — the same act says so. That marriage is recorded as a marriage,
listed first because it was the earlier one; the act gives no date for either the
wedding or the divorce, so neither is recorded. FRONTIER — the Schreel–Rossel marriage
and divorce acts, which would date both and give his parents.

She is a step-relation, not blood: no line of descent here runs through her.

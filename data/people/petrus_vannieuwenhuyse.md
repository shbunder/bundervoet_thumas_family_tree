Source detail: Geneanet isavdw; birth act agatha.arch.be scan 513_9000_000_00774 (Sosa 3382)

Father of Anna Van Nieuwenhuyse (1676). Served as a schepen (alderman/magistrate) of Oostkamp. Parents Marinus Van Nieuwenhuyse (~1620-1666) x Cornelia Govaert (~1625-1653).

THE REFERENCE IS REAL, BUT STILL NOT `doc` (2026-07-26). §52 downgraded this record because
the "scan 513_9000_000_00774" citation could not be checked against anything real. It now
can be: `/nl/search/genealogie/35229` on AGATHA resolves to exactly that item —
[[agatha-oostkamp-doopregister-1630-1652]], the **Oostkamp Sint-Pietersbanden parish
register, Doopakten 26 June 1630 – 14 April 1652** — and a 7 March 1649 baptism falls
inside its span. That does not restore `doc`, for three reasons: (1) the reference names a
**volume**, not a page or an act — it says where to look, not what was read; (2) the images
require an AGATHA login this project's browser session does not have — logged **blocked**,
not **miss**; and (3) it is a register of **baptisms**, so a date read there would be a
baptism date, and this record's `birth: 1649-03-07` field would need relabelling, not just
sourcing.

THREE NAMED FRONTIERS, from the same pass. First, his **1673 marriage** is mapped the same
way: `/nl/search/genealogie/35236` = AGATHA item 513_9000_000_00777, from scan 0_0660,
covering 5 July 1673 – 30 April 1702 — the volume holding his 23 July 1673 wedding to
[[jacoba_monballiu]]. Second, the **Oostkamp baptisms of 1652–1673** — which would carry
his siblings and his own earliest children — are marked by AGATHA itself as *"Niet
beschikbaar — hiaten in de originele registers en op de microfilms"* (not available — gaps
in the original registers and on the microfilms): a hole in the source, not in the search.
Third, **Begrafenisakten Oostkamp 1662** lists a *Marinus Van Nieuwenhuyse* as *"Partner van
de overledene"* — which does not sit with this tree's own ~1653 death for
[[cornelia_govaert]], his mother. No act id has been read for that entry, so it stays a
frontier rather than a correction: it may be a second Marinus, a mis-transcribed date, or a
real conflict with her death year.

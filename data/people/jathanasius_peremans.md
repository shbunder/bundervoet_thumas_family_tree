The third sibling found for [[peremans]], and the eldest of them — born about **1822**, two
years before her. He married **Maria Elisabeth Cuypers** at **Zaventem**, banns on 17 and 24
November 1844 and the marriage registered on the **28th** (S31).

**This entry is better than the other two.** Where Cornelius's record left three people
unlabelled, this one names the roles outright: *Vader* [[egidius_peremans]], *Moeder*
[[joanna_verelst]], the spouse, and her parents [[cuypers_ae]] and [[dehose]]. Nothing about
the family's shape has to be inferred.

The birth year is approximate because it is FamilySearch's estimate from an age, not a date
from an act — hence `~1822` and not a day.

*Athanasius* is a rare enough forename to search on alone, which is what makes him easier to
follow than his brother.

give both trades and his exact age.

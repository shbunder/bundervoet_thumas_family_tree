Named as the child in a civil birth act at **Zaventem, 1859-09-05**, which gives both
parents — [[franciscus_pardon]] and [[anna_maria_bossin]] — and so places this person in the tree by the act rather than
by a name. The father, present as *aangever*, is a *papiermakerswerkman* living at Zaventem —
his trade at his 1853 marriage was *dienstbode*, so it had already changed by this second child.

The act is held in the harvested corpus and reached through Open Archives:
https://www.openarchieven.nl/abl:17bb266b-3a29-270a-469d-40e86d230954

**The family's move from Sint-Stevens-Woluwe to Zaventem, between this birth and the two births
before it, is one migrating family and not two couples.** [[henricus_pardon54]] (1854) and
[[joanna_pardon56]] (1856) were born at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe; this child (1859) and
[[maria_pardon63]] (1863) at Zaventem. Three of the four — Henricus, Joanna Coleta and Maria
Thérèsia — are double-attested by their own later Zaventem marriage acts, and each of those three
independently names the same father, "Frans/Franciscus/Jan Frans Pardon, deceased Zaventem
18/07/1874," and the same mother, Anna Maria Bossin (`zaventem-pardon-children-marriages-1877-1886`).
That carries the identity across the move rather than starting a second family at the new
address; this child alone has no later marriage act found in the held corpus, so is
single-attested by the birth act, which is already two independent identifiers (both parents'
names and birthplaces) beyond the shared surname.

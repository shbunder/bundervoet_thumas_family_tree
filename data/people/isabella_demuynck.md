First wife of [[jacobus_stroobandt]]. isavdw gives her years as 1782–1809 and
nothing more — no day, no place, no parents, and no children.

Her death in 1809 is what dates the sequence: Jacobus married
[[joanna_keirsebilck]] at Oostkamp on 24 July 1810, the year after. Our line runs
through that second marriage, not this one.

Son of [[vincke_lucien_1840]] and [[vanalderweireldt_ludovica_1845]], and [[vincke_emma_1880]]'s brother — one of
four children recovered from the couple's **1866-1870 blind window**, where the Diksmuide
birth index gives only the bare father surname VINCKE, no forename and no mother (his own
birth row is Diksmuide 1870 nr. 247). His parentage instead comes from his death: he died
at Diksmuide 9 September 1871, act nr. 189 (`rab-bs-overlijdens`, indexed as *Carolus
Ludovicus*), naming both parents in full — Vincke Lucien Julien × Vanalderweireldt
Ludovica Maria — an infant who died before he could ever have married. Index only, not
the register image, hence `sup`.

**Name reuse.** [[vincke_charles_1871]] ("Charles Louis Vincke") was born to the same couple
just under four months later, 30 December 1871 — the ordinary pattern of a name given to
a new child after an infant of the same name had died.

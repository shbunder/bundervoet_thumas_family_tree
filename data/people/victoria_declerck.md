Mother of [[josephus_janssen]], named on his 1851 marriage act (Oostende act nr. 090) as
"DECLERCK Victoria, 64, *werkvrouw*" — still living. Her own marriage act, Oostende 17
November 1819 (RAB ID 572117), gives her birth as 30/04/1786 at **Klemskerke**, age 33 at
the wedding, residence Oostende. The 1820 Oostende birth register, still French at that
date, spells her "Declerck Victoire".

**A stated conflict, not smoothed over.** A birth of 30/04/1786 makes her 65 at the August
1851 wedding, but that act's Detail transcription gives her age as 64. This is ordinary
index slippage on a self-reported age thirty-two years after the fact, and does not veto
the identification, which rests on both spouses' exact-matching birth date and place plus
a second, independent corroboration of the parent names on [[josephus_janssen]]'s own
1820 birth act.

Three further children with [[joannes_janssen]] at Oostende: [[petrus_janssen22]]
(1822), [[hermanus_janssen25]] (1825), [[clementia_janssen32]] (1832).

FRONTIER, not pursued. Her own parents, per the 1819 act, are **Declerck Pierre × Pauwels
Josephe** — both recorded as having died at Klemskerke, no dates given. Rests on that
single index row alone, with no independent document. Left unlinked.

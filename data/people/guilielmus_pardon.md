Father of the groom in his son [[franciscus_pardon]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe
nr. 9, 1 December 1853 (S22). A *daglooner* of Winksele, present at the wedding and
consenting to it, and recorded as unable to write.

The act gives him no age, so this record carries no birth date. His son was twenty-three,
which places him loosely in the 1790s or early 1800s and no closer.

The death entry says only that he was alive on the day of the act.

FRONTIER — his marriage to [[maria_anna_pergijsels]], which the Winksele or Herent
registers should hold for roughly 1820–1830, and which would name his own parents.

Younger brother of [[georges_cj]], and **the reason his father's death act survives at
all**. He married **Sophie Miranda Deridder** at Grez-Doiceau on **7 October 1868**, with
banns on 20 and 27 September. Because his father was dead, the commune drew up an extract of
[[georges2_t]]'s 1864 death act on **28 September 1868** and bound it into the marriage
volume — that extract is [[S23]], the document that finally put his father's death date and
his grandparents' names onto civil registration. This wedding is why it exists.

His own birth was declared at Grez-Doiceau on **31 March 1843**; the birth itself is a day or
two earlier, which is why it sits in `raw`.

**Do not merge him with his brother.** [[georges_cj]] is *Georges Charles Joseph*, born 1836,
married at Kraainem in 1872. This is *Georges Joseph*, born 1843, married at Grez-Doiceau in
1868. Two sons of one couple, both called Georges, seven years apart — exactly the confusion
this project's rules exist to catch.

`sup`: index rows only, no register image read.

Son of [[vaniseghem_eduardus_1880]] and [[vincke_emma_1880]], and [[vaniseghem_joannes_1903]]'s brother — named for their
father. His own marriage act — Oostende nr. 10, 15 January 1927 (vrijwilligersrab
Huwelijken index, `rab-bs-huwelijken`) — names his parents in full as Eduardus Van Iseghem
x Emma Celesta Vincke, matching this couple exactly; one of five such acts spanning
1920-1935 that all converge on the identical parent-name pair. He married **Angela
Coralia Gunst**. This 1927 act of his own is index only, not a register image. The
marriage act does not give his own birth date.

**Birth date (2026-07-27).** A birth-index sweep for rows naming [[vincke_emma_1880]] as mother
turned up his own row: Oostende act nr. 1469, 2 November 1901 (`rab-bs-geboorten`). That
reading was not independently re-checked against a second act, and this same index's
"Date"/"Certificate Date" columns are confirmed elsewhere in this family to be conflated
(see `rab-bs-overlijdens`'s note and the corrections on [[vincke_irma_1874]] and
[[vincke_jerome_1886]]) — recorded with that caveat rather than left out, since the row cites a
specific act number.

**THE CAVEAT IS RESOLVED (2026-07-27) — RECORD UPGRADED `sup` → `doc`.** His parents' own
1907 marriage act, read as a register image
([[fs-oostende-1907-marriage-vaniseghem-vincke]],
`data/artifacts/vaniseghem-vincke-1907-marriage-oostende.md`), carries a legitimation
clause naming him directly: "voor hunne wettige zoons: Eduardus Vanyseghem, geboren te
Oostende den tweeden November negentien honderd en een" — 2 November 1901, Oostende,
exactly the date above. This is a wholly independent doc-grade primary source — a
different register and clerk from the birth-index row — so the earlier caveat no longer
applies. It also newly establishes that he was born nearly six years before his parents'
marriage and was legitimized by it, the same mechanism as his sister [[vaniseghem_flavie_1898]] and
brother [[vaniseghem_joannes_1903]], named in the same clause.

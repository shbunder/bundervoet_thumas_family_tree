One of the eight children of [[bundervoet_petrus_1727]] and [[dedeckere_marie_1731]]. Their father was
the younger brother of [[bundervoet_christoffel_1709]], through whom this tree's line runs, so these eight are
first cousins of [[bundervoet_philippus_1756]]'s generation — blood relatives of Renée and Léon under
objective 2.

Recorded first from the paulderidder tree's list of the couple's children, which gave a
birth year and a death year and nothing else. **Two Assenede records now stand behind
both.** The Assenede register has her birth there in 1766; and a *memorie van successie*
— an estate-duty declaration, kantoor Hulst nr. 2027 — was filed at Assenede on
**27 December 1807** in her name, giving her husband as **[[bouchier_constantin]]**.

Three identifiers agree, which is what licences reading the estate as hers: a surname worth
13 bits with an uncommon forename, the death year 1807 that the held record already
carried from a different source, and Assenede, where her father was buried in 1777.

**The estate date is not a death date.** A memorie is filed after a death, so 27 December
1807 says only that she was dead by then. The year stands as the fact and the filing sits
in `raw`, the same distinction [[bundervoet_petrus_1727]]'s burial draws.

Still index-level on both — the memorie itself has not been read, and it would give the
date of death, an address and what she left.

Father of [[josephus_janssen]], named on his son's 1851 marriage act (Oostende, act nr.
090) as "JANSSEN Joannes, deceased at Oostende" — no date given, only that he had already
died by 1851. His own marriage act, Oostende 17 November 1819 (RAB ID 572117, microfilm
1358564, remark "SCAN 812 GSU"), gives the fuller record: b. 11/04/1778 **Moersum
(Deutschland)** — Germany — age 41, *zeeman* (seaman), residence not stated. The 1820
Oostende birth register, still kept in French at that date, spells him "Jean Janssen" —
the same man, a register-language difference rather than two people; the 1851 act, in
Dutch, gives "Joannes".

Three further children with [[victoria_declerck]] at Oostende: [[petrus_janssen22]]
(1822), [[hermanus_janssen25]] (1825), [[clementia_janssen32]] (1832), besides
[[josephus_janssen]] himself (1820).

FRONTIER, not pursued. His own parents, per the 1819 act, are **Jean Janssen × Karckman
Catherine** — both recorded only as having died at an unknown place ("Onbekend"), no
dates given. Rests on that single index row alone, with no independent document. Left
unlinked.

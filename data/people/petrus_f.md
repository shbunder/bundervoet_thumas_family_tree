The FIRST of the Bundervoet line to leave Evergem for the coast: born in Evergem, he settled in Oostende, where all his children were born from 1905 onward. Married Augusta Vanstechelman (the Bundervoet–Vanstechelman junction). FRONTIER (MyHeritage, July 2026): two independent Bundervoet family trees on MyHeritage — one run by Lucien Bundervoet, one by Johny Henricus Bundervoet, both in Belgium — agree that he and Augusta had SIX children, not the one recorded here: Alphons Bernardus Cyprianus, Emilius Josephus, Achilles, Henricus Eduardus, Irena Augusta and Camillus Edmundus. The same trees give him at least four siblings, one named Maria Bundervoet. His own birth and death match ours exactly (19 Mar 1879 Evergem, 21 Jul 1943), which is what makes the match credible. NOT GRAFTED: member trees are not records and no dates for the children were readable, so the five siblings need their Oostende birth acts (1905 onward) before they go in.

CORROBORATED (July 2026), and by two archives that do not share a source. Doodsprentjes.be
and Familiekunde Vlaanderen Westkust each hold a memorial-card record for him, and both
give birth 19 March 1879 at Evergem, death 21 July 1943 at Oostende, and a Vanstechelman
partner — the Familiekunde one naming her in full as Augusta. Four identifiers agreeing
across two independent collections, where before this record rested on a Geneanet tree.

Confidence stays `sup` rather than rising to `doc`: these are transcriptions of memorial
cards. The Doodsprentjes scan is behind a session — the record's image URL returns the
site's search page, not the card — so no image has been read here. Reading it, or the
Oostende 1943 civil death act, is what would make this `doc`.

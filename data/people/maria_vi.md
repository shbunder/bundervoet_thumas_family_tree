Daughter of [[eduardus_vi]] and [[emma_vincke]], and [[joannes_vi]]'s sister. Her own
marriage act — Stene nr. 21, 17 August 1935 (vrijwilligersrab Huwelijken index,
`rab-bs-huwelijken`) — names her parents in full as Eduardus Van Iseghem x Emma Celesta
Vincke, matching this couple exactly; one of five such acts spanning 1920-1935 that all
converge on the identical parent-name pair. Index only, not the register image, hence
`sup`. The marriage act does not give her own birth date.

**Birth date (2026-07-27), NOT INDEPENDENTLY RE-CHECKED.** A birth-index sweep for rows
naming [[emma_vincke]] as mother turned up her own row: Oostende act nr. 80, 22 January
1913 (`rab-bs-geboorten`). The verifier did not itself confirm this date against a second
act or memorial card, and this same index's "Date"/"Certificate Date" columns are
confirmed elsewhere in this family to be conflated (see `rab-bs-overlijdens`'s note and
the corrections on [[irma_vincke]] and [[jerome_vincke]]) — recorded with that caveat
rather than left out, since the row cites a specific act number.

FRONTIER — her husband. The same index names him **Joseph Louis Zwaenepoel**, but he has
no record of his own here: this pass is naming [[eduardus_vi]] and [[emma_vincke]]'s
children, not their in-laws, so his own record is left for objective 2's next pass rather
than entered now.

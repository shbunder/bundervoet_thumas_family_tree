Daughter of [[francois_looten]] × [[brigitte_wyllie]], died at Diksmuide in 1823 — found
while reading the couple's 1818 marriage act, not from her own act number, which was not
individually re-read this pass. See [[francois_looten]] for the other three children found
the same way.

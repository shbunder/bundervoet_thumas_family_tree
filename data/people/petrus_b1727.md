Younger son of Joannes Bundervoet (~1682–1760) and Livina Stockman, and the brother of
Christoffel (1709–1786) through whom our line descends. He existed in this tree only as
the string "Pieter (1727)" inside his father's note; every person referenced anywhere is
supposed to have their own record, and now he does.

The death is recorded as a burial, not a death: paulderidder gives "deceased, aged about
50" with a burial at Assenede on 13 December 1777, and no date of death. The year is
safe, the day is the burial's, and the two are kept apart rather than collapsed into a
death date the source never gave. Assenede, a little north of Evergem, is also the first
sign of this branch moving off the home parish.

HIS FAMILY IS NOW IN THE TREE — his wife [[marie_anna_dedeckere]] and all eight children. From
paulderidder: married about 1750 to Marie Anna de Deckere (~1731–1809), with Joannis
(1751), Livinus (1752), Petronella (1754–1794), Christophorus (1755), Joannis Baptista
(1757), Livina Maria (1760–1831), Joanna (1761–1825) and Coleta (1766–1807). They are
blood relatives of Renée and Léon — a great-uncle's whole household — and belong in the
tree under objective 2. They are listed here rather than grafted because this pass took
the direct ancestors first, as the charter's priority requires.

A second document sits on his paulderidder profile, captioned "Petrus Bundervoet Akte
van teraardebestelling" — a burial act. Not yet retrieved.

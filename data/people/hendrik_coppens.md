Father of the groom in his son [[jbcoppens]]'s marriage act — Kraainem nr. 4, 27 July 1892
(S26). A *metser* of Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, the trade his son also followed.

The act gives him no age and no dates, so this record has none. It does place him alive in
July 1892.

FRONTIER: his marriage to [[catharina_bossin]], which the Sint-Stevens-Woluwe registers should
hold for roughly 1865–1870 and which would name his own parents.

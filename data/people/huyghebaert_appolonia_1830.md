Born in Oudenburg; widowed in 1866 and remarried two years later.

Her own marriage act — Oostende, 28 August 1851, act nr. 090 (RAB ID 574198) — names her
parents for the first time on any civil-registration document: [[huyghebaert_hubertus_1791]]
(*schoenmaker*, then living) and [[derudder_joanna_1793]] (59, *zonder beroep*). The spelling
there, "Apollonia Johanna", is not a discrepancy against this record's "Appolonia Joanna":
`data/forenames.json` folds `apollonia~appolonia` and `joanna~johanna`.

**A second marriage, found without looking for it.** Widowed 26 July 1866, she remarried
[[pieren_leopoldus_1839]] at Oostende on 27 October 1868 (act nr. 106, RAB ID 580783), civil
status "weduwe". The act's "Previous Partners" table names her first husband exactly —
"JANSSEN | Josephus Joannes | Oostende | 26/07/1866" — corroborating
[[janssen_josephus_1820]]'s death date and place **from a civil act**, where until now it had
rested only on tree-gverdievel. Note for a future search: her 1895 death may be
registered under **Pieren**, not Huyghebaert or Janssen.

**Her own birth act was not found.** Scope: both spellings (Huyghebaert 2,668 rows,
Huygebaert 740), province-wide, all years; Oudenburg 1828–1833 read row by row. Oudenburg
1830 retains only one Huyghebaert row (nr. 96, 26/12/1830) — this reads as an
**untranscribed batch, not a contradiction**: her siblings bracket the slot exactly —
[[huyghebaert_adelaide_1829]] (04/03/1829, nr. 24) and [[huyghebaert_marie_1832]]
(04/03/1832, nr. 20), an 18-month cadence with a gap precisely where 26/09/1830 falls.

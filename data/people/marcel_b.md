Full name Marcel Henri Bundervoet. Confirmed by his memorial card, which named the Bostyn family — pointing to his mother Elodia Bostyn and father Alphonsus.

DATES SHARPENED (July 2026). This record held only the years 1933 and 2015. The
Familiekunde Vlaanderen Westkust memorial-card index gives both in full — born
10 May 1933 at Oostende, died 20 November 2015 at Oostende — and names both partners,
Francine Bisschop and a Van Iseghem, which independently confirms the two marriages the
family had reported. Found by harvesting the surname from Open Archives rather than by
searching for him.

father. His own marriage act — Oostende nr. 10, 15 January 1927 (vrijwilligersrab

x Emma Celesta Vincke, matching this couple exactly; one of five such acts spanning
1920-1935 that all converge on the identical parent-name pair. He married **Angela
Coralia Gunst**. Index only, not the register image, hence `sup`. The act does not give
his own birth date, so none is recorded here (not in the reachable pages).

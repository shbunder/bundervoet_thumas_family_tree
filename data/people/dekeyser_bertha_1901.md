A newly-found child of Édouard Dekeyser × Louise Bocklandt — born 7 Aug 1901 Oostende, three months after her parents' 4 May 1901 marriage. Her birth act names both parents (Eduardus de Keyser × Louisa Maria Bocklandt). Sister of Jérôme (1897), Gustavus (1899), Maria (1902) and the two infants Angela Luciana (†1903) and Cesar Mauritius (†1905). Her own later life is not yet traced.

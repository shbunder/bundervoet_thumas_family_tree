Elder brother of [[augusta]], and so a brother-in-law of Petrus Franciscus Bundervoet.

From his own Oostende death registration of 17 January 1941, which names his parents as
Petrus Jacobus and Clemencia Sophia Wagebaert and his wife as Alicia Mathildis St Martin.
The birth year of 1877 is the one the death act implies rather than a stated date, so it is
recorded as a bare year.

The act is an index entry and has not been read as an image, which is why this stays `sup`.
His wife has no record of her own yet.

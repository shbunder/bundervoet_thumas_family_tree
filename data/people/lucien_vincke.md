A mason's assistant; son of Engel Antonius Vincke & Virginia Leynseele.

SEVEN MORE CHILDREN GRAFTED (research-log §64), from a province-wide sweep of the
vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index (`rab-bs-geboorten`) for Diksmuide, each act naming both
parents in full as Vincke Lucien Julianus x Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria: [[charles_vincke]]
(b. 30 Dec 1871), [[romanie_vincke]] (b. 7 Oct 1873 — one capture spells the father "Lucien
Julien", a forename variant, not a rival father), [[irma_vincke]] (b. 1 Nov 1874),
[[camilla_vincke]] (b. 20 Apr 1876), [[eugene_vincke]] (b. 23 Jul 1877), [[emma_vincke]]
(b. 23 Jan 1880, already held) and [[jerome_vincke]] (b. 22 Sep 1886). Filtering the same
index separately by father's forename and by mother's rare surname returns the identical
set — no rival couple. This closes the enumeration for Diksmuide **1871 onward**.

An EIGHTH CHILD, [[florence_vincke]] (b. Diksmuide 1869), is grafted separately from her
own 1893 marriage act ([[agatha-diksmuide-1893-marriage-bocklandt-vincke]]), not from a
birth act: Diksmuide's 1866-1870 birth rows carry only a bare father surname, no forename
and no mother, so parentage cannot be read from the index in that window. Her marriage
also joins this couple's line to the De Keyser/Bocklandt branch (objective 3).

THE 1866-1870 BLIND WINDOW, BROKEN (research-log §67, 2026-07-27). Diksmuide births in
those years name no parents in the index at all — but the couple's children's own LATER
acts do, the same route that recovered Florence Amelie. Two further routes closed most of
it: each blind-window child's own marriage act, and — crucially, because it reaches
children who died before ever marrying — each one's own death act.

TWO EXCLUDED, deliberately not grafted. "Medard Edmond Vincke" (blind-window birth 1866
nr. 214) is a different Vincke woman's child: his own death act (Diksmuide 1866 nr. 274)
names father NN NN, mother Vincke Philomena Helena. "Elodie Vincke" (blind-window birth

act names her parents as Richard Aloïs Vincke × Eugenie Dekeyrel, not this couple — a

own [[romanie_vincke]] ("Romanie Elodie", act 206/1873, parents named in full) is not the
same person as the excluded 1870 Elodie above — exactly the near-miss class CLAUDE.md's

FIVE STILL NOT PROVEN, a named frontier rather than a link: **Emil Fidèle** (1867 nr.
256), **Gustave Camil** (1867 nr. 266), **Florida Juliana** (1869 nr. 74), **Arthur

no parent-naming act found for any of them, in either the marriage or the death index.
Scope actually covered, so the wall stays re-openable rather than closed: the whole
West-Flemish marriage index (all communes, all years, Vincke + Vyncke, eight spellings of
the mother's surname); the whole West-Flemish death index (Vincke, 2212 rows — Florida
returns zero province-wide); Open Archives Belgium-wide, twelve forename queries, 75
records. Diksmuide itself carries 26 Vincke-touching marriages 1880-1910 and 124 death
rows 1798-1910 — the commune **is** covered; these five are absent from it, not hidden
behind it. What would settle them is the register image behind the `SCAN nnn GSU`
reference on the AGATHA side, or a FamilySearch session on the vrijwilligersrab arks — not
a further index sweep. Also ambiguous: a Nieuwkerke marriage of "VINCKE Cyrille Camille" ×
"REUBRECHT Valerie Eugenie" (act nr. 4) is blank in every Detail field and two corpus
memorial cards confirm the couple existed (d. 22/05/1937) but name no parents — it neither
confirms nor refutes a link to Cyrille Camil above.

AN ARGUMENT, USED ONLY TO EXCLUDE, NEVER TO INCLUDE. Diksmuide birth acts are
strictly date-ordered (77 act-number/date pairs checked 1871-1910, zero violations), so
act-number spacing against a known birth rate can flag an implausible gap. The birth rate
is not constant, though — it declines from ~0.73-0.81 acts/day in the early 1870s to
~0.2-0.3 by 1900-1910 — and recomputed with the conservative in-period rate (~0.75), the
gap around the 1870 acts comes out to roughly 212-237 days, still under the ~270-300-day
floor a second full-term birth needs. This spacing argument is corroboration only, and it
is never grounds for a graft (CLAUDE.md rule 1): it agrees with the independent exclusion
of Medard Edmond above (~48 days' spacing, plausible only as a different mother's child,
which his own death act already settles), and it does **not** close the excluded Elodie
above (~282 days, borderline) — that case is settled by her own marriage act, a document,
not by this arithmetic.

Named as the child in a civil birth act at **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 8, 15 Mar 1876**,
22:00, born at home. **Sex is stated in the act** ("Man"), not read off the forename.

The father, [[bossin_joannes_1834]], self-declares (*aangever*), aged 41, born at
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, a *landbouwer*, marked *gehuwd*. The mother, [[wolf_maria_1836]], written
here *Anna Maria De Wolf*, is aged 39, born at Brussel, a *huishoudster*. Her age is an
exact match to her 1836-04-04 birthdate (her April birthday not yet due); the father's
age is a year short of his own 1834-01-02 — ordinary rounding, given the mother's exact
match on the same act.

**Double-attested by his own marriage, which also fixes his father's death.** His
marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 12 June 1897, to Maria Florentia Verbist of
Sterrebeek — restates his birth as 1876-03-15 Sint-Stevens-Woluwe directly, and names his
father as *Jan Baptist Bossin*, **deceased Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 19 March 1887** — an exact
match, to the day, to [[bossin_joannes_1834]]'s already-held death. His mother appears again as
*Anna Maria Dewolf*, aged 62, present and consenting.

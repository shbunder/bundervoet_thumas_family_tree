Granddaughter of [[bundervoet_petrus_1727]] through her mother
[[bundervoet_petronella_1754]], and so a blood relative of Renée and Léon under
objective 2 — the furthest this branch is yet documented across the Dutch border.

Her death act at Hontenisse, in Zeeuws-Vlaanderen, on **30 March 1814** gives her age as
27 and her birthplace as **Assenede**, names her parents as Karel Vermeeren and Petronella
Bundervoet, and gives her husband [[steijaert_livinus]], a *veldwachter* — a village
constable — also aged 27.

**The birth year is derived from a stated age, so it is approximate.** "27 jaar" in March
1814 puts her birth in 1786 or 1787 and the record says `~1787` rather than picking one.
Her parents married at Assenede in 1781, which is consistent and is the second identifier
that ties this act to that couple rather than to another Petronella Bundervoet.

The act has a scan at the Zeeuws Archief that has not been read; it would give the day of
her birth or at least the exact age, and where the couple were living.

Married [[bundervoet_livina_1760]] at Assenede in 1788. She died there in 1831.

He exists on one line of the Assenede marriage index and nothing more is stated — no
birth, no death, no trade, and none of them is guessed. The act itself would give his age
and his parents, and whether the couple had children, which is the open question: they
married in 1788 and she lived to 1831, so a household of some size should be findable in
the Assenede registers under objective 2.

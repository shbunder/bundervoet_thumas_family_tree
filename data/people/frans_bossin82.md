Named as the child in a civil birth act at **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 36, 11 Nov 1882**,
23:00, born at home. **Sex is stated in the act** ("Man"), not read off the forename.

The father, [[jbbossin]], self-declares (*aangever*), aged 48, born at
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, a *herbergier* — an exact match to his own 1834-01-02 birthdate (his
January birthday had passed). The mother, [[maria_wolf]], is aged 46, born at Brussel, a
*huishoudster* — an exact match to her own 1836-04-04 birthdate (her April birthday had
passed). Forty-six is late for a birth but not implausible for a last child.

The father's trade here, *herbergier* (innkeeper), is the first appearance in this
family's acts of the trade [[maria_wolf]] herself is later recorded keeping in her own
right after his death — see [[jbbossin]] for the fuller occupation history.

`sup`: an act analysis, not the register image. No later act naming him has been found in
this pass.

Source detail: Geneanet; daughter Joanna's 1902 Kraainem marriage act

Born in Everberg; married Maria Theresia Coekelberghs in 1873; died young (1889) at Kraainem. Named in daughter Joanna's 1902 marriage act.

CORRECTED (July 2026). This record gave his death place as Sint-Stevens-Woluwe.
the Kraainem marriage act of 3 February 1902 (akte nr. 2, [[S5]]), which names him as the bride's late father, states **Kraainem** — with the death
date, 12 August 1889, agreeing exactly. The same man, the wrong commune; the place is now
as the act has it. He had died thirteen years before his daughter married.

GRAFTED and CORRECTED (2026-07-26): his own marriage act — **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 6,
24 April 1873** — names his parents. This record carried them as a Geneanet claim flagged
*to verify*: "Joannes Baptista Vandenbemden × Agatha Langa". The act gives
[[jb_vandenbemden]] and [[elisabeth_langes]] — so the father was roughly right, the mother
was **Elisabeth Agatha**, not Agatha, and the surname was **Langes**, not Langa. The
Geneanet reading is retired. The act also gives his trade at twenty-three, *dienstbode*,
and the commune the marriage was passed in.

Two identifiers carry it beyond the surname: his birth, Everberg 19 August 1849, agreeing
with this record to the day, and the marriage date 24 April 1873, likewise. His death —
Kraainem, 12 August 1889 — is then confirmed a *third* time by his widow's 1903 remarriage
act, which names him as her late husband with that date and that commune.

Only his mother is recorded as present and consenting. His father is a bare name with no
residence, no trade and no consent, which is the register's usual shape for a parent
already dead; nothing is recorded from that silence beyond noting it on
[[jb_vandenbemden]].

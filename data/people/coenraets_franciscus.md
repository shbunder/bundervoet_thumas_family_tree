Father of the groom in his son [[coenraets_franciscus_1872]]'s marriage act — Kraainem nr. 4, 15 April
1899 (S27). A *winkelier*, a shopkeeper, of Kraainem, and alive at the wedding.

Father and son carry the same name in full, which is why the son's record says so explicitly:
two Franciscus Coenraets, one act.

His wife [[godts_anna]] had been dead twenty-four years by then.

FRONTIER: their marriage, roughly 1865–1872 at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe or Kraainem.

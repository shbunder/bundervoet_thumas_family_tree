Source detail: Stene marriage act nr. 9, 1 March 1924 (FamilySearch register image, read
directly — [[fs-stene-1924-marriage-blomme-bocklandt]]); Familiekunde Westkust memorial
card ([[S17]]); Geneanet tree wete1998 (Erik Bekaert, for the ancestors above her parents).

Born 31 Dec 1877 in Hamme (OOST-VLAANDEREN, Waasland) — the SAME town as her first husband
Édouard Dekeyser (both Hamme natives who moved to Oostende). This is the true source of the
long 'Hamme' confusion: it was the couple's shared birth-town, not Hamme-Merchtem and not a
marriage place. Married Édouard 4 May 1901 (the marriage legitimized their two eldest
children); they DIVORCED on 14 November 1922, by decree of the Rechtbank van eersten aanleg
te Brugge. Died 1946 Oostende, buried at Stene. Her Bocklandt family is a Hamme (O-Vl) /
Waasland family documented back to the 1760s (see her parents and the Bocklandt / Van
Bergen ancestors).

SHE REMARRIED (July 2026). A Familiekunde Westkust memorial-card record ([[S17]]) gives her
death as **15 July 1946 at Oostende** — the tree had only the year — with her birthplace
Hamme, and names her partner as **Petrus Blomme**.

Not Édouard. She and [[dekeyser_edouard_1876]] divorced in 1922, and this is the first sign of what
she did afterwards: she married again. The chronology is quietly striking — Édouard
remarried Leontine Schreel at Oostende on 9 May 1946, and Louise died nine weeks later, on
15 July, a Blomme.

FOUND (2026-07-26), closing the frontier this record used to carry. The Bocklandt–Blomme
marriage act itself: Stene, 1 March 1924, akte nr. 9, register image read directly
([[fs-stene-1924-marriage-blomme-bocklandt]]). She is a *werkvrouw*, born Hamme 31 December
1877 — exact to the day and to the commune — daughter of Eduardus Bocklandt (d. Oostende
4 Jul 1907) and Maria Louisa Van Den Bergen (d. Oostende 22 Nov 1888), and the act states in
full that she is the *gescheiden echtgenoote* of Eduardus De Keyser, divorced by the
Rechtbank van eersten aanleg te Brugge on 14 November 1922 — dating the divorce this record
had only as `~1923`. The groom, [[blomme_petrus_1884]], is given as *werkman*, born Stene 23
January 1884, son of Josephus Blomme (living, Oostende) and Mathilde Sophia Meseure (d.
Oostende 21 Jul 1922).

A TRAP FOR THE NEXT READER. FamilySearch's own index dates this act **1 May 1924**. The
register itself reads *"den eersten Maart"* — 1 March — confirmed by the facing act (nr. 8,
23 February 1924) and by the banns, published Sunday 10 February 1924: neither fits a May
wedding. The index's own raw month field reads `"Maand": "Maa"`, which the site auto-expands
to the English "May" instead of the Dutch "Maart" — a demonstrable indexing bug, not a
second date to reconcile. The register, not the index, is what is cited here.

The migration generation: born in the Thumas heartland of Grez-Doiceau, he broke the four-generation carpentry tradition to become a farmer and innkeeper, moving north to Kraainem and then Sint-Stevens-Woluwe — founding the Kraainem Thumas family. Widowed of Antoina Bossin (d. 1894), he later partnered Maria Dehoux.

DOCUMENTED (July 2026) as the bridegroom's father in the Kraainem marriage act of 3 February 1902 (akte nr. 2, [[S5]]), living at Kraainem, with
his trade given as *landbouwer en herbergier* — exactly the occupation this record already
carried, which is itself a check that this is the same man. The act fixes him as the
father of [[jbgeorgius]] and the husband of [[bossin]].

His own marriage act — Kraainem, 20 June 1872, naming his parents as Georges Thumas and
Maria Catharina Joostens — is held in the harvested corpus and not yet read on AGATHA.

Also 'Schabbe'. Married Judoca Vandenberghe 1616; nine children incl. Simon Sabbe (1627). Witness at the wedding was his uncle Michiel Sabbe (b.1557). Parents Willem Sabbe x Clara Vande Putte. The Sabbe line runs back to Judocus 'Joos' Sabbe (~1475) around Harelbeke.

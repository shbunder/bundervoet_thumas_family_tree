Son of [[bocklandt_edouard_1839]] and [[vanbergen_marielouise_1842]], and [[bocklandt_louise_1877]]'s youngest
known brother. His marriage act — Oostende nr. 100, 12 May 1900
([[agatha-oostende-1900-marriage-bocklandt-mewis]]) — names him born at Hamme 10 June
1880, *touwslagersknecht*, resident at Oostende, son of Eduardus Bocklandt (62, still
*touwslagersknecht*) and the already-deceased Maria Louisa Vanbergen. He married **Rosalia
Cecilia Mewis** (b. Oostende 29 Sep 1881, *werkmeid*), daughter of Franciscus Antonius
Mewis and Theresia Lomard of Oostende. AGATHA serves this act only as an "analyse van akte"
(index transcription), not the register image, hence `sup`.

Born at Hamme just two days after his infant brother [[bocklandt_ludovicus_1875]] died in the
same town — 10 June against 8 June 1880. That is close enough to read the marriage act's
birthdate with some caution: it is a date recalled twenty years later, not a contemporary
record, and the true gap between the two events may not be quite this narrow.

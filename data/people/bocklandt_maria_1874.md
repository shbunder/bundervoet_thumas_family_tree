Daughter of [[bocklandt_edouard_1839]] and [[vanbergen_marielouise_1842]], and [[bocklandt_louise_1877]]'s
sister. Her marriage act — Oostende nr. 42, 26 March 1892
([[agatha-oostende-1892-marriage-bocklandt-debuf]]) — names her born at Hamme 9 March
1874, *werkmeid*, resident at Oostende, daughter of Eduardus Bocklandt (54,
*touwslagersknecht*) and the already-deceased Maria Ludovica Vanbergen (d. Oostende
22 Nov 1888). She married **Fredericus Desiderius Debuf** (b. Oostende 28 Sep 1871,
*matroos bij het zeewezen*), whose own father, Desiderius Joannes Debuf, was lost at sea —
*"kwam vermoedelijk op zee om"* — per an act of bekendheid of 23 February 1892, days before
this wedding. AGATHA serves this act only as an "analyse van akte" (index transcription),
not the register image, hence `sup`.

Her husband very likely stood as informant, "Fredericus Debuf, 33, matroos bij de
staatsmarine", at the 1903 Oostende death of her niece [[dekeyser_angela]].

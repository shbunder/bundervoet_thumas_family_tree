[[coekelberghs]]'s second husband, married at Kraainem on 6 November 1903 (act nr. 16) —
fourteen years after she was widowed of [[hendrik_vdb]], and he a widower himself.

Born at Bertem on 23 November 1836, which is her own birth commune, and an *herbergier* at
Zaventem, which is her own trade: she is recorded as *herbergierster* on her daughter's
1902 marriage act. Two innkeepers from the same village, marrying in their sixties.

He marries into this tree rather than descending in it: nothing here claims a blood
relation, and he is recorded because the act that names him is the same act that gives
[[jb_coekelberghs]] and [[anna_haesaerts]] their death dates.

`sup`: an AGATHA act analysis (Demogen project huvlb20), not the register image. Beware the
Open Archives mirror of this act — its rendered HTML gives all three death dates one day
earlier than both its own API record and the AGATHA page.

FRONTIER — his first marriage and his parents, neither read. A widower of Bertem born in
1836 should have both a first marriage and a first wife's death in the Bertem or Zaventem
registers.

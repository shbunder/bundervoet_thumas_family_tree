The first sibling this tree has for [[peremans]]. She married **Petrús van der Varent** at
**Zaventem on 5 November 1840** (S29), her parents given as [[egidius_peremans]] and
[[joanna_verelst]] — the same pair, with the same distinctive three-part maternal name, that
her sister's 1846 act gives.

Her father had been dead three and a half years by then; her mother had three years to live.
Her sister married in the same commune six years later.

No birth date: the record gives her none, not even an age, so this record has none either.

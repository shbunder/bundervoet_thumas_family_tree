Mother of [[bogaert_petronilla]], and through her a direct ancestor of Renée and Léon.

Like her husband [[bogaert_petrus]] she is here on the strength of one line of the Evergem
burial register of 31 August 1792, which names her daughter's parents. Nothing else about
her is stated and nothing else is entered.

Taedts is a rare surname in this corpus, which makes her the better of the two to search
on: a marriage or a burial at Evergem would place the family, where Bogaert alone would
return too many.

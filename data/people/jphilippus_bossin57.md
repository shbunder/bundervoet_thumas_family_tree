Named as the child in a civil birth act at **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 15, 28 Jun 1857**,
10:30. **Sex is stated in the act** ("Man"), not read off the forename.

The father, [[jbbossin]], self-declares (*vaderlands kind aangever*), aged 24, born at
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, a *papiermaekersgast*, marked *ongehuwd* (unmarried). The mother,
[[maria_wolf]], is aged 21, born at Brussel, a *dagloonster*, also marked *ongehuwd*. Her
age is an exact match to the 1836-04-04 birthdate her own marriage act gives (her April
birthday had already passed); the father's age is a year short of the same act's
1834-01-02 — ordinary rounding, not a conflict, given the mother's exact match on the
same document.

**FLAG, not resolved: born before his parents' marriage.** [[jbbossin]] and [[maria_wolf]]
married at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on **17 June 1858** — about eleven months *after* this
birth. Both dates stand exactly as their own acts give them; neither is altered to make
them agree. Legitimation by a marriage that follows the birth is the obvious reading, and
this tree documents the same shape elsewhere ([[joannes_vi]], flagged for the same
reason). It is recorded here as an open question, not resolved.

**Double-attested, fifty years on.** His own marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 25 May
1907, to Maria Theresia De Rom, a widow of Neerijse — restates his birth as 1857-06-28
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe directly, and names his mother as *Maria Dewolf*, aged 71, a match to
1836-04-04 exact. By 1907 he had lived at Pironchamps (Hainaut) and returned; his trade
there is given as *fabriekswerker*.

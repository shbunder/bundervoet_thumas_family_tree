A FIFTH CHILD of [[guilielmus_bossin]] and [[peremans]] (2026-07-27), found as a witness
at his sister [[ludovica_bossin]]'s wedding — Kraainem, 25 February 1884 — aged 21,
already a *schaliedekker*. That appearance names no parents; it is his own **marriage
act**, Kraainem, **23 November 1887** (the act that also brought [[guns_mc]] into the
tree), that states them directly: **Guillelmus Bossin**, landbouwer, and **Joanna
Catharina Peremans**, landbouwster, both of Kraainem. It also gives his own birth as
**Kraainem, 1 January 1863**.

**Two identifiers carry the parent link, not one.** First, the parent names on his own
1887 act, matching [[guilielmus_bossin]] and [[peremans]] exactly. Second, and
independent of the first: [[guilielmus_bossin]]'s death — **Kraainem, 10 October 1888** —
recurs identically, to the day, in two further acts for two *different* children five
years apart: his sister [[ludovica_bossin]]'s 1901 remarriage and his brother
[[joannes_franciscus_bossin]]'s 1896 Werchter marriage. Two unconnected documents
agreeing to the day is what confirms this is the same Guilielmus Bossin family and not a
namesake — a real risk in this commune, since [[jbbossin]] already turned out to be an
entirely different, unconnected Joannes Baptista Bossin two generations earlier.

**Age consistency across five appearances (1884–1902) is a DERIVED check, not an
independent identifier.** Against the 1 January 1863 birth: 21 at the 1884 wedding
(exact), 32 at the 1896 Werchter wedding (one year short of the 33 the date implies), 36
at a 1900 Kraainem act (one year short of 37), 38 at his sister's 1901 remarriage (exact),
39 at a 1902 Kraainem act (exact). Two of five a year short is ordinary rounding, the same
pattern already accepted on [[jbbossin]]'s own age record — it shows one continuous man,
which the parent-name-plus-death-date pair above had already established; it adds no new
identifying weight of its own.

**The mother's forename is the already-reconciled variant, not a new dispute.** The 1887
act calls her *Joanna Catharina Peremans* — closer to [[peremans]]'s own anchor act
(*Joanna Catharina Jacoba*) than the *Anna Catharina* several other acts in this family
use. It applies the standing reconciliation (peremans.md, joannes_bossin51.md,
research-log §65) and changes nothing.

Joannes Bundervoet's SECOND wife (married ~1745, died 1750) — NOT the mother of
Christoffel or Pieter; both were by his first wife, Livina Stockman. Several trees
confuse the two wives.

GIVEN NAME DISPUTED. This record calls her Livina; the paulderidder tree calls her
**Joanna de Wilde**, and gives her no dates at all — the 1750 death here comes from
glorieuxp. Both trees are cited below, and they do not agree, so the name is held
loosely: "Livina" is what this record has always said and is kept for stability, but it
is not settled. The confusion is easy to see how it started, since the first wife was
also a Livina. FRONTIER: the ~1745 Evergem marriage act would name her outright and
settle it.

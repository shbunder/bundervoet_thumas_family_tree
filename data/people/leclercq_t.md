CORROBORATED (July 2026) as a mother. Her son Georges's death act (Grez-Doiceau, 20
November 1808) names his mother as *Marie Leclercq*, alongside the father Lambert Thumas —
which puts this couple, and her motherhood of the line's Georges, on a civil act.

Second wife of Jean Lambert Thumas (m. Wavre 1743) and mother of the line's Georges (1744); the couple had eight children.

Mother of the groom in her son [[ludovicus_bossin79]]'s marriage act — Kraainem nr. 3, 10
April 1900 (S28). A *herbergierster* of Sint-Stevens-Woluwe: she kept an inn, as her son's
new father-in-law [[georges_cj]] did at Kraainem.

Widowed since 1887 and running the house herself thirteen years later — the act gives her the
trade in her own right, not her husband's.

No dates: the act gives none. *Wolf* is rare enough in this corner of Brabant to be searchable
on its own, which is the cheap next move.

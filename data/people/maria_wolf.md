Mother of the groom in her son [[ludovicus_bossin79]]'s marriage act — Kraainem nr. 3, 10
April 1900 (S28). A *herbergierster* of Sint-Stevens-Woluwe: she kept an inn, as her son's
new father-in-law [[georges_cj]] did at Kraainem.

Widowed since 1887 and running the house herself thirteen years later — the act gives her the
trade in her own right, not her husband's.

**BIRTH DATED, AND A HARD CHILDHOOD NAMED (2026-07-27).** Her own marriage act — Sint-
Stevens-Woluwe nr. 4, **17 June 1858** (S33) — gives her birth as **4 April 1836,
Brussel**, a *dienstmeyt*, and records her as a *verlaten kind* (abandoned child): she
declared under oath that her mother's last residence was unknown to her and that she had
never known her, which her witnesses confirmed under oath. The act nonetheless names her
mother formally, [[petronella_wolf]], a *dagloonster* — presumably from her own birth
record rather than personal knowledge — and names no father for her at all. Both facts are
recorded as the act gives them, without resolving one into the other.

**The couple's five further children, all read from acts naming this exact birthdate
against her stated age, agreeing to the year in five of five:** 21 in 1857
([[jphilippus_bossin57]]), 22 in 1859 ([[pamandus_bossin59]]), 39 in 1876
([[janphilip_bossin76]]), 46 in 1882 ([[frans_bossin82]]), and 43 on
[[ludovicus_bossin79]]'s own 1879 birth act. See [[jbbossin]] for the couple's fuller
record, including a sixth child, [[gjosef_bossin69]] (b. Kraainem 1869), known only from
his own 1896 marriage act, which also independently restates her husband's death.

**Her surname is spelled five ways across these acts**: *Dewolf* (1857), *Wolf* (1859 and
her own 1858 marriage), *Anna Maria De Wolf* (1876), *Wolf* again (1882), *Anna Maria
Dewolf* (her son [[janphilip_bossin76]]'s 1897 marriage, age 62) and *Maria Anna Dewolf*
([[gjosef_bossin69]]'s 1896 marriage) — one woman, several clerks, across more than forty
years of documents.

Her own trade moves too: *dienstmeyt* at her 1858 marriage, *dagloonster* (1857),
unstated (1859), *huishoudster* (1876, 1882, 1897), *herbergierster* in her own right by
1900, after widowhood.

*Wolf* is rare enough in this corner of Brabant to be searchable on its own, which is the
cheap next move — as is [[petronella_wolf]]'s own record, which names no known husband at
all.

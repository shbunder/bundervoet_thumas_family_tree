DEEPEST-TIER ANCESTOR — born ~1500, Oostkamp. Father of Barbara Wittenheyns (~1535). The Wittenheyns of Oostkamp appear on two independent lines of ours (also
[[mattheus_wittenheyns]] ~1500, via the Vandewalle line); whether Petrus and Mattheus
are brothers is not established. No parents recorded.

CHECKED AND REJECTED (July 2026) — the duplicate detector flags Petrus and Mattheus as
possibly one person, on a rare surname plus a birth year of about 1500. They are not.
Four things separate them, and none is a spelling difference:

- different given names — Mattheus and Petrus are not variants of one another;
- different wives — Judoca 't Jonck here, Maria De Ruwe there;
- different deaths — before 1575 here, about 1546 at Oostkamp there;
- different children — Barbara (~1535) here; Mattheus's children are named with him in
  the 1547 Bruges orphan register.

Two men of one uncommon surname, born about the same time in the same parish, is what a
pair of brothers or cousins looks like. The warning is expected to keep firing, because
it is derived from surname and year alone; this note is the answer to it.

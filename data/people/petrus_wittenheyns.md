DEEPEST-TIER ANCESTOR — born ~1500, Oostkamp. Father of Barbara Wittenheyns (~1535). The Wittenheyns of Oostkamp appear on two independent lines of ours (also Mattheus Wittenheyns ~1500 via the Vandewalle line); whether Petrus and Mattheus are brothers is not established. No parents recorded.

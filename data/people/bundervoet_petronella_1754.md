One of the eight children of [[bundervoet_petrus_1727]] and [[dedeckere_marie_1731]]. Their father was
the younger brother of [[bundervoet_christoffel_1709]], through whom this tree's line runs, so these eight are
first cousins of [[bundervoet_philippus_1756]]'s generation — blood relatives of Renée and Léon under
objective 2.

Recorded first from the paulderidder tree's list of the couple's children, which gave two
years and nothing else. **She now has a marriage and a daughter.** The Assenede register
has her marriage to [[vermeire_carolus]] in **1781**; and a death act at Hontenisse, over
the border in Zeeuws-Vlaanderen, records their daughter [[vermeeren_maria_1787]] in 1814,
aged 27, born Assenede, naming her parents as Karel Vermeeren and Petronella Bundervoet.

Two independent documents in two countries name the same couple, and Assenede is where
this household already was — her father was buried there in 1777 and her sister
[[bundervoet_coleta_1766]] was born there. That is what settles which Petronella
Bundervoet this is: the corpus holds at least two others of the same generation, a
Petronella Judoca who married Jacobus Baudonck at Lotenhulle and a Petronille Thérèse who
married André Lybaert at Evergem, and neither is at Assenede.

Index-level on both, so `sup`. The Hontenisse act has a scan that has not been read.

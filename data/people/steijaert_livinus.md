Husband of [[vermeeren_maria_1787]], who died at Hontenisse in 1814. The death act gives
his age as 27 — hence `~1787` — and his trade as *veldwachter*, the village constable.

That is everything the act says about him: no birthplace, no parents, no death. A
*veldwachter* was an appointed municipal officer, so the Hontenisse commune records would
know more about him than the parish registers will.

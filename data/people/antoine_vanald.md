THE DEATH DAY RETRACTED (2026-07-27). This record held 21 July 1808 as a day-level death
date. That value is the death act's **Certificate Date** column — the day the death was
*registered* — not a stated day of death; the act's own Date column is blank. No two of the
three transcriptions that give a day for his death agree with each other: **6 July 1808**
(his widow's 1818 remarriage act, Previous-Partners table —
`data/artifacts/looten-wyllie-1818-marriage-diksmuide.md`), **16 July 1808** (his son
[[petrus_vanald]]'s 1828 marriage act) and **21 July 1808** (the certificate date, this
death act — `data/artifacts/vanalderweireldt-antoine-1808-death-diksmuide.md`). July 1808 is
solid across all three; the day is not, so the day-level precision is withdrawn and only the
month is held. What would settle it: the death act's own register image (FamilySearch film
4794755, 403 unauthenticated) or either son's 1828 marriage act image.

FOUR PARENTS NAMED, NONE GRAFTED. The 1803 act names his parents **Vanaldeweireldt Jacques
Ignace × Verhaeghe Isabella Claire**, both said to have been born at Langemark (his mother
at Diksmuide). The document was reached and read; the generation above it is still a
frontier, not a link — the same standard this pass applied throughout. The mother, Isabella
Claire/Clara Verhaeghe, leans toward acceptance: named identically across **three**
independent acts (this one, his own 1808 death act, and the 1857 death act of a probable
sister below), with no rival family found. The father does not clear the same bar: he is
**Jacques Ignace** here, **François** on the 1808 death act, and **Jacobus Ignatius** on the
1857 act — the same man beyond real doubt, but no two of the three readings of his forename
agree. No father/mother is set on this record.

A PROBABLE ELDER SISTER, NOT GRAFTED. **Maria Joanna Vanalderweireldt**, died Diksmuide act
211, certificate 30 September 1857, aged 82y 8m 28d, born Diksmuide, father Jacobus
Ignatius, mother Isabella Clara Verhaeghe — a third act naming that parent pair. Working
back from the certificate date, 82y 8m 28d before 30 September 1857 lands on 2 January 1775
almost exactly (checked by computation) — about 5.3 years before this record's own birth, a
plausible elder sister. But she cannot be linked as a sibling while the parents themselves

same registration lag documented above — so this stays "about 1775-01-02", a named frontier
in prose, not a person file.

TWO FRONTIERS SCOPED AND LEFT OPEN. No death act for **Jacobus Ignatius Vanalderweireldt**
or **Isabella Clara Verhaeghe** was found in the Diksmuide death index under any spelling
tried — they most plausibly died before civil registration began, i.e. they sit in the
parish registers. And the Diksmuide **birth** index for 1796–1815 is a blind window of

sibship cannot be fixed from the birth side — only from the marriage and death acts above.

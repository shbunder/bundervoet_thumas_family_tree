Wife of [[bundervoet_christoffel_1709]], and the mother of the line: their son
[[bundervoet_philippus_1756]] is the direct ancestor of Renée and Léon.

**The Evergem burial register of 1792 supplies almost everything this record used to
lack.** It held a bare `death: 1792` from the fauconier parenteel and nothing else — no
birth, no parents. The register entry gives her burial on **31 August 1792**, a birth year
of **1711**, and her own parents, [[bogaert_petrus]] and [[taedts_maria]] — a generation
the tree did not have at all.

Two independent identifiers licence the join: the death year 1792, which the held record
already carried from a different source, and the husband, named in the register as
Christophorus Bundervoet.

**The date is the burial, not the death.** The register gives no date of death, so the year
stands as the fact and the day of burial sits beside it in `raw` rather than being
collapsed into a death date the source never stated. The same distinction her
brother-in-law's record draws — see [[bundervoet_petrus_1727]], buried at Assenede in 1777.

Read from the FamilySearch index entry, not from the register image, so `sup` throughout.
Reading the image would give the burial in the parish's own words, and might name a
widower — which would date [[bundervoet_christoffel_1709]]'s death against the 1786 the
tree holds.

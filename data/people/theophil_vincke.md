Son of [[lucien_vincke]] and [[ludovica_vanald]], and [[emma_vincke]]'s brother — one of
four children this pass recovers from the couple's **1866-1870 blind window**: the
Diksmuide birth index there gives only the bare father surname VINCKE, no forename, no
mother, and a birth date recorded as `YYYY/00/00`. His own birth act (Diksmuide 1866 nr.
175) is one of those bare rows, so his parentage comes from his own marriage, 29 years
later, not from his birth act.

His marriage — Diksmuide, 13 December 1893, act nr. 239 (`rab-bs-huwelijken`) — gives him
born Diksmuide 23 July 1866 (matching act nr. 175 exactly), age 27, *schoenmaker*, and
names his parents as Vincke Lucien Julien (53, metser) × Vanderweirelt Louise Marie (48,
kantwerkster). His own death — Oostende, 22 April 1922, act nr. 280
(`rab-bs-overlijdens`) — repeats the parent names (Vincke Lucien Julien × Vanalderweireldt
Louise Maria) 29 years later, in a different register and commune, and separately names
his surviving spouse, Bolle Valerie Maria, matching the 1893 bride exactly. Full
parent-name agreement across two independent registers 29 years apart, plus the
death act's own spouse cross-check, is what carries the graft.

**The mother's surname on the 1893 act reads VANDERWEIRELT — the "-AL-" dropped — which is
why this row is invisible to a query for Vanalderweireldt.** Accepted as the same woman:
this pass's own marriage-index sweep queried eight spellings of the surname
(Vanalderweireldt, Vanalderweirelt, Vanderweirelt, Vanderweireldt, Alderweireldt,
Vanaldeweireldt, Vanalderwerelt, Vandeweireldt) precisely because the family name is
transcribed inconsistently across acts, and `Louise`/`Ludovica` is a documented forename
fold — `data/forenames.json`'s `f` block groups `ludovica`, `lodewijka` and `louise`
together, sex-split so the fold can never cross into a male form.

**A rival, ruled out, is the reason this needed care.** A second Diksmuide man,
*Theophile Achille Vincke*, son of Adolf Vincke × Celina Maria Vandenberghe, married
Delaleeuwe Eugenia in 1903 and died at Diksmuide in 1959
(`fwk:e427e82c-0ce3-5a43-afac-4eea747ce86a`) — same forename pair, same surname, same
commune. Distinguished by parents, spouse and death year: name and surname alone would
have been dangerous here.

Index only, not the register image, hence `sup`.

Mother of the groom in her son [[hendrik_vdb]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 6,
24 April 1873. A *landbouwster* of Everberg, present at the wedding and consenting to it,
and recorded as unable to write.

Everberg is where her son was born, in August 1849, so the family was still there
twenty-four years later.

**She is a correction, not only an addition.** [[hendrik_vdb]]'s record carried, as an
unverified Geneanet claim, a mother named *Agatha Langa*. The act names her **Elisabeth
Agatha** and spells the surname **Langes**. The forename was incomplete and the surname
was wrong; both are now as the act has them, and the Geneanet reading is retired.

The act gives her no age, so this record carries no birth date. The death entry records
only that she was alive on the day.

`sup`: an AGATHA act analysis, not the register image.

FRONTIER — the spelling is worth pinning before it is searched on. *Langes* as written here
also occurs as Lange, Langhe and Langens in Brabant registers, and the Geneanet reading
*Langa* is a fourth. Her own marriage or her death act at Everberg would settle it, and a
surname that rare is one of the few that can be searched on its own.

Father of the groom in his son [[ludovicus_bossin79]]'s marriage act — Kraainem nr. 3, 10
April 1900 (S28), which gives his death as **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 19 March 1887**. His son was
seven.

The act gives him no age and no trade, so this record has neither.

**Whether he belongs to [[bossin]]'s family is the open question of this act** — the same
surname, the same small commune, the same generation. It is not answered here and not assumed.

FRONTIER: his 1887 death act and his marriage to [[maria_wolf]], either of which would name
his parents and settle it.

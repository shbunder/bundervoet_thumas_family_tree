Wife of [[sabbe_judocus_1475]] (~1475–1534) and, on that reading, the mother of
[[sabbe_petrus_1504]] — though isavdw states the marriage and not the maternity, so
the parent link is deliberately not made here.

Her given name is not recorded; only the family name survives. Written the same way
as [[elisabeth]], the opposite case — a given name and no surname. A name half
known is recorded half, never completed.

No relation to [[vandenberghe_judoca_1594]] is established: that is a different
Vandenberghe, a century later and in a different parish. Same surname is not a link.

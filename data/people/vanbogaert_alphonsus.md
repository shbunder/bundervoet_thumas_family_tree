Son of [[vanbogaert_carolus_1819]] and [[martinet_maria_1838]], and so a brother of [[vanbogaert_maria_1861]] — an uncle of
Léonie Paelinck.

From his own Sint-Niklaas marriage act of 7 April 1909, which names both his parents and his
bride, **Rosalia Moerloos**, daughter of Vitalis Moerloos and Nathalia Van Acker.

No dates of his own: the act is indexed under the marriage and has not been read as an image.
FRONTIER — reading it would give both spouses' ages and therefore his birth year, and his
wife's family are three more people the act already names.

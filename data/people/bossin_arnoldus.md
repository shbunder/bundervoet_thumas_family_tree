Father of the groom in his son [[bossin_guilielmus_1824]]'s marriage act — Zaventem, act nr.
1, 24 February 1846 (S19), read as an image. The act puts him at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe,
working as an *arbeider*, present at the wedding and consenting to it. He signed with a
mark: the act states he could not write.

The death entry says only that he was alive on the day of the act. He is nowhere given
an age, so this record carries no birth date — his son was 21, which places him loosely
in the 1780s or 1790s and no closer than that.

FRONTIER — his own marriage to [[deyn_elisabeth]], which would name his parents, and
which the Sint-Stevens-Woluwe or Zaventem registers should hold for roughly 1815–1824.
Neither has been searched for him.

ACT READ (2026-07-26): named again seven years later, as father of the bride in his
daughter [[bossin_anna_1829]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 9, 1 December 1853
(S22). Still at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, present and consenting, still unable to write, and
his trade written *daglooner* where the 1846 act had said *arbeider*. The two words say the
same thing; the record keeps each act's own.

He is therefore alive on 1 December 1853, and the death bound moves from 1846 to that date.
His known children go from one to two.

Named as mother of the bride in her daughter Octavia's 1906 Oostende marriage act (nr.
258, S20), read as an image — "overleden" (deceased) by that date, no date given there.
The Bredene birth act (nr. 116, 1886, S21), also read as an image, names her as the
declarant's wife, age 42, occupation "huishoudster", "geboortig van Brugge" (native of
Bruges) — a birthplace stated directly by the act, though the act gives no birth date
for her, only her age.

FRONTIER — her age of 42 in the 1886 act implies a birth around 1844, but that is a
derivation from an age, not a birth date, and is not recorded as one. The
vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index carries an unread entry giving Brugge, 17 March 1844 —
consistent with the derived year, but not read at image level, so not graftable. Her
own parents, also index-only and unread: Petrus Standaert x Anna Deckers.

Each needs the underlying act image read before it becomes a fact (verification pass,
2026-07-26).

Father of Joanna (Janneken) Verbrugghe (~1655–1704), and so a direct ancestor through
her son Joannes Bundervoet (~1682).

Named as her father in the paulderidder tree's ancestry chart; the tree gives him no
dates at all and no wife, which is why this record carries none either. Joanna's
record previously said her parents were "not recorded here" — they were, and this
corrects that.

FRONTIER: everything about him. Evergem parish registers for a Verbrugghe–? marriage
in the 1640s–50s would be the first place to look, since a daughter born ~1655 puts
the marriage a little before it.

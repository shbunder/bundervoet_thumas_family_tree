Source detail: Rijksarchief AGATHA (parents on her record); Geneanet tree wete1998 (Erik Bekaert); Édouard's 1946 marriage act (records their divorce)

Born 31 Dec 1877 in Hamme (OOST-VLAANDEREN, Waasland) — the SAME town as her husband Édouard Dekeyser (both Hamme natives who moved to Oostende). This is the true source of the long 'Hamme' confusion: it was the couple's shared birth-town, not Hamme-Merchtem and not a marriage place. Married Édouard 4 May 1901 (the marriage legitimized their two eldest children); they later DIVORCED (~1923, decree Rechtbank van eersten aanleg Brugge — per Édouard's 1946 remarriage act). Died 1946 Oostende, buried at Stene. Her Bocklandt family is a Hamme (O-Vl) / Waasland family documented back to the 1760s (see her parents and the Bocklandt / Van Bergen ancestors).

SHE REMARRIED (July 2026). A Familiekunde Westkust memorial-card record ([[S17]]) gives her
death as **15 July 1946 at Oostende** — the tree had only the year — with her birthplace Hamme,
and names her partner as **Petrus Blomme**.

Not Édouard. She and [[edouard_dk]] divorced around 1923, and this is the first sign of what
she did afterwards: she married again. The chronology is quietly striking — Édouard remarried
Leontine Schreel at Oostende on 9 May 1946, and Louise died nine weeks later, on 15 July, a
Blomme.

Petrus Blomme has no record here: the card names him and nothing more. FRONTIER — the
Bocklandt–Blomme marriage act, which would date the remarriage and give his parents. It is
post-1923 Oostende, so it sits at the Stadsarchief rather than in AGATHA.

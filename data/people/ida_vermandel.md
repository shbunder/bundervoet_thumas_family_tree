Maternal grandmother of Louise Bocklandt; wife of Joannes Franciscus Van Bergen.

ACT READ (2026-07-26) — **Hamme death act nr. 123, 1865**, read as the register image. She
had been in this tree with a bare year at each end and no place at all. The act gives:

> …dat gisteren, den een en twintigsten Juny, om half negen uren 's avonds, **Ida
> Vermandel**, oud negen en dertig jaren, *arbeidster*, **geboortig van Zeltzaete**,
> wonende te Hamme, **huisvrouw van Joannes Franciscus Van Bergen**, dochter van nog
> levende […] Vermandel en van […] Verplancken, overleden is in het burgerhospitael…

She died on **21 June 1865** at Hamme, in the town hospital on the Marktplein, aged
thirty-nine and working as a labourer. Two identifiers carry the identification and neither
is her name: the commune, which [[marie_vanbergen]]'s 1888 Oostende death act independently
gives as where Ida died, and her husband's full name, matching [[jan_van_bergen]] exactly.
*Ida Vermandel* is in any case close to unique — the only other in the whole Open Archives
index is a 1949 death in France.

**Her birthplace is Zelzate, not Waasmunster**, and that needs stating carefully, because
§56 had just moved this family's assumed origin from Hamme to Waasmunster on her daughter's
evidence. Both are true and they are two different people: Marie-Louise was born at
Waasmunster in 1842, Ida at Zelzate around 1825. Zelzate is not in the Waasland at all — it
is on the Dutch border north of Gent. Her own birth act and her parents are therefore in
the **Zelzate** registers, and the marriage could be in any of three communes.

Her age, thirty-nine in June 1865, puts her birth between June 1825 and June 1826. The held
`1825` survives that without being confirmed by it, so the year stays as it was.

FRONTIER — **her parents are named in this act and are deliberately not recorded.** It says
they were *nog levende*, still living, in June 1865, which is itself a search constraint.
But the forenames do not survive magnification: the father's is a looped capital followed
by *-rimus*, where P, F and S are all plausible against the same clerk's *Petrus* and
*Franciscus* elsewhere on the page, and the mother's renders as something between
*Josephina* and *Delphina*. The surnames are legible — **Vermandel** and **Verplancken**.
A forename read at the edge of legibility is exactly the guess this project has no syntax
for, so nothing is written. Her Zelzate birth act settles both, and the Zelzate registers
are mapped: `fs-zelzate-civil-registers-1796-1870`, with a ten-year table covering
1802–1870 (DGS 005089047) that turns it into a single lookup.

FRONTIER — two more probable children, index-level only and not grafted: **Josephus**
(b. 1852, m. Clementina Van Buyten at Hamme 1911, d. Hamme 27 October 1927) and **Henricus**
(b. 1861, d. Hamme 2 May 1919), both indexed with this mother and a father *Franciscus*.
Their own acts have not been read. [[paulina_vdberghe]] stood in exactly that position until
her 1882 act was opened, which is the argument for opening theirs.

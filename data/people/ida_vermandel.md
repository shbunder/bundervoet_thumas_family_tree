Maternal grandmother of Louise Bocklandt; wife of Joannes Franciscus Van Bergen.

FRONTIER (2026-07-26). Her daughter [[marie_vanbergen]]'s 1888 Oostende death act gives
her *geboorteplaats* as **Waasmunster**, not Hamme — correcting the family prose that had
placed the whole Van Bergen family at Hamme throughout. That makes it more likely this
couple's own marriage, and this record's own dates, sit in the **Waasmunster** registers
rather than Hamme's. High on the research queue.

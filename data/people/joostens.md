DOCUMENTED (July 2026) by her own death act — Grez-Doiceau, 10 June 1857, the year this
record already held — which gives two things the tree did not have at all: her **parents**,
Guillaume Joostens and Jeanne Marie Deconninck, and her **birthplace**,
Woluwe-Saint-Lambert. Her husband Georges Thumas is named as the survivor.

Her parents are a documented frontier rather than a graft: they are named here plainly, and
adding them is the obvious next step, but neither has a record yet and nothing beyond this
act has been read about them.

ACT READ (July 2026). Her own death act — Grez-Doiceau, akte nr. 35, registered 11 June
1857 and giving the death as the **10th** — read at the archive and saved
([[joostens-1857-death-grez-doiceau]]). It fixes the day where this record had only the year,
confirms her birthplace of Woluwe-Saint-Lambert from the act itself, gives her occupation as
*ménagère*, and names her parents [[guillaume_joostens]] and [[jeanne_deconninck]], who until
a pass ago were not in this tree at all.

Her stated age of 47 puts her birth in 1809–10, which agrees with the 1810 held here.

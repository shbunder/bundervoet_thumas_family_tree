Mother of the bride in her daughter [[coekelberghs]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe
nr. 6, 24 April 1873. A *landbouwster* of Bertem, present and consenting, and like her
husband [[jb_coekelberghs]] recorded as unable to write or sign.

Named again in the same daughter's 1903 Kraainem remarriage act, which gives her death:
Bertem, 8 January 1880 — ten months before her husband, and both of them nine years dead
when their son-in-law died at Kraainem.

The act gives her no age, so this record carries no birth date.

`sup`: AGATHA act analyses, not the register images.

FRONTIER — **her forename is unstable across the acts, and it is not settled here.** The
1838 Bertem marriage, the 1853 birth, the 1873 marriage and the 1903 marriage all write
her *Anna Haesaerts* plain; two 1867 acts write *Anna Maria Haesaerts*; an 1858 birth
writes *Anna Catharina Haesaerts*. One husband, one commune, one occupation and an
unbroken run of children from 1839 to 1858 make one woman written three ways much the
likeliest reading — but it is a reading, not a fact, and the records are deliberately
**not merged** on it. Only the register images, or her own death act at Bertem in January
1880, will settle which forename she was baptised with.

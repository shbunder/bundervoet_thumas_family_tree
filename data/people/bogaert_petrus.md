Father of [[bogaert_petronilla]], and through her a direct ancestor of Renée and Léon.

He exists here on one line of the Evergem burial register of 31 August 1792, which names
his daughter's parents as Petrus Bogaert and [[taedts_maria]]. That is all the source
gives: no date, no place, no occupation, and none of those is guessed here.

A first frontier, and a narrow one: his daughter was born about 1711, so a marriage to
Maria Taedts should sit in the Evergem registers somewhere before then, and it would give
both their origins.

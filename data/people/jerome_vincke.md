Son of [[lucien_vincke]] and [[ludovica_vanald]], and [[emma_vincke]]'s brother — the
youngest known child of the couple. His birth — Diksmuide act nr. 167, 22 September 1886
— is one of seven Diksmuide rows in the vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index
(`rab-bs-geboorten`) naming both parents in full as Vincke Lucien Julianus x
Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria. His mother was 40.8 at his birth — the upper end of
childbearing but not implausible, and the 6.6-year gap since [[emma_vincke]] (1880) is
consistent with unrecorded infant losses in the couple's 1866-1870 blind window (see
[[lucien_vincke]]'s record). A province-wide sweep of the same index, filtered separately
by father's forename and by mother's surname, returns the identical seven rows — no rival
couple. Index only, not the register image, hence `sup`.

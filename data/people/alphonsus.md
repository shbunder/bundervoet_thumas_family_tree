The first Bundervoet of the line born in Oostende (1905); eldest child of Petrus Franciscus Bundervoet & Augusta Vanstechelman.

CORROBORATED (July 2026), twice over. Two unrelated heemkring collections —
Familiekunde Vlaanderen Westkust and Spaenhiers — each hold a memorial-card record
giving birth 6 January 1905 at Oostende, death 11 March 1980 at Oostende, and spouse
Elodia Bostyn. Two independent indexes, the same four facts.

Stays `sup`: both are transcriptions. The Spaenhiers index publishes as a PDF of card
listings rather than card images, so there is still nothing here anyone has read.

Second husband of [[bocklandt_louise_1877]], who had divorced [[dekeyser_edouard_1876]] on 14 November
1922. The Familiekunde Westkust memorial-card record ([[S17]]) first named him as her
partner, at her death on 15 July 1946 — she died a Blomme.

FOUND (2026-07-26), replacing the FRONTIER this record used to carry. Their own marriage
act — Stene, 1 March 1924, akte nr. 9, register image read directly
([[fs-stene-1924-marriage-blomme-bocklandt]]). He is *werkman*, living at Stene and born
there **23 January 1884**, major son of **Josephus Blomme**, *zonder beroep*, living at
Oostende and present in person, consenting to the marriage; and of **Mathilde Sophia
Meseure**, dead at Oostende **21 July 1922**. Both parents are given their own record here
for the first time: [[blomme_josephus]] and [[meseure_mathilde]].

He is a step-relation, not blood: no line of descent in this tree runs through him.

Third of the four carpenter generations. Civil records give his birth as 24 Jan 1804 (earlier trees said 1805).

CORROBORATED (July 2026) as a father, by his son's Kraainem marriage act of 20 June 1872,
which names the bridegroom's parents as Georges Thumas and Maria Catharina Joostens — this
couple. He appears again in his wife's 1857 death act ([[S9]]) as her surviving husband.

ACT READ (July 2026): named as the bridegroom's father in the Kraainem act of 20 June 1872,
with [[joostens]] as the mother — the same couple his wife's 1857 death act shows.

AGE CONFLICT (July 2026), recorded and left open. His wife's death act of 10 June 1857
names him as her surviving husband — *Georges Thumas, menuisier of Grez-Doiceau*, the trade
this record already carries, so the identification is not in doubt. But it gives his age as
**63**, which puts his birth around 1793–94. This record says **24 January 1804**, which
would have made him 53.

Ten years is too wide to be the usual slippage, and the same act gets his wife's age right —
47, agreeing with her recorded 1810. So one of the two is wrong and it is not obvious which:
the 1804 date has never been documented, and the 63 was spoken by whoever reported the death.

Nothing changed. FRONTIER: the Grez-Doiceau baptisms for 1793–94 and for 1804, and his own
1864 death act, which would state an age to compare. His father [[georgeslambert_t]] was born
in 1772, so either year makes an ordinary age at fatherhood — the parent link does not decide
it.

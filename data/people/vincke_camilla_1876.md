Daughter of [[vincke_lucien_1840]] and [[vanalderweireldt_ludovica_1845]], and [[vincke_emma_1880]]'s sister. Her
birth — Diksmuide act nr. 84, 20 April 1876 — is one of seven Diksmuide rows in the
vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index (`rab-bs-geboorten`) naming both parents in full as
Vincke Lucien Julianus x Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria (mother aged 30.4). A
province-wide sweep of the same index, filtered separately by father's forename and by
mother's surname, returns the identical seven rows — no rival couple. Index only, not the
register image, hence `sup`.

Died in infancy, Diksmuide act nr. 246, 10 December 1876, at about eight months
(`rab-bs-overlijdens`), naming both parents again. Both date columns on this act agree —
unlike her siblings [[vincke_irma_1874]] and [[vincke_jerome_1886]], whose proposed dates needed
correcting for the Date-vs-Certificate-Date conflation this index shares with
`rab-bs-geboorten` — so 10 December stands without adjustment.

Son of [[thumas_georges_1772]] and [[quinart_marie_1780]], and so a brother of [[thumas_georges_1804]].

He is known from his own Grez-Doiceau death act of 18 October 1812, which names both his
parents ([[S13]]). That act is the reason his mother's marriage is corroborated at all.

No age was read off the death act: it is indexed under the death and has not been read as
an image, so it has not settled how old he died.

BIRTH DATE ADDED (2026-07-27): [[netradyle-grez-naissances]] independently indexes his
birth as **THUMAS Georges Étienne, 26 December 1802**, father Georges Lambert, mother
Kinart Marie Catherine — the same couple held here, so this is his own birth act, not a
new person. Two identifiers agree: both parents' names, matching exactly, and the double
forename (Georges Étienne / Étienne) intact across both this act and his 1812 death act.
Dying in October 1812 at not quite ten years old is chronologically clean against this
birth. `identify.py` did not itself flag this as the same person when this record was
first written — this record then carried no birth date at all for it to compare, an
observation about the tool's reach rather than a complaint. `sup`: an index row, no
register image read.

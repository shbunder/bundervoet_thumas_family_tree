**The Gent Bundervoet who emigrated, and the reason this whole family is now documented.**
Born at Gent on 21 April 1788 and baptised the next day at Sint-Maarten; a **cooper** by
trade; married at Rotterdam on 25 November 1818; died there on 28 February 1872, in his
eighty-fourth year. He is a son of [[bundervoet_egidius]] and [[peeters_annamaria]], and
belongs to the Gent forest of objective 3, not to Renée and Léon's line.

**The birth date is the act's, and it corrects four other records.** Four Rotterdam
population-register entries give his birth as *22 May 1788*. The baptism extract says
*Vigesima secunda aprilis 1788 baptizavi … natum heri vesperi quadrante post undecimam* —
baptised 22 April, born the evening before at a quarter past eleven. The Rotterdam death
act settles the argument from the third side: 83 years and 10 months on 28 February 1872
computes to April 1788, not May. So the day and the hour stand as written here, and the
`raw` line keeps the baptism beside the birth rather than merging them.

**His marriage is why the Gent acts survive in a Dutch archive.** Dutch civil marriage
required the groom's birth certificate; because he was born abroad, the Gent extract was
physically filed with the Rotterdam act, together with a Gent notarial act of 21 October
1818 in which his father consented. Both are in
`bundervoet-1788-baptism-extract-gent-sintmaarten` and
`bundervoet-1818-notarial-consent-gent`. That route — a Fleming who married in the
Netherlands leaves a Belgian parish act inside an open Dutch archive — is the transferable
finding of this pass.

His children by [[vangerven_joanna_1790]] are recorded in the Rotterdam civil registers:
Hermanus Wilhelmus (1821), Johanna Cornelia (d. 1823), Gijsbertus (1824, d. 1826), an
unnamed child (d. 1826), Cornelia (1827, d. 1829), Gijsbertus Gerardus (1829, d. 1829) and
Dominicus Antonius Hendricus (1832, d. 1832). Only Hermanus Wilhelmus reached adulthood.
They are named here rather than given records, because this pass stopped at the generation
the read acts cover; they are the obvious next tranche under objective 3.

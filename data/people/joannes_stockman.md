Father of Livina Stockman (~1688–1744), who married Joannes Bundervoet (~1682) — so a
direct ancestor through her sons Christoffel and Pieter.

From the paulderidder ancestry chart of his grandson Pieter Bundervoet, which gives the
couple as Joannes Stockman (~1646–1696) × Guillielma "Willemijne" Dellaert (1650–1694).
Livina's record carried no parents at all before this.

FRONTIER: his own parents, and the Stockman–Dellaert marriage, in the Evergem registers
around 1670.

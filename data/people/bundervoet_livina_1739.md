Named in the Gent notarial act of 1739 as **"Livine Bundervoet filia Lieven"** — see
[[bundervoet-1739-gent-notarial-lieven-children]].

One of four children of [[bundervoet_lieven_gent]] and [[desmet_joanna_gent]] that the act
sets out side by side, each with the parentage stated rather than inferred. That is what
makes this family `doc` where a parish index could only have given four separate entries.

**The act is a transport of rents, not a life record.** It gives no birth, no age, no death
and no occupation for Livina, and none is entered. What it does establish is the parentage
and that the family held rents charged on property in the seigneury of Sint-Pieters-Aalst.

Her husband is named in the same clause: she appears *"met hem bij sijnen consente ende
authoritaetie"* — with the consent and authority of **Frans Ceuterick**, as a married woman
of 1739 had to. Geneanet's archive index carries the matching marriage, *"BUNDERVOET Livina
× CEUTERICX Franciscus (1737)"*, so the couple has a second and independent source. He has
no record here because the act gives him nothing beyond the name.

**Unconnected to Renée and Léon's line**, deliberately — nothing read joins this family to
the tree, and a shared surname is one identifier where the rules want two. Recorded under
objective 3, which expects disconnected roots.

Son of [[vaniseghem_eduardus_1880]] and [[vincke_emma_1880]], and [[vaniseghem_joannes_1903]]'s brother — found
incidentally while sweeping the birth index for rows naming Emma Celesta Vincke as
mother (Oostende act nr. 14, `rab-bs-geboorten`). Not itemised on this couple's own
records before now: he has no marriage act in this sweep, unlike his five siblings
[[vaniseghem_flavie_1898]], [[vaniseghem_eduardus_1901]], [[vaniseghem_joannes_1903]], [[vaniseghem_valentina_1909]] and [[vaniseghem_maria_1913]], so his
own birth row is the only evidence for him.

**NOT INDEPENDENTLY RE-CHECKED.** This birth date was read off the same index whose
"Date" and "Certificate Date" columns are confirmed elsewhere in this family to be
conflated (see `rab-bs-overlijdens`'s note, and the corrections on [[vincke_irma_1874]] and
[[vincke_jerome_1886]], and on Albert Joseph Vincke, not itself a record here — see
[[vincke_emma_1880]]'s frontier note). No second act or memorial card has confirmed this
date, so treat it as unverified until a second source is read. He shares
his first two forenames with an elder brother, [[vaniseghem_camillus_1905]], born six years
earlier — a second such reuse in this family is plausible (the pattern is already
documented twice over on [[vincke_theophil_1866]]'s own children).

**UPDATE (2026-07-27):** [[vaniseghem_camillus_1905]]'s own death act has since been found
(Oostende, 29 November 1905, age about seven months) — see his record. That resolves his
fate but not this one's: **no death act for Camillus Henricus has been found** in this
venue's transcribed window, so whether the reuse of "Camillus" followed a death in this
case too remains open, not asserted.

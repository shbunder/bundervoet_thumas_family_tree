Earlier husband of [[vanrenterghem_maria_1610]], who afterwards married [[rups_michael]]
— our line runs through that second marriage, via their daughter [[rups_maria_1642]].

A Vandewalle marrying into this family is not a coincidence worth ignoring: the
Vandewalles appear again a generation on, when Maria Rups married
[[vandewalle_carolus_1638]] at Oostkamp in 1662. FRONTIER — whether Cornelius and
Carolus are of the same family. Nothing here establishes it, and the surname alone
is not evidence.

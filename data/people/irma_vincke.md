Daughter of [[lucien_vincke]] and [[ludovica_vanald]], and [[emma_vincke]]'s sister. Her
birth — Diksmuide act nr. 236, 1 November 1874 — is one of seven Diksmuide rows in the
vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index (`rab-bs-geboorten`) naming both parents in full as
Vincke Lucien Julianus x Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria (mother aged 28.9). A
province-wide sweep of the same index, filtered separately by father's forename and by
mother's surname, returns the identical seven rows — no rival couple. Index only, not the
register image, hence `sup`.

Died in infancy, Diksmuide act nr. 86, 25 April 1875, at about six months
(`rab-bs-overlijdens`), naming both parents again. **CORRECTED on entry**: the death
index's "Certificate Date" column reads 26 April; the "Date" column, which is the death
itself, reads 25 April. This is the same Date-vs-Certificate-Date conflation already
documented on `rab-bs-geboorten`'s note for the births index, now confirmed to affect the
deaths index too — the note there has been updated accordingly. 25 April is recorded
here, not 26.

First husband of [[leontine_schreel]]. `doc`, because the evidence is the same document
that carries her: [[edouard_dk]]'s Oostende marriage act of 9 May 1946, read as an image,
which describes his bride as divorced from Benoni Joseph Rossel.

That is the whole of him. The act names him to establish that Leontine was free to marry,
not to say anything about him, so there are no dates, no places and no parents — and
nothing here justifies inventing any. Neither the wedding nor the divorce is dated in it,
which is why his entry below carries neither.

He is at the far edge of this tree: the first husband of the second wife of a man who
married into the family. No line of descent runs through him, and none ever will.
FRONTIER — the Schreel–Rossel marriage and divorce acts, both West-Vlaanderen civil
registration, which would date the marriage and give his parents.

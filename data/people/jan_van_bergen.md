Maternal grandfather of Louise Bocklandt — father of Maria Louisa Van Bergen (1842). Of Hamme (Oost-Vlaanderen).

CORROBORATED (2026-07-26) by two register images and one index, none of which derive from
the `tree-wete1998` tree this record had rested on alone. His wife [[ida_vermandel]]'s
**1865 Hamme death act** names him by his full name as her husband, living at Hamme. His
daughter [[paulina_vdberghe]]'s **1882 Hamme marriage act** has him present in person,
consenting, **sixty-five years old** and a *metser*. And his daughter
[[marie_vanbergen]]'s 1888 Oostende death act gives him the same trade at Hamme.

Two things follow. The trade is now recorded, from three sources agreeing. And sixty-five
in May 1882 puts his birth about 1817, which the held `1815` survives but is not confirmed
by — so the year stays as it was, and it is worth knowing it comes from a member tree and
not from an act.

`sup`, not `doc`, and deliberately: the two images prove he existed, married, worked as a
mason and was alive at Hamme in 1865 and 1882. Neither gives his birth, his death or his
parents, which are the three things this record still asserts on a tree's word. See
docs/research-log.md §52 for why that distinction is kept rather than rounded up.

FRONTIER — his death, held as 1898. The 1882 act proves he was alive that May, so the
window is 1882–1898 and the register is mapped: Hamme deaths 1894–1900 is FamilySearch
DGS **004835775**. His marriage to Ida is the higher-value act, since it would name all
four of their parents; on her birthplace it should be sought at **Zelzate** (Huwelijken
1821–1860, DGS 004670484) before Waasmunster or Hamme.

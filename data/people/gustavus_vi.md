Son of [[joannes_vi2]] and [[hermanie_janssen]] — one of eight children the vrijwilligersrab
indexes give this couple; see [[joannes_vi2]] for the full sibship and the identifier
that grounds it.

Born Oostende, birth act nr. 13, RAB ID 1486318 (Birth Date column). **No second act has
been found for him** — no marriage, no death in this venue's transcribed window — so this
date is single-sourced, unlike six of his seven siblings, and is recorded with that
caveat rather than upgraded past `sup`. The hypothesis that he is the unnamed infant
("Sn") who died at Oostende 18/11/1878, ten months after this birth, was tested and
rejected — see [[joannes_vi2]]'s record for the reasoning. See that same record for the
four open alternatives on his own fate (death after 1939; death at Stene before 1915;
death outside West-Vlaanderen; or an unmatched name-form).

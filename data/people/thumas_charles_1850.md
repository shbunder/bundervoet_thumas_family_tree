The youngest known child of [[thumas_georges_1804]] and [[joostens_marie_1810]], and the only one of them
this tree can follow to the end. He was born in 1850 and **died at Grez-Doiceau on 9 April
1851**, an infant.

He is here on two independent indexes rather than one. FamilySearch's transcription of the
Brabant civil registration names him with both parents (S25); AGATHA's Grez-Doiceau death acts
carry the same entry — *9 April 1851, Charles Eugène Thumas, father Georges*. Two venues, one
child.

The birth is a year and not a date because neither index gives more.

`sup`: no register image read. His death act would give the day of his birth and probably his
father's trade and address.

Daughter of [[vincke_lucien_1840]] and [[vanalderweireldt_ludovica_1845]], and [[vincke_emma_1880]]'s eldest known
sister. **Her parentage comes from her own 1893 MARRIAGE act, not her birth act.** The
Diksmuide birth index for 1866-1870 records only a bare father surname, no forename and no
mother at all (`rab-bs-geboorten`) — so her own birth row, Diksmuide act nr. 87 of 1869,
does not name her parents. The marriage act — Diksmuide nr. 40, 11 February 1893
([[agatha-diksmuide-1893-marriage-bocklandt-vincke]]) — names her born at Diksmuide
22 April 1869, *kantwerkster*, daughter of Lucien Julianus Vincke and Ludovica Maria
Vanalderweireldt, and married **Caesar Antonius Bocklandt** (b. Hamme 2 Jul 1867,
*koordedraaier*), son of [[bocklandt_edouard_1839]] and [[vanbergen_marielouise_1842]]. AGATHA serves this
act only as an "analyse van akte" (index transcription), not the register image, hence
`sup`.

This is the link the tree had already recorded but not acted on: the 1893 act sat on
[[bocklandt_caesar_1867]]'s own record naming the bride's parents by name only — one
identifier, short of a graft — and Florence Amelie had no record of her own to graft onto.
With her own record now written, the parents match [[vincke_lucien_1840]] x [[vanalderweireldt_ludovica_1845]]
by full name, and a province-wide sweep of the Diksmuide birth index found no rival
couple. This joins the Van Iseghem and De Keyser/Bocklandt branches of this tree (objective
3) — research-log §64.

Named as mother of the bride in her daughter [[wolf_maria_1836]]'s marriage act —
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 4, 17 June 1858 (S33), a *dagloonster*. No father is named for
the bride at all — the act records her as a *verlaten kind*, an abandoned child.

**Named, but not known to her own daughter.** The same act has [[wolf_maria_1836]] declare
under oath that her mother's last residence was unknown to her and that she had never
known her, a declaration her witnesses confirmed under oath. Both facts stand as the act
gives them: a name and a trade recorded formally, and a daughter who swears she never
knew the woman. Neither is resolved into the other here.

The act gives no age, so this record carries no birth date.

FRONTIER: whatever record named her to the 1858 clerk in the first place — a foundling
register or [[wolf_maria_1836]]'s own birth act — neither yet searched for.

A Bundervoet born at **Assenede in 1772**, from the Assenede register index. She is not in
the paulderidder list of [[bundervoet_petrus_1727]]'s eight children, and no act read this
pass names her parents.

**Her parent links are `asm`, deliberately.** What is known is one identifier and a
supporting circumstance: the surname, worth 13 bits in this corpus, and the commune, where
this is the only Bundervoet household anywhere attested — the father buried there in 1777,
[[bundervoet_coleta_1766]] born there in 1766, [[bundervoet_petronella_1754]] married there
in 1781, [[bundervoet_livina_1760]] married there in 1788 and dead there in 1831,
[[bundervoet_joanna_1761]] dead there in 1825. The rules want two independent identifiers
for a parent link and this has one, so the link is drawn as assumed: the scorer cannot see
it in either direction, and it can never come back as evidence licensing another graft.

**The chronology is the reason to hedge rather than to graft.** The mother
[[dedeckere_marie_1731]] would have been about 41 in 1772, and the paulderidder list of
children stops at 1766. That is possible and not at all impossible — but it is exactly the
gap where a niece, or the daughter of a brother not yet in the tree, would sit just as well.

**What would settle it:** her Assenede baptism act, which names both parents outright.
Under objective 2 that is a cheap, well-aimed fetch — the same register run that holds
Coleta's 1766 birth.

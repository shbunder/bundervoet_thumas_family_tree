Son of [[georgeslambert_t]] and [[quinart]], and so a brother of [[georges2_t]].

He is known from his own Grez-Doiceau death act of 18 October 1812, which names both his
parents ([[S13]]). That act is the reason his mother's marriage is corroborated at all.

No birth date: the act is indexed under the death and has not been read as an image, so his
age — which the act almost certainly states, as the other Grez-Doiceau acts of this period do
— is unread. Reading it would date his birth and show whether he died a child or a grown man.

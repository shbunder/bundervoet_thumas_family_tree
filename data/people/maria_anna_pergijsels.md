Mother of the groom in her son [[franciscus_pardon]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe
nr. 9, 1 December 1853 (S22). A *dagloonster* of Winksele, present and consenting, and
like her husband [[guilielmus_pardon]] recorded as unable to write.

The act gives her no age, so this record carries no birth date, and the death entry says
only that she was alive on the day.

The surname is a rare one and its spelling is unsettled — *Pergijsels* is what the act
writes, and Pergijssels, Pergisels and Peregijsels all occur in Brabant. That rarity is
worth more than it looks: a surname this uncommon is exactly the kind that can be searched
on its own, where Bossin and Pardon cannot.

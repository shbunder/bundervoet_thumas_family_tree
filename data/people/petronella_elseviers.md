Mother of the bridegroom in her son [[jb_coekelberghs]]'s marriage act — Bertem nr. 8,
30 October 1838, read as the register image. A *landbouwster* of Berthem, present at the
wedding and consenting to it in person, and recorded as unable to sign.

The act gives her no age, so this record carries no birth date, and does not call her
*wijlen*, so the death entry says only that she was alive on the day.

`sup` for the same reason as her husband [[henricus_coekelberghs]]: the image proves what
the act says, not that its groom is this tree's man.

FRONTIER — the surname is the useful thing about her. *Elseviers* is rare enough to be
searched on its own, where Coekelberghs and Haesaerts at Bertem are not; it also occurs as
Elsevier, Elzeviers and Elseveers in Brabant registers, and her own marriage or death act
would settle which this family used.

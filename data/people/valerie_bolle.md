Wife of [[theophil_vincke]]. Named on their marriage act — Diksmuide, 13 December 1893,
act nr. 239 (`rab-bs-huwelijken`) — and again, as his surviving spouse, on his own death
act — Oostende, 22 April 1922, act nr. 280 (`rab-bs-overlijdens`), where she is written
Bolle Valerie Maria. Index only, not the register image, hence `sup`. Her own birth,
death and parents are not in the reachable pages.

Source detail: Stef Paelinck (stefpaelinck) tree; Oostende Burgerzaken; Rijksarchief AGATHA (Oostende birth act nr. 585, 1897)

CONFIRMED Roland's grandfather (Cosette). Full first names Jérôme Josephus; born Oostende 14 Jun 1897 (birth act nr. 585, Rijksarchief AGATHA). Born before his parents married: he was LEGITIMIZED by their marriage on 4 May 1901 (marginal note 'Gewettigd 4 5 1901' on his birth act). Lived in Oostende (Blauwkasteelstraat 21; later Amsterdamstraat 38). Recorded child: Albert Dekeyser (1920–1972); Gustaaf (Roland's father) is another of their sons. His siblings (children of Édouard × Louise, per AGATHA): Gustavus Josephus (1899, also legitimized 1901), Bertha Alida Theresia (1901), Maria (1902–2002), Angela Luciana (†1903) and Cesar Mauritius (†1905) — the last two died in infancy.

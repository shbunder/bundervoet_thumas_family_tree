Second husband of [[appolonia_huyghebaert]], widow of [[josephus_janssen]]: Oostende, 27
October 1868, act nr. 106 (RAB ID 580783, microfilm 1358593). Son of Petrus Gaspar Pieren
(d. 30/04/1858 Oostende) × Catharina Dorothea Verlaecken (d. 04/08/1853 Oostende) — named
on the same act but not themselves given a record here, since nothing beyond this one
index row names them.

The act's remark notes the groom's paternal grandmother was named Anna Maria Pieren — a
detail with no further trail this pass.

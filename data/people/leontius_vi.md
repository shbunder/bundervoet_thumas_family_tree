Son of [[joannes_vi2]] and [[hermanie_janssen]] — one of eight children the vrijwilligersrab
indexes give this couple; see [[joannes_vi2]] for the full sibship and the identifier
that grounds it. He sits chronologically between siblings born August 1875 and January
1878.

Registered at Oostende, act nr. 541, RAB ID 2042291, microfilm 4794210 — both parents
named in full. **No birth date is recorded.** The row's Birth Date column is empty; only
a Certificate Date of 24/10/1876 is given, and this venue's registration lag is not known
to run same-day (a lone Certificate Date has been shown elsewhere in this family, and in
this same pass, to be a declaration date rather than the birth), so not even a
"born before 24/10/1876" bound is recorded here — only that his birth was registered on
or shortly before that date. Born Oostende (the place is on the row; the date is not).

No death and no marriage act for him has been found in this venue — see [[joannes_vi2]]'s
record for the four open alternatives that apply equally to [[gustavus_vi]].

Named as the child in a civil birth act at **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 9, 22 Mar 1859**,
03:30. **Sex is stated in the act** ("Man"), not read off the forename.

The father, [[jbbossin]], self-declares (*aangever*), aged 25, born at
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, a *daglooner* — an exact match to the 1834-01-02 birthdate his own
1858 marriage act gives (his January birthday had passed). The mother, [[maria_wolf]], is
aged 22, born at Brussel — an exact match to her own 1836-04-04 birthdate (her April
birthday not yet due).

**Both parents are marked *ongehuwd* (unmarried) on this act**, despite their marriage at
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on 17 June 1858, nine months earlier. This reads as a transcription
anomaly rather than a rival couple: every other identifier — both exact ages, both
birthplaces, both forenames and surnames — agrees precisely with [[jbbossin]] and
[[maria_wolf]], and no second couple sharing both names and both birthdates to the year
is a plausible alternative. It is recorded rather than smoothed over.

**Later seen as a witness.** As *Amandus Bossin*, 48, *landbouwer*, he stands witness at
his brother [[jphilippus_bossin57]]'s 1907 wedding — an age consistent with this birth.

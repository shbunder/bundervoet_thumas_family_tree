Sister of [[augusta]], named in her own Oostende marriage registration of 11 May 1924, which
gives her parents as Petrus Jacobus and Clemence Wagebaert — the mother's name shortened
there, as it is in several of these acts — and her husband as Alberic Luciaan Delrue.

No dates of her own yet; the act is indexed under the marriage. FRONTIER: her birth act, and
the marriage act read rather than indexed.

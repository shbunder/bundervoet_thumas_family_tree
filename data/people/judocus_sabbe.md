Father of Adriana Theresia Sabbe (1703). Married four times; farmer at Stuyvenberghe, Oostkamp. Parents Simon Sabbe (1627-1711) x Joanna Rotsaert.

FRONTIER — three of the four marriages are unrecorded here. The one held is the **third**;
isavdw gives no name, date or place for the first, second or fourth. Only Anna Van
Nieuwenhuyse is placed, so the spouse list below is not the sequence it looks like: it has
one entry of four, and any child found for him may belong to a wife this tree does not hold.

Born the same year his father died — his father Antoine died in July 1808, about four months after Carolus's birth.

HIS OWN 1828 MARRIAGE ACT RESTATES HIS PARENTS (2026-07-27) — Diksmuide, 4 June 1828, act
nr. 106, RAB ID 85449, microfilm 1166239 (VANALDEWEIRELDT Carolus Ludovicus × DUTREEUW
Eugenia Henrica, son of VANALDEWEIRELDT Antonius × WYLLIE Brigitta) — a second, independent
civil act on the same parent pair, 25 years after [[vanalderweireldt_antoine_1780]] and [[wyllie_brigitte_1779]]'s
own wedding. It also gives both parents' death dates: father 21 July 1808 (the Certificate
Date column of his death act, now understood to be a registration date rather than the day
itself — see [[vanalderweireldt_antoine_1780]]) and mother 8 November 1824 (matching this couple's held
value for [[wyllie_brigitte_1779]] to the day). SPELLING TRAP: this act writes the surname
VANALDEWEIRELDT — no R after -ALD- — so it is invisible to a `naam=Vanalderweireldt` query
and surfaced only via the mother's surname Wyllie and the bride's surname Dutreeuw.

HIS OWN BIRTH REGISTRATION CORROBORATES THE HELD DATE (2026-07-27). The Diksmuide birth
index carries a bare row "VANALDERWEIRELDT Charles, certificate 12/03/1808" with no parents
attached — part of the same 1796–1815 blind window noted on [[vanalderweireldt_antoine_1780]]'s record, so
it cannot by itself prove the link — but its certificate date sits one day after this
record's held birth of 11 March 1808, the ordinary declaration lag, and the 1828 marriage
act above independently states the same 11 March 1808. Two acts agree; the held date is
unchanged.

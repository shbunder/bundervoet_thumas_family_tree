Second husband of [[louise_bocklandt]], who had divorced [[edouard_dk]] around 1923.
The Familiekunde Westkust memorial-card record ([[S17]]) names him as her partner at
her death on 15 July 1946 — she died a Blomme. The card names him and nothing more.

No marriage date, so none is recorded. FRONTIER — the Bocklandt–Blomme marriage act.
It is post-1923 Oostende, so it sits at the Stadsarchief rather than in AGATHA.

He is a step-relation, not blood: no line of descent here runs through him.

Son of [[lucien_vincke]] and [[ludovica_vanald]], and [[emma_vincke]]'s brother. His birth
— Diksmuide act nr. 169, 23 July 1877 — is one of seven Diksmuide rows in the
vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index (`rab-bs-geboorten`) naming both parents in full as
Vincke Lucien Julianus x Vanalderweireldt Ludovica Maria (mother aged 31.7). A
province-wide sweep of the same index, filtered separately by father's forename and by
mother's surname, returns the identical seven rows — no rival couple. Index only, not the
register image, hence `sup`.

Died at Diksmuide, act nr. 24, 4 April 1902, aged 24 (`rab-bs-overlijdens`; mother
written Vanalderweireldt Louise Maria there). Both date columns on this act agree —
unlike his siblings [[irma_vincke]] and [[jerome_vincke]], whose proposed dates needed
correcting for the Date-vs-Certificate-Date conflation this index shares with
`rab-bs-geboorten` — so 4 April stands without adjustment.

Born in the French coal-mining town of Lens, where his Oostende family had emigrated for work; the family later returned to Belgium. Died six days short of his 66th birthday.

CORROBORATED (July 2026) by a Familiekunde Vlaanderen Westkust memorial-card record:
born **Lens** 11 May 1903, died Oostende 5 May 1969, partner Adrienne Devriendt. The
birthplace is the striking part — an Oostende family with a son born at Lens — and the
card gives it independently of the trees, which is the strongest single reason to believe
this is the same man. This record notes Lens as being in France; the card says only
"Lens", so which Lens is not settled here.

MARRIAGE DATED (2026-07-27): his own marriage act — Stene nr. 6, 24 March 1928
(vrijwilligersrab Huwelijken index, `rab-bs-huwelijken`) — names his parents in full as
Eduardus Van Iseghem × Emma Celesta Vincke, matching this tree's existing
[[eduardus_vi]] × [[emma_vincke]] exactly, and the bride's parents as Devriendt Eugenius
Alphonsius × Schalandryn Octavia Maria, matching [[eugenius_dv]] × [[octavia_schal]]
exactly. Both couples were already held in this tree as his and Adrienne's parents; this
act corroborates an already-fully-linked marriage and supplies the date and place that
were previously missing from both records, not a new tree collapse.

FRONTIER — an unexplained gap of over four years. His held birth, 11 May 1903 at Lens,
precedes his parents' held marriage, 28 September 1907, by more than four years. Either
an earlier, unregistered union or a later legitimation would explain it — this tree
already carries the second pattern elsewhere (see [[edouard_dk]]'s marriage to Louise
Bocklandt, which legitimized two children already born). Left open; neither date is
altered here.

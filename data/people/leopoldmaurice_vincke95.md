birth-index entries surface (Diksmuide act nr. 11, `rab-bs-geboorten`), contingent on
their parent link being accepted. Died in infancy, aged about eleven weeks (Diksmuide act
nr. 44, `rab-bs-overlijdens`). Index only, not the register image, hence `sup`.

years later and also died in infancy — two distinct children, not a transcription

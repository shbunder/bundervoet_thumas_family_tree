Named in her daughter [[cuypers_me]]'s marriage entry — Zaventem, November 1844 (S31), and
nothing beyond the name.

*de Hose* is an unusual spelling and an unusual name for this corner of Brabant — de Hoze,
Dehose and d'Hooghe are all plausible variants of the same family — which makes her worth
searching on her own, and makes the spelling worth keeping exactly as the record has it.

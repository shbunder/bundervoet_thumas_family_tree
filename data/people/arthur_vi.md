Son of [[joannes_vi2]] and [[hermanie_janssen]] — one of eight children the vrijwilligersrab
indexes give this couple; see [[joannes_vi2]] for the full sibship and the identifier that
grounds it (a province-wide sweep found nine different men recorded identically as
"Joannes Vaniseghem", so it is the parent PAIR, not the father's name alone, that carries
this record).

Born Oostende, birth act nr. 140, RAB ID 1486295 (Birth Date column, not Certificate
Date). His own first marriage act — Oostende, 29 June 1901, act nr. 147, RAB ID 666102 —
independently restates his birth as 17/03/1874 Oostende, an exact match and a second
identifier beyond the parent pair; he is recorded there as *boodschapper*, *ongehuwd*.
Married Elodia Joanna Schellynck (b. 20/08/1876 Oostende, d/o Petrus Constantinus
Schellynck x Maria Coleta Vigne — not a record here).

**A SECOND MARRIAGE, not a ninth child.** Oostende, 22 February 1936, act nr. 40, RAB ID
711795, names a groom "VANISEGHEM Arthur Leopoldus" marrying Judith Maria Debbaut; its
Previous Partners table names Schellynck Elodia Joanna, the 1901 bride, which is what
identifies this as the same man remarrying rather than a distinct sibling. This venue's
Previous Partners table elsewhere in this family names only spouses who had already died
(e.g. [[josephus_janssen]]'s remarried widow), consistent with Elodia having died before
1936, though no death act for her was found in this pass — not asserted as fact, only as
the ordinary reading of the table. Index rows throughout, no register image read, so
confidence stays `sup`.

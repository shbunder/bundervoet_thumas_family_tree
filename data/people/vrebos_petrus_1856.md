Not previously in the tree. He married [[bossin_ludovica_1853]] — [[bossin_antonia_1849]]'s sister,
[[bossin_guilielmus_1824]] and [[peremans_joanna_1824]]'s daughter — at **Kraainem on 30 May 1901**, her
remarriage after the death of her first husband, [[swaelens_joannes_1858]] (Kraainem, 7 October 1898).
Born **Kraainem, 30 May 1856**, a *pleisteraar*, and himself a widower at the marriage:
his first wife, **Elisabeth Theresia Geuffens**, had died at Kraainem on 30 April 1900,
barely thirteen months before this act — named in it only as his deceased "vorige
partner", with no birth date, age or parents given, so no record is made for her beyond
this note.

His parents are named in the act: **Henricus Franciscus Vrebos**, dead at Kraainem on 18
December 1871, and **Josina Schoolmeesters**, landbouwster of Kraainem — see [[vrebos_henricus]]
and [[schoolmeesters_josina]].

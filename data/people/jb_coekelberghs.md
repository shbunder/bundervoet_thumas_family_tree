Father of the bride in his daughter [[coekelberghs]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe
nr. 6, 24 April 1873. A *landbouwer* of Bertem, present at the wedding and consenting to
it, and recorded as unable to write or sign, *ongeletterd zijnde*.

Named a second time, thirty years later, in the same daughter's remarriage act — Kraainem
nr. 16, 6 November 1903 — which is where his death comes from: Bertem, 19 November 1880.
Two acts three decades apart naming the same man as her father is what carries this link;
the 1873 act alone would be a surname and a commune.

`sup` rather than `doc`: both are AGATHA act analyses — the Rijksarchief's own Demogen
transcriptions — and neither act has a scan on AGATHA or Open Archives.

FRONTIER — his own marriage, which the registry already holds as
`bertem-1838-marriage-coeckelberghs-haesaerts`: Bertem nr. 8, 30 October 1838, giving him
as born at Bertem 29 July 1809, a *slagter*, son of *Henricus Coeckelberghs* and
*Petronella Elseviers*. It is not grafted here for two reasons. The act is unread beyond
its transcription, and the trade moves — *slagter* in 1838, 1853 and 1867, *landbouwer* in
1858, 1861 and 1873 — while the wife's forename is written three ways across the same
span (see [[anna_haesaerts]]). One man recorded loosely over forty-five years is much the
likeliest reading, but *Joannes Baptista Coekelberghs* is a common enough name in Brabant
that several distinct men carry it between 1793 and 1921, so the birth and the generation
above wait for the register image. That image exists and is reachable:
`https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:9392-HGZ1-T?i=217&cc=1482191&cat=140936`.

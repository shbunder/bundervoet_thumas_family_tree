Mother of the bride in her daughter [[peremans]]'s marriage act — Zaventem, act nr. 1,
24 February 1846 (S19), read as an image. She was dead by then, at Zaventem on 19 December
1843, six years after her husband [[egidius_peremans]] and barely two years before her
daughter married.

The act gives her no age and no occupation, so this record has neither.

The surname is written *Ver Elst* on the act, in two words. Verelst and Van Elst are the
usual Brabant forms, and her own acts will show which the family wrote.

FRONTIER — her 1843 Zaventem death act, which should give her age and her parents. Note
that her daughter declared to the 1846 act, with her witnesses, that she had never known
her grandparents nor where they died: this generation was not reachable through the
family's own memory in 1846, and it will have to come from the registers.

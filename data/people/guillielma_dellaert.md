Mother of Livina Stockman (~1688–1744) and so a direct ancestor. Recorded in the
paulderidder tree with both a Latin form and a vernacular one — Guillielma, called
Willemijne — in the same style as Joanna "Janneken" van Hecke and Joanna "Janneken"
Verbrugghe elsewhere on this line.

She died in 1694, when Livina would have been about six.

FRONTIER: her parents, and the Stockman–Dellaert marriage.

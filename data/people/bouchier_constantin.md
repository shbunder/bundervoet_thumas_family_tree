Husband of [[bundervoet_coleta_1766]], named in the one line of remark attached to the
estate-duty declaration filed in her name at Assenede on 27 December 1807: *"Gehuwd met
Constantin Stanislas Bouchier."*

The marriage has no date and no place here because the memorie gives neither. It states
only that they were married, which is why the `spouses` entry carries no `married` field —
the marriage is the fact, the date is not known.

**A French name in a Flemish village, and worth noticing.** Bouchier is not a local
surname, and a three-part French given name is unusual at Assenede in the 1790s. The
Assenede marriage register is the place to look; the act would date the marriage and say
where he came from.

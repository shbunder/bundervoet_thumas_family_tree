Named as father of the groom in his son [[jbbossin]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe
nr. 4, 17 June 1858 (S33). The act gives him no age, no residence and no trade, and no
remark of him being present — where his wife [[maria_desmedt]] is recorded as present and
consenting on the same act. Nothing is inferred from that silence: it may mean he was
already dead by 1858, or simply that the transcription omitted the field.

This is the first generation this tree has for [[jbbossin]], previously top-of-branch on
his own record.

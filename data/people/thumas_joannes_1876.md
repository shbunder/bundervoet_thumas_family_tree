Son of Georges Carolus Josephus and Antoina Bossin. A factory worker who also served as a schepen (municipal alderman) in Kraainem. Married Joanna Vandenbemden in Kraainem on 3 Feb 1902.

DOCUMENTED (July 2026) by the Kraainem marriage act of 3 February 1902 (akte nr. 2, [[S5]]), where he is the bridegroom. It gives his birth at
Kraainem on 26 September 1876 and his trade as *fabriekwerker*, both as held here, and
names his parents outright — Georgius Carolus Josephus Thumas and Antonia Bossin. He was
living at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe when he married. The alderman's office in his occupation
field came from elsewhere and is not in this act.

Daughter of [[vaniseghem_eduardus_1880]] and [[vincke_emma_1880]], and [[vaniseghem_joannes_1903]]'s sister. Her own
marriage act — Stene nr. 21, 22 May 1920 (vrijwilligersrab Huwelijken index,
`rab-bs-huwelijken`) — names her parents in full as Eduardus Van Iseghem x Emma Celesta
Vincke, matching this couple exactly; one of five such acts spanning 1920-1935 that all
converge on the identical parent-name pair. That reading was index only, not the register
image; her parentage from it alone stayed `sup`.

FRONTIER — her husband. The same index names him **Desire Camille Gunst**, but he has no
record of his own here: this pass is naming [[vaniseghem_eduardus_1880]] and [[vincke_emma_1880]]'s children,
not their in-laws, so his own record is left for objective 2's next pass rather than
entered now.

RESOLVED (2026-07-27) — BIRTH DATE AND PLACE SUPPLIED, RECORD UPGRADED `sup` → `doc`. The
open frontier below — a birth nine years before her parents' 1907 marriage — is answered
directly by the marriage act's own legitimation clause, read as a register image
([[fs-oostende-1907-marriage-vaniseghem-vincke]],
`data/artifacts/vaniseghem-vincke-1907-marriage-oostende.md`): "voor hunne wettige
dochter: Flavie-Eulalie Vincke, geboren te Dixmude den zes en twintigsten Maart achttien
honderd acht en negentig" — their legitimate daughter, born Diksmuide 26 March 1898. The
tie to the birth-index row below is by forename pair, day, month and year and commune —
**not** by act reference, since the 1907 act cites no birth act number for her. This is a
legitimation of a child already born, the same mechanism as [[dekeyser_edouard_1876]]'s marriage
elsewhere in this tree, not an unrecorded earlier union.

The birth-index sweep that first found her (incidental to an unrelated 1866-1870
blind-window pass): "VINCKE Flavie Eulalie", b. Diksmuide 26 March 1898 act nr. 21, father
NN NN, mother VINCKE Emma Celesta — registered under her mother's surname, exactly as the
1907 act itself also enters her (her two younger brothers are already entered there as
Vanyseghem/Van Yseghem). A second such row, "VINCKE Albert Joseph" (b. Diksmuide 9
December 1899 act nr. 84, d. Diksmuide 26 March 1900), is **not** named in the
legitimation clause — see [[vincke_emma_1880]]'s record for why that stays unproven and
unlinked.

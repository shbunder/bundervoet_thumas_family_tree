Father of four Bundervoets named in one notarial act at Gent in 1739 — see
[[bundervoet-1739-gent-notarial-lieven-children]]. **The act names him only as their
father**, four times over: *filia Lieven*, *filius Lieven voornoemt*, *dochter van
voorseyden Lieven*, *filius Lieven*. It gives him no date, no age and no trade, and none is
entered here.

`doc` because the register image was read, not because much is known: what is documented is
the parentage, and that is all.

His wife is [[desmet_joanna_gent]], named in the act as *"Joanne de Smet syne
huijsvrauwe"*. His son [[bundervoet_paschier_1739]] is recorded there as living in the
seigneury of **Sint-Pieters-Aalst**, which is the only place the family is attached to —
and, as the artifact note sets out, is *not* Gent's Sint-Pieters parish.

**No link is drawn to any other Bundervoet in this tree.** He belongs to the forest under
objective 3 and nothing read connects him to Renée and Léon's line. The frontier that would:
a marriage of Lieven Bundervoet and Joanna de Smet, which the Aalst-area registers should
hold, and which would give both their parents.

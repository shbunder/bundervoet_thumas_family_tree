Wife of [[bundervoet_livinus_1788]], married at Rotterdam on 25 November 1818.

Her own baptism extract was filed with the marriage and read this pass: *Uit het doop
Register R.C. Kerk … is geëxtraheerd, dat op den vyftienden September des jaars zeventien
honderd negentig is gedoopt Joanna Christina, dochter van Hermanus van Gerven en van
Joanna Daalsen*. The extract is dated at Rotterdam 23 November 1818, two days before the
wedding.

**The date is a baptism, not a birth.** The extract gives the day she was christened and
says nothing about when she was born, so the `raw` line keeps them apart. Her parents,
Hermanus van Gerven and Joanna Daalsen, are named in the extract and again in the marriage
act; they have no records here because this pass stopped at the Bundervoet side of the
family.

She died at Rotterdam on 26 May 1858, fourteen years before her husband.

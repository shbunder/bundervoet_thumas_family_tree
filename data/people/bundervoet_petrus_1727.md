Christoffel (1709–1786) through whom our line descends. He existed in this tree only as
the string "Pieter (1727)" inside his father's note; every person referenced anywhere is
supposed to have their own record, and now he does.

The death is recorded as a burial, not a death: paulderidder gives "deceased, aged about
50" with a burial at Assenede on 13 December 1777, and no date of death. The year is
safe, the day is the burial's, and the two are kept apart rather than collapsed into a

sign of this branch moving off the home parish.

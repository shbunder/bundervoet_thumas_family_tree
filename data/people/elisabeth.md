First wife of Livinus Bundervoet and mother of Segerius Bundervoet (~1615–1669), so a
direct ancestor. Her given name is all that survives: the paulderidder tree records her
as "Elisabeth NN", with no surname, no dates and no parents.

SURNAME NOT KNOWN — and specifically not "Hovelynck". Livinus's record here used to
call her Elisabeth Hovelynck; the paulderidder tree, which that claim was attributed
to, gives no surname at all. The surname has been removed rather than carried forward,
because a name that no reachable source states is exactly the kind of detail that
hardens into fact by being repeated. If another tree does give a surname, it can be
restored with that tree cited.

FRONTIER: an Evergem marriage of Livinus Bundervoet in the 1610s would name her in
full, and would also settle which of his two marriages came first.

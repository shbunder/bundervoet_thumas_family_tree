Father of Louise Marie Bocklandt (1877, Hamme O-Vl). Born 1839 at Hamme (Oost-Vlaanderen);
married Maria Louisa Van Bergen (1842-1888). Died Oostende 4 July 1907.

CITATION REPAIRED (2026-07-26), replacing the §52 downgrade. Two acts now stand behind this
record. His own death act — Oostende nr. 417, 4 July 1907
([[agatha-oostende-1907-death-eduardus-bocklandt]]) — names his parents **Antonius
Dominicus Bocklandt** and **Isabella Livina Ichau** (both already deceased, place not
stated), independently of the wete1998 tree that first supplied them, and gives his death
date and Hamme birthplace. The act spells her **Isabella Livina**, against this record's
**Isabella Livia** — a single-letter variant, and with no rival Isabella Ichau anywhere in
the record to collide with, so it is read as the same woman; the same note is on
[[ichau_isabella_1803]]'s own record. The Hamme death act of his young son Ludovicus, 9 June
1880 — register image read directly ([[fs-hamme-1880-death-ludovicus-bocklandt]]) — puts
him at Hamme with Maria Louisa Van Bergen, aged 40 (consistent with the held birth of
1839), trade *zeeldraaier* (ropemaker).

CONFIDENCE STAYS `sup`, not `doc`. The parent-link fact — his own parentage — rests on the
1907 death act, and AGATHA serves that act only as an "analyse van akte" (a volunteer index
transcription); the image itself sits behind an AGATHA login and was not opened. The 1880
register image WAS read, but it does not name his own parents, so it cannot carry that
particular fact to `doc` on its own.

Six more of his and Maria Louisa's children now have their own records, each sourced to an
act naming him by name as father: [[bocklandt_paulina_1870]] (m. Oostende 1888),
[[bocklandt_maria_1874]] (m. Oostende 1892), [[bocklandt_maria_1872]] (m. Oostende 1893),
[[bocklandt_caesar_1867]] (m. Diksmuide 1893), [[bocklandt_philemondus_1880]] (m. Oostende 1900) and
[[bocklandt_alphonsus]] (d. Oostende 1892), alongside [[bocklandt_ludovicus_1875]] (d. Hamme
1880) and [[bocklandt_louise_1877]] herself. Across the four marriage acts of 1888-1900 he is
consistently *touwslagersknecht* (a ropemaker's assistant) at Oostende — the same trade as
*zeeldraaier* at Hamme in 1880, under a different word for it — and his stated age rises
from 50 (1888) to 62 (1900), which fits a birth of 1838-1839 throughout.

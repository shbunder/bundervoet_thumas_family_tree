Source detail: Geneanet; the 1902 Kraainem marriage act of her daughter Joanna

Joanna Vandenbemden's mother; married Henricus Vandenbemden in 1873.

DOCUMENTED (July 2026) as the bride's mother in the Kraainem marriage act of 3 February 1902 (akte nr. 2, [[S5]]): living at Kraainem,
*herbergierster*, the occupation already held here. The act makes her the mother of
[[vandenbemden]] and the wife of [[hendrik_vdb]].

GRAFTED (2026-07-26): her own marriage act — **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 6, 24 April 1873** —
names her parents as [[jb_coekelberghs]], *landbouwer* of Bertem, and [[anna_haesaerts]],
*landbouwster* of Bertem, both present and both consenting. It also gives her birth to the
day, **Bertem, 22 November 1848**, where this record held only the year; her trade at
twenty-four, *dienstmeid*; and the commune the marriage was passed in, which neither
spouse's record carried.

The link does not rest on her name. Two identifiers already in this tree agree with the
act exactly: the marriage date 24 April 1873, and her husband's birth — Everberg,
19 August 1849, to the day. A third bridge pins her across thirty years: her **1903
Kraainem remarriage act** names the same parents again, and names her late first husband
as *Henricus August Vandenbemden, overleden Kraainem 12/08/1889* — the same death date
**and** the same commune that the 1902 act established when it corrected this tree's
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe to Kraainem. Three acts, thirty years apart, triangulating.

That 1903 act also gives her a second marriage this tree did not have, to the widower
[[henricus_vanesch]], and her parents' deaths: Bertem, 8 January and 19 November 1880.

DOWNGRADED (2026-07-26) from `doc` to `sup`. Nothing about her was refuted — the evidence
grew. The demotion is about what the evidence *is*: every act behind this record, [[S5]]
included, is an AGATHA act analysis, the Rijksarchief's own Demogen transcription, and not
one of them has a scan on AGATHA or Open Archives. `doc` means an act or its image was
read. None was. See docs/research-log.md §51 — this record is one of about twenty carrying
the same overstatement, and the sweep is a pass of its own.

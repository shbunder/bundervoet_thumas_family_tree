Earlier husband of [[maria_vanrenterghem]], who afterwards married [[michael_rups]]
— our line runs through that second marriage, via their daughter [[maria_rups]].

A Vandewalle marrying into this family is not a coincidence worth ignoring: the
Vandewalles appear again a generation on, when Maria Rups married
[[carolus_vandewalle]] at Oostkamp in 1662. FRONTIER — whether Cornelius and
Carolus are of the same family. Nothing here establishes it, and the surname alone
is not evidence.

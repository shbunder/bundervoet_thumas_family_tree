Named as father of the groom in his son [[swaelens]]'s marriage act — Kraainem, 25
February 1884 — resident **Braine-l'Alleud**, *hovenier*. The act gives him no age and no
dates, so this record has none.

**UPGRADED TO `doc` (2026-07-27) — no longer single-source.** The Kraainem act's own
register image was read: he was **present in person and consenting**, and the act states
he **could not sign, being illiterate** ("verklaart niet te kunnen teekenen, ongeletterd
zijnde"), a detail only the image carries. A second, independent register corroborates
him before the act existed at all — the **Alsemberg marriage banns**, nrs. 7 and 8 (10 and
17 February 1884), a different commune, a different register series and a different
clerk, name the same pair: *Egidius Swaelens, hovenier, wonende te Braken-Eigen
[Eigenbrakel = Braine-l'Alleud]*. Two independent identifiers — occupation (*hovenier*)
and residence (Braine-l'Alleud) — now agree across two unconnected registers, which is
what lifts this record off the single-act standard it was accepted at (see
[[cornelius_bossin]], [[joannes_bossin51]] and [[bossin]] (Antonia) for that standard).

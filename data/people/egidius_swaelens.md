February 1884 — resident **Braine-l'Alleud**, *hovenier*. The act gives him no age and no
dates, so this record has none.

that is not a cross-family one: it is already inside this same family, on
[[cornelius_bossin]], [[joannes_bossin51]] and [[bossin]] (Antonia), each of whom entered
this tree the same way — a civil act's purpose-built parentage statement, one act, no
second source, and no register image. That is the standard this record meets too.

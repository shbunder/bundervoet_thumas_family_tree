Second wife of [[livinus_bundervoet]], and so a step-mother to [[segerius_bundervoet]] rather
than an ancestor of this tree.

WORTH NOTING, NOT CLAIMED: Livinus's son Segerius married [[joanna_vanhecke]] (~1610), and
Livinus himself married this Catharina van Hecke (~1620) — two van Hecke women marrying father
and son. In a parish the size of Evergem that suggests one van Hecke family and possibly two
sisters, but nothing read so far states any relationship between them, so none is recorded.

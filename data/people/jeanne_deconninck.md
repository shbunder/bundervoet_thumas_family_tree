Mother of Marie Catharina Joostens (1810–1857) and so a direct ancestor.

Named alongside her husband in her daughter's Grez-Doiceau death act of 10 June 1857, which
is the only source read for her. It gives her no dates and no place; none is invented here.

The surname is Flemish (De Coninck / Deconinck are the commoner spellings) in a
Walloon-Brabant setting, which is worth noting for the search: the family may have come to
Woluwe-Saint-Lambert or Grez-Doiceau from Flanders, and the name may be spelled either way
in the registers.

FRONTIER: the Joostens–Deconninck marriage, and her own parents.

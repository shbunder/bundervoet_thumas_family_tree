Daughter of Carolus Vanalderweireldt & Eugenia Dutreeuw; mother of Emma Celesta Vincke
(1880–1974), and of at least seven more children.

SEVEN MORE CHILDREN GRAFTED (research-log §64), from a province-wide sweep of the
vrijwilligersrab Geboorten index (`rab-bs-geboorten`) for Diksmuide, each act naming both
parents in full: [[charles_vincke]] (b. 1871), [[romanie_vincke]] (b. 1873),
[[irma_vincke]] (b. 1874), [[camilla_vincke]] (b. 1876), [[eugene_vincke]] (b. 1877),
[[emma_vincke]] (b. 1880, already held) and [[jerome_vincke]] (b. 1886, aged 40.8 at his
birth — the upper end of childbearing but not implausible). See [[lucien_vincke]]'s record
for the act numbers and the two independent filters that found no rival couple.

An EIGHTH CHILD, [[florence_vincke]] (b. Diksmuide 1869), is grafted from her own 1893
marriage act rather than a birth act — see [[lucien_vincke]]'s record for why. This joins
the couple's line to the De Keyser/Bocklandt branch of this tree (objective 3).

THE BLIND WINDOW BROKEN (research-log §67, 2026-07-27). FOUR MORE CHILDREN GRAFTED, via
their own later marriage and death acts rather than a birth act: [[theophil_vincke]]
(b. 1866, m. Valerie Bolle 1893, d. Oostende 1922), [[bellarmin_vincke]] (b. 1867, d.
Diksmuide 1872, an infant), [[karolus_vincke]] (b. 1870, d. Diksmuide 1871, an infant,
whose name was reused for [[charles_vincke]] four months later) and [[alfons_vincke]]
(b. 1865, just outside this window, m. Eugenia Vandecappelle 1888). TWELVE
GRANDCHILDREN follow, contingent on those two marriages — six through Theophil ×
Valerie Bolle, six through Alfons × Eugenia Vandecappelle — see [[lucien_vincke]]'s record
for each one and the act numbers.

TWO EXCLUDED: "Medard Edmond Vincke" (1866) is a different Vincke woman's child, settled
by his own death act; "Elodie Vincke" (1870) belongs to a separate Diksmuide Vincke line
(parents Richard Aloïs Vincke × Eugenie Dekeyrel per her own 1894 marriage act) and is not
the same person as this couple's own [[romanie_vincke]] ("Romanie Elodie"). FIVE STILL
NOT PROVEN — Emil Fidèle, Gustave Camil, Florida Juliana, Arthur Carolus, Cyrille Camil —

was searched and came back negative, and for the act-spacing argument used only to

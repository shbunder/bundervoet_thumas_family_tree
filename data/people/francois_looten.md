Second husband of [[brigitte_wyllie]], widow of [[antoine_vanald]]. Married her at
Diksmuide on 25 June 1818, act nr. 82, RAB ID 85255, microfilm 1166237
(`data/artifacts/looten-wyllie-1818-marriage-diksmuide.md`) — a volunteer INDEX/Detail
transcription, `sup`, never `doc`. He was 33, a *timmerman*, born just across the French
border at Rexpoede; she was 38, a widow. **This act is what identifies her as this record**,
not the name alone — see [[brigitte_wyllie]] for the "Previous Partners" row that does the
work.

His own parents are named on the same act but not linked here — a single index row, the
same standard applied throughout this pass: Jeremie Jacques Looten, died 20 August 1804 (2
Fructidor XII, the Republican-calendar conversion checked by computation) at Stavele, ×
Anne Therese Deprez, died 12 April 1792 at Rexpoede.

At least four children with [[brigitte_wyllie]], found in the same sweep that turned up
this marriage, each naming both parents in full as Looten Franciscus Josephus × Wyllie
Brigitta Josephina: [[catharina_looten]] (died Diksmuide 1823), [[renatus_looten]] (died
Diksmuide 1825, weeks after his mother), [[ludovica_looten]] (married 1842) and
[[maria_looten]] (married 1843). No individual act number was read for any of the four — the
detail comes from the same summary already gathered while reading the 1818 act — so the
sibship should be treated as complete only as far as that sweep reached, not as a closed
list.

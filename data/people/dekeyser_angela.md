Daughter of [[dekeyser_edouard_1876]] and [[bocklandt_louise_1877]] — named until now only inside her
father's own prose, and inside a §52 note on her mother's record. Her death act — Oostende
nr. 828, 20 October 1903 ([[agatha-oostende-1903-death-angela-dekeyser]]) — names Eduardus
Dekeyser, 26, *werkman*, as her father and Louisa Maria Bocklandt, *zonder beroep*, as her
mother; born and died at Oostende. AGATHA serves this act only as an "analyse van akte"
(index transcription), not the register image, hence `sup`. No age or birth date is given
in the act, and none is otherwise held.

One of the two witnesses declaring her death, Fredericus Debuf, 33, *matroos bij de
staatsmarine*, is very likely her uncle by marriage — [[bocklandt_maria_1874]]'s husband
Fredericus Desiderius Debuf.

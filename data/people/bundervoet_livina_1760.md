One of the eight children of [[bundervoet_petrus_1727]] and [[dedeckere_marie_1731]]. Their father was
the younger brother of [[bundervoet_christoffel_1709]], through whom this tree's line runs, so these eight are
first cousins of [[bundervoet_philippus_1756]]'s generation — blood relatives of Renée and Léon under
objective 2.

Recorded first from the paulderidder tree's list of the couple's children. **The Assenede
registers now stand behind the death year and add a husband**: her marriage to
[[vervaeck_antonius]] in **1788**, and her death at Assenede in **1831**.

Two identifiers agree — the death year 1831, which the held record already carried from a
different source, and Assenede, where this household is otherwise attested (her father
buried there in 1777, her sister [[bundervoet_coleta_1766]] born there in 1766).

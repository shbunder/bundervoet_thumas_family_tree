A second sibling for [[peremans]]. His marriage banns were published at **Zaventem on 21 April
1844** (S30), naming his parents as [[egidius_peremans]] and *Theresia Verelst* — the short
form of [[joanna_verelst]]'s name, which is how half this family's acts write her.

The timing is worth noticing. His father had been dead seven years and his mother **four
months** — she died on 19 December 1843. He married in the spring after burying her, and his
sister [[peremans]] married in the February after that, at twenty-one and orphaned on both
sides.

Anchored the same way as [[mjosephina_peremans]]: the record itself gives no place, and the
commune comes from the title of the volume the page sits in. Zaventem is where both his parents
died and where two of his sisters married.

**His wife is not recorded, deliberately.** The index lists three more people in the same
document — *Lambertus van der Pelen*, *Maria Catharina van der Vaeren* and *Maria Anna van der
Vaeren* — and states the role of none of them. Maria Anna is the likeliest bride and the other
two the likeliest parents, but the surnames do not line up in the obvious way, and guessing
which of three unlabelled people someone married is exactly the kind of invention this project
forbids.

FRONTIER: the marriage act itself, which would name the bride and both sets of parents; and his
own birth act at Zaventem, probably in the 1810s or 1820s.

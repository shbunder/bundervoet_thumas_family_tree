Father of the bridegroom in his son [[jb_coekelberghs]]'s marriage act — Bertem nr. 8,
30 October 1838, **read as the register image**. A *landbouwer* of Berthem, and the act
puts him there in person: *hier present en Consenteerende*. He is not called *wijlen*, so
he was alive that day, and he is given no age, so this record carries no birth date.

Like everyone else named in the act except the witnesses — the bridegroom, the bride and
all four parents — he declared he could not sign.

**Why this record is `sup` although the image was read.** The image is `doc`-quality
evidence for what the 1838 act *says*. It is not evidence that the groom it names is this
tree's [[jb_coekelberghs]]. That bridge runs through the 1873 and 1903 acts, which are
AGATHA transcriptions with no scan, so the chain is only as strong as its weakest link.
See docs/research-log.md §52 for why that distinction is now enforced, and §53 for this
pass.

FRONTIER — his own marriage to [[petronella_elseviers]], which the Bertem registers should
hold for roughly 1800–1809 and which would name his parents. The Bertem film that would
carry it is **DGS 004471767**, *Geboorten 1796–1870, Huwelijken 1796–1832, Overlijdens
1796–1842* — the three record types are bound as separate blocks rather than interleaved,
and there is no index over the film, so it is a browse rather than a lookup.

A fifth sibling for [[peremans]]. He married **Joanna Catharina Van Gindertaelen** at
**Zaventem on 21 August 1848** (S34), banns published on 6 and 13 August. He was **28**,
an *arbeider*, and the act states he could not write and had already completed his
militia obligations; she was **23**, an *arbeidster*, also unable to write. Twenty-eight
in August 1848 gives the *about 1820* birth year here — an age converted to a year and
marked approximate, not a date the act itself gives.

**Two independent identifiers anchor the parent link**, not one. First: the act names his
mother as *Joanna Theresia Peremans* — read [[joanna_verelst]] — already dead, at
**Zaventem, 19 December 1843**, an exact day-level match to the death date this tree
already holds for her, drawn from a *separately transcribed* Demogen batch (S19, his
sister's 1846 marriage act, a different register entirely). Second: his father is named
as *Egidius Peremans*, matching [[egidius_peremans]] by name and commune. A day-level
death date repeated identically across two independent transcriptions of two different
registers is not the kind of thing two unrelated Egidius Peremans × Joanna Theresia
Verelst couples would coincidentally share.

**This clears a higher bar than the three siblings already in the tree.**
[[cornelius_peremans]], [[jathanasius_peremans]] and [[mjosephina_peremans]] were each
grafted on the parent-name pair alone, with the commune supplied only by the title of the
volume the indexed page sat in, and with no exact date anywhere in the record. This act
gives an exact day-level date on one of the two parents, independently corroborating a
fact already held rather than merely repeating an unanchored name.

Her parents are named directly in the act, with their roles labelled, so there is no
identity question to resolve: **Joannes Franciscus Van Gindertaelen** (present and
consenting) × **Anna Catharina Huenaerts** (present and consenting), both *arbeider(ster)*
of Zaventem — see [[vangindertaelen_jf]] and [[huenaerts_ac]].

**Four witnesses stood at the wedding.** Two are already-held siblings and add little:
[[cornelius_peremans]], 33 (implying a birth around 1814–1815, recorded on his own record
as a witness-line lead, not a date), and [[jathanasius_peremans]], 26 (consistent with
his already-held ~1822, adding no precision). A third, **Petrus Van Der Varen**, 35, is a
plausible but unconfirmed match for [[mjosephina_peremans]]'s husband
[[vandervarent_p]] — the surname is spelled differently here, the act states no relation,
and the reasoning is recorded on his own record as a frontier, not a link. The fourth,
**Franciscus Engels**, 38, *veldwachter* (rural constable), is not a family lead at all: he
is a recurring professional witness, not kin — see the note on that below.

**A conflict, not smoothed over.** This act gives Egidius Peremans's own death as
**1836-03-06** — one year off the **1837-03-06** already held from S19. Both dates and
the reasoning for leaving the question open are recorded on [[egidius_peremans]] itself,
not resolved here.

**A recurring non-candidate.** *Franciscus Engels, veldwachter*, also witnesses
[[peremans]]'s own 1846 marriage act at Zaventem, aged 35 there and 38 here — the two ages
intersect cleanly at a birth in Feb–Aug 1810, consistent with one man holding a single
per-commune post across both weddings. No person file is warranted for a recurring
professional witness.

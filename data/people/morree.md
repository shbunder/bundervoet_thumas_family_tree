STRONG LEAD, NOT GRAFTED (July 2026) — see [[francl_s]] for the whole of it. A Brugge
marriage act of 1851 gives an Anna Francisca **Morbeé**, daughter of Petrus Josephus Morbeé
and Joanna De Clerck, marrying a Franciscus Léopoldus Smessaert. The forenames and the
husband match; the surname does not.

Morree and Morbeé are a plausible pair to confuse in a nineteenth-century hand — the double
r and the rb are near-identical in cursive — and this record's spelling comes from a member
tree, not from an act. That makes the act the better authority in principle, but "in
principle" is not evidence, so nothing is changed here. Her son's 1857 Oostende birth act
would settle which spelling the registrar used.

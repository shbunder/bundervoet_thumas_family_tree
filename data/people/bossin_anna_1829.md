Sister of [[bossin_guilielmus_1824]], and the first of that generation this tree has besides
him. Her marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 9, 1 December 1853 (S22) — names her
parents as [[bossin_arnoldus]] and [[deyn_elisabeth]], exactly as her brother's 1846 act
names his, and it is her brother who stands as a witness at her wedding.

She was of age, born at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on 3 November 1829 — a date the act gives to
the day — living there still, and working as a *dagloonster*. Like almost everyone in the
act, she declared herself unable to write.

She married Françiscus Pardon, a servant of Zaventem. Banns were published in both
communes on 20 and 27 November.

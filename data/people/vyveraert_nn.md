Wife of [[bundervoet_petrus_1560]], the earliest recorded ancestor of the Bundervoet
line. Her given name is not recorded — only the family name — so she is written the
way [[vandenberghe_nn]] is, and for the same reason.

His record says he married twice; this is the only wife either tree names, and
which of the two marriages it was is not stated. Whether [[bundervoet_livinus_1600]] is
her son or the other wife's is therefore open, and the maternity is deliberately not
recorded. FRONTIER — an Evergem marriage act naming her.

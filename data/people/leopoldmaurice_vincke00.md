Son of [[theophil_vincke]] and [[valerie_bolle]] — one of six children the couple's own
birth-index entries surface (Diksmuide act nr. 11, `rab-bs-geboorten`), contingent on
their parent link being accepted. Died in infancy, aged about three weeks (Diksmuide act
nr. 26, `rab-bs-overlijdens`). Index only, not the register image, hence `sup`.

**Name reuse, not a duplicate.** An elder brother, [[leopoldmaurice_vincke95]], was born
under the identical name five years earlier and also died in infancy. Two distinct
children given the same name after the first died — the ordinary pattern already
recorded elsewhere in this family (see [[karolus_vincke]]/[[charles_vincke]]) — not a
transcription artefact of one child counted twice.

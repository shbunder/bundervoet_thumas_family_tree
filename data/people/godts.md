Mother of the groom in her son [[coenraets_f]]'s marriage act — Kraainem nr. 4, 15 April
1899 (S27), which gives her death exactly: **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 5 January 1875**. Her son
was two and a half.

The act gives her no age and no occupation, so this record has neither.

A witness at that wedding is **Franciscus Godts**, 54, a plasterer of Kraainem — her surname,
her commune's neighbour, and the right age to be her brother. He is named here and not
grafted: the act calls him a witness and nothing more.

Born in Tottenham, London in 1943 while his family were WWII refugees (Tottenham was then in the Edmonton registration district); an English registrar mis-wrote the Flemish name. Buried at Stene, Oostende. His 1943 London birth certificate is the key record to confirm his father Gustaaf and mother Simonne Vandewalle — and, if his father died in the war, Simonne likely returned to Oostende a widow with Roland and his sister.

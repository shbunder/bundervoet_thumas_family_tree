Wife of the mason Petrus Jacobus Vanstechelman; died in 1927, the same year as her husband.

CORROBORATED (July 2026). She is named as the mother — as *Clementia Sophia Wagebaert*, and
in one act as *Clemence* — on three Oostende civil acts of her children, alongside Petrus
Jacobus ([[fs-wvl-bs]]). Her own dates are still unrecorded here; the acts name her without
giving them.

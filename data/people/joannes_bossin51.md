Named as the child in a civil birth act at **Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 19, 15 Aug 1851**.
**Sex is stated in the act** ("Man"), not read off the forename.

The father, [[guilielmus_bossin]], self-declares (*aangever*), aged 27, born at
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, a *daglooner*. The mother, [[peremans]], is aged 27, born at
Zaventem. Both ages fall inside this couple's already-established 1824 birth years, and
both birthplaces match exactly — two independent identifiers on each side.

A fourth sibling: FamilySearch's index (S24) had already found [[cornelius_bossin]]
(1847), [[bossin]] (1849) and [[ludovica_bossin]] (1853) for this couple, but not this
child, born between the second and third of those. This act closes that gap.

**A namesake, not a relative — do not confuse with [[jbbossin]].** This tree already
holds a *different* Joannes Baptista Bossin, husband of [[maria_wolf]] and father of
[[ludovicus_bossin79]]. The two are not the same man and this act does not connect their
families: this pass's other finding — [[jbbossin]]'s own 1858 marriage act — names *his*
parents as Judocus Bossin and Maria Desmedt, not [[guilielmus_bossin]] and [[peremans]].
Same forename, same surname, same small commune, two unrelated men a generation apart in
the records. See the note on [[jbbossin]] for the act that settles this.

**The mother's forename here is a reconciled variant, not an open question.** The act
calls her *Anna Catharina Peremans*, shorter than this tree's *Joanna Catharina Jacoba*.
That is not the unresolved Haesaerts-style conflict flagged elsewhere in this family
(§51/§53/§54): the anchor act that put [[peremans]] in this tree in the first place — her
own 1846 marriage act, S19, read as an image — already gives her the full *Joanna
Catharina Jacoba Peremans*, and her own record already reasons that shorter forms
elsewhere are a clerk's quotation from memory of that document. This 1851 act repeats an
already-reconciled shortening; it does not itself have to do the reconciling, which is
exactly what distinguishes it from the Haesaerts case, where no document ever settled the
variant independently of the disputed act.

**CORROBORATED (2026-07-27), independently of his own birth act for the first time.** He
stands witness at his sister [[ludovica_bossin]]'s wedding — Kraainem, 25 February 1884 —
given as 32, *pleisteraar*. Age 32 on that date is an exact arithmetic match to the
1851-08-15 birth this record already holds.

**UPGRADED TO `doc` (2026-07-27) — THE REGISTER IMAGE WAS READ, and it states the
relationship outright.** The act's own words, not this tree's inference from a shared
birth act: *"Joannes Baptista Bossin, pleisteraar, oud twee en dertig jaren, wonende te
Crainhem, **broeder der bruid**"* — brother of the bride, in the register itself. The
sibship no longer rests on the arithmetic match to his own 1851 birth act alone.

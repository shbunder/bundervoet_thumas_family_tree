Married [[misabella_t]] at Kraainem on **10 April 1900** (act nr. 3, S28). Born at
**Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on 5 December 1879**, a *polijster*, son of [[jbbossin]] — dead since
1887 — and [[maria_wolf]], who kept an inn there.

**He shares his wife's mother's surname, and that is left as a question, not an answer.** His
bride's mother is [[bossin]], Antonia Bossin of Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, and the witnesses at this
wedding include two more Bossins, hoveniers of Zaventem and Kraainem. Sint-Stevens-Woluwe is a
small commune and the families are very likely connected — but nothing in the act says how,
and a shared surname in one village is not a link. No parent line is drawn.

**Note the id.** He is not [[ludovica_bossin]], Antonia's sister born 1853; the two ids differ
by a single letter and the people differ by a generation and a sex. This one carries his birth
year for exactly that reason.

FRONTIER: his parents' marriage, which would say whether [[jbbossin]] descends from the same
Bossins as Antonia.

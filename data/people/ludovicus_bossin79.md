Married [[misabella_t]] at Kraainem on **10 April 1900** (act nr. 3, S28). Born at
**Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on 5 December 1879**, a *polijster*, son of [[jbbossin]] — dead since
1887 — and [[maria_wolf]], who kept an inn there.

**RESOLVED (2026-07-27) — the shared surname is not a shared family.** This record used
to leave open whether he shared blood, not just a surname, with his bride's mother
[[bossin]] (Antonia Bossin of Sint-Stevens-Woluwe). His father [[jbbossin]]'s own 1858
marriage act now names *his* parents — Judocus Bossin and Maria Desmedt — a different
couple from Antonia's parents, [[guilielmus_bossin]] and [[peremans]]. The two Bossin
families are not connected by this line. The witnesses at this 1900 wedding — two more
Bossins, hoveniers of Zaventem and Kraainem — remain unexplained by this finding; a small
commune can still hold more than one unconnected Bossin family, and no claim is made about
them here.

**Note the id.** He is not [[ludovica_bossin]], Antonia's sister born 1853; the two ids differ
by a single letter and the people differ by a generation and a sex. This one carries his birth
year for exactly that reason.

**A first known son (2026-07-27):** [[karel_bossin01]], born Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, 27
February 1901. That birth act gives him, self-declaring at 21, as a *daglooner* — where
his own 1900 marriage act (S28) gives *polijster*. Both trades are kept on their own
records; a labourer's trade moving within a year is not a conflict.

His own birth is independently corroborated by a further act, the Sint-Stevens-Woluwe
register entry his parents' ages are checked against on [[jbbossin]] and [[maria_wolf]]'s
records (S33): his father aged 45, *landbouwer*, and his mother aged 43, both matching
their marriage-act birthdates exactly.

Married [[anna_maria_bossin]] at Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on 1 December 1853 (act nr. 9, S22),
so he is [[guilielmus_bossin]]'s brother-in-law.

Born at Winksele on 16 February 1830, and living at Zaventem by the time he married,
working as a *dienstbode*. He brought his National Militia certificate to the wedding —
the act says it was attached to the record — and, like his bride and his parents, declared
himself unable to write.

Note the spelling: the act writes *Françiscus*, with the cedilla, and that is what this
record follows.

`sup`: AGATHA's transcription, not the register image.

DIED (2026-07-26): **Zaventem, 18 July 1874**, per three of his children's own later marriage
acts — Joanna Coleta's (Zaventem, 29 January 1877), Henricus's (Zaventem, 18 July 1881) and Maria
Thérèsia's (Zaventem, 15 November 1886) — each independently naming him as their late father with
that exact date and place (`zaventem-pardon-children-marriages-1877-1886`). None of the acts this
tree already held for him states a death directly; it comes from his children's own registers,
read a generation later.

Four children are recorded: [[henricus_pardon54]], [[joanna_pardon56]], [[cornelius_pardon59]]
and [[maria_pardon63]]. The family's residence moves from Sint-Stevens-Woluwe to Zaventem between
the 1856 and 1859 births — one migrating family, not two couples, since the same "Frans Pardon,
deceased Zaventem 18/07/1874" and the same mother, Anna Maria Bossin, recur across all three later
marriage acts, tying the Sint-Stevens-Woluwe-born and the Zaventem-born children to the same
parents. See [[cornelius_pardon59]] for the reasoning in full.

FRONTIER — **Isabella Pardon**, stated in her own Zaventem marriage act (to August Deryck, 21 May
1890) to have been born at Zaventem on 16 June 1868, daughter of "Jan Frans Pardon, deceased
Zaventem 18/07/1874" and Maria Anna Bossin
(https://www.openarchieven.nl/abl:d81c47c5-c5c5-a339-0df1-03d43a09b42c). Both the father's death
date and place and the mother's name match this couple exactly, and her stated birth year would
make her a fifth child, younger than [[maria_pardon63]]. No birth act naming her has turned up in
the held corpus — the date above is what her own marriage act states about her, not a birth
registration — so she stays a named frontier and not a record of her own.

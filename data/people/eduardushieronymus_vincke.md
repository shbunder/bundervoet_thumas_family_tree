Son of [[theophil_vincke]] and [[valerie_bolle]] — the last of six children the couple's
own birth-index entries surface, contingent on their parent link being accepted. Born at
**Oostende**, act nr. 1184, rather than Diksmuide like his five older siblings: the
family had moved there by 1905, which is also where [[theophil_vincke]] himself died in
1922. No death act found in the reachable pages. Index only, not the register image,
hence `sup`.

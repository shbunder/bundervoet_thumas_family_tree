Daughter of [[thumas_georges_1836]] and [[bossin_antonia_1849]], and so a sister of [[thumas_joannes_1876]] — an aunt of the
Thumas line that runs down to Dorien.

Found in AGATHA's index while searching for her parents' own 1872 marriage: a **Kraainem
marriage of 15 April 1899** in which she is the bride and her parents are given as Georgius
Carolus Josephus Thumas and Antonia Bossin.

ACT OPENED (2026-07-26) — the frontier this record named is closed. Kraainem act nr. 4 of 15
April 1899 (S27) gives her birth as **Kraainem, 24 June 1878**, her occupation as *zonder
beroep*, and her husband as **[[coenraets_franciscus_1872]]**, a *brouwersgast* of Kraainem born at
Sint-Stevens-Woluwe in 1872, son of a shopkeeper of the same name and of [[godts_anna]], who had
died in 1875.

The act also states her mother's death exactly — Kraainem, **7 November 1894** — where
[[bossin_antonia_1849]]'s own record had carried only the year.

She is one of at least three sisters: [[thumas_joanna_1873]] married in 1892 and [[thumas_maria_1880]] in
1900, both at Kraainem, both from acts naming the same two parents.

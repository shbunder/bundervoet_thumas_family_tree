Lived to 81; born in the city of Oostende but died in neighbouring rural Stene, where the family had settled.

**THE IDENTIFIER FOR THIS WHOLE SIBSHIP (2026-07-27).** "Joannes Vaniseghem" is not, by
itself, a name that picks out one man in this venue: grouping every VANISEGHEM Joannes
father row in the province-wide birth index by the named mother turns up **at least nine
different men recorded identically as "Joannes Vaniseghem"**, each paired with a different
wife — Piesens Sophia, Werbrouck Barbara, Dekegelaere Philippina, Demeyer Maria, Carette
Virginia, Lycke Isabelle, Vanhaecke Justine/Christina, and this record's own Hermanie
(Hermania/Hermina) Ludovica Janssen. The father's forename alone is therefore not an
identifier here — **the pair, both parents' full names written in full, is the
two-identifier minimum**, and it holds for the eight birth rows below: no row anywhere in
the province pairs "Joannes" with any mother but Hermania/Hermina Ludovica Janssen.

**TWO UNNAMED INFANT DEATHS, NOT GRAFTED — no name, no id, no file (CLAUDE.md rule 5).**
Oostende, 18/11/1878, act nr. 626, RAB ID 264403: Sex M, born Oostende, Christinastraat
72, no age given; father comparant "VANISEGHEM Joannes, 26, Werkman … vader" and mother
25 — both ages consistent with this couple in 1878. And Oostende, 06/11/1879, act nr.
570, RAB ID 266409: Detail entirely blank. Both are transcribed with "Sn" (no forename)
in the Deceased field. **"Sn" is a name-position code, not a sex marker** — the codes
compound ("VANISEGHEM Sn Vr", "DEVLOO Sn Ml"), so Sn means registered without a forename
and Ml/Vr (manlijk/vrouwelijk) carry the sex separately; the venue's own Vocabularium does
not define the code, this is read off the corpus itself. Of 71 such rows scanned
province-wide across the Van Iseghem and Janssen result sets, only **33 reproduce** on a
second read; the other 38 could not be reproduced and are not claimed. Not one of the 33
names a Partner and most name both parents — the signature of a newborn registration, not
an adult's. Recorded as **"unnamed child"**, not "stillborn": the death Detail form has no
"Stillborn" field at all (only the birth form does), so that distinction needs the
register image, not this index. The hypothesis that the 1878 row is [[vaniseghem_gustavus_1878]] dying
at ten months is **rejected**: every other child of this couple who died after being
named appears in this index *under that name* (see [[vaniseghem_augustus_1875]], [[vaniseghem_joannes_1883]],
[[vaniseghem_ludovicus_1888]] below), so the venue's own demonstrated convention is that a named child
is transcribed by name — "Sn" is consistent only with a child never named.

Full brother of [[bundervoet_segerius_1615]], by [[bundervoet_livinus_1600]] and [[elisabeth]], and
named for his father.

He is the third Livinus in three generations of this family, which is worth keeping in mind
when reading any record: a Livinus Bundervoet of the mid-1600s at Evergem could be the father
(~1600-~1651) or this son (~1615-~1670).

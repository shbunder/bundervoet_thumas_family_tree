Son of [[edouard_bocklandt]] and [[marie_vanbergen]] — a previously unrecorded sibling of
[[louise_bocklandt]]. His death act — Oostende nr. 712, 26 October 1892
([[agatha-oostende-1892-death-alphonsus-bocklandt]]) — names his father and mother and
states he was born at Hamme (Oost-Vlaanderen), but gives no age, so no birth date is
recorded here. AGATHA serves this act only as an "analyse van akte" (index transcription),
not the register image, hence `sup`.

Second wife of [[bundervoet_egidius]]. Her death act at Gent on 15 July 1853 gives her age
as 76 — hence a birth about 1777 — her birthplace as **Lokeren**, her parents as Joannes
Baptista Ledent and Joanna Catharina De Strooper, and her husband as Egidius Bundervoet.
That act is the whole basis of §73's sixth Gent patriarch couple.

**Necessarily the later marriage.** Egidius married [[peeters_annamaria]] at Gent in 1787,
when Maria Francisca was ten years old. So she cannot be the wife of 1787, and the two
marriages sit in that order — which is what reconciles §73's death-index reading of this
couple with the 1787 marriage act found this pass. Her own marriage to Egidius is
undated here because no act for it has been read.

Index-level throughout, so `sup`. Her parents are named in the source but have no records:
they are Lokeren people, one generation up, and nothing in this tree reaches them yet.

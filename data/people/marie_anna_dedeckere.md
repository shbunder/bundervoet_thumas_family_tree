Wife of [[petrus_b1727]] and mother of his eight children, so a great-aunt by marriage of
this tree's direct line rather than an ancestor of it.

She outlived him by more than thirty years: he was buried at Assenede in 1777, she died in
1809. Her own parents are not recorded in the paulderidder tree, and nothing else has been
read for her.

Daughter of [[edouard_bocklandt]] and [[marie_vanbergen]], and [[louise_bocklandt]]'s
eldest known sister. Her marriage act — Oostende nr. 80, 26 May 1888
([[agatha-oostende-1888-marriage-bocklandt-ketels]]) — names her born at Hamme 30 April
1870, *werkmeid*, resident at Oostende, daughter of Eduardus Bocklandt (50,
*touwslagersknecht*) and Maria Louisa Vanbergen. She married **Petrus Dominicus Ketels**
(b. Waasmunster 15 Oct 1857, *touwslagersknecht*), whose own father is not named in the act
(*NN NN*) and whose mother is Maria Louisa Ketels of Waasmunster. AGATHA serves this act
only as an "analyse van akte" (index transcription), not the register image, hence `sup`.

FRONTIER, not pursued here. Two further AGATHA-indexed Stene acts of 1898 name her as
mother — a birth, Elisa Ketels (13 Oct 1898, act GEWVL_GEBWVL_00356758, which also repeats
her own birthplace as Hamme O-Vl), and an infant death, Philemon Ketels (12 Nov 1898, act
OVWVL_00329163) — so she and Petrus Ketels had at least one more child than this record
shows. Neither act is registered as a source and neither child has a record here: objective
2 reaches this generation, but it is not this pass's work.

Married [[bundervoet_petronella_1754]] at Assenede in 1781. Their daughter
[[vermeeren_maria_1787]] died across the border at Hontenisse in 1814.

**His surname is written two ways by the two sources that name him** — *Vermeire* in the
Assenede marriage index, *Vermeeren* in the Dutch death act of 1814, where he appears as
"Karel Vermeeren". The Flemish form is kept here because that is what the register of his
own marriage gives; the Dutch act is a clerk in another country writing down a name
reported to him. Neither is corrected into the other and no third form is invented.

Nothing else is stated about him: no birth, no death, no trade. The Assenede marriage act
would give his age and his parents.

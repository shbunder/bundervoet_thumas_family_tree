**One of the six Gent Bundervoet patriarchs of research-log §73, and the first of them to
become a person rather than a name in a death index.** He is not connected to Renée and
Léon's line: this record exists under **objective 3**, which expects disconnected
Bundervoet roots and asks for the links between them.

Two acts read this pass name him directly. His son's **baptism extract** (Sint-Maarten,
Gent, 22 April 1788) has *filium **Ægidii Bundervoet ex Sancti Petri*** — of Gent's
**Sint-Pieters parish**, which is where the Bundervoet surname is thickest in the Gent
registers — and he signs the register himself in the Flemish form, **Gillis Bundervoet**.
Thirty-one years later the **notarial act of 21 October 1818** finds him alive in Gent, a
*stekwercker*, living **in de Ackerstraete numero twee en twintig**, consenting to that
son's marriage in Rotterdam.

**Married twice.** First, in **1787** at Gent Sint-Michiels Noord, to
[[peeters_annamaria]] — the Geneanet index has the marriage, and the 1818 act calls her
*by wylent*, "the late", so she died between 1788 and 1818. Second to
[[ledent_mariafrancisca_1777]], who was born at Lokeren in 1777 and died at Gent in 1853;
she was ten years old in 1787, so hers is necessarily the later marriage. That ordering is
what reconciles §73's death-index reading of this couple with the 1787 act.

**A name conflict, settled.** Both Rotterdam civil records — the 1818 marriage and the 1872
death — call his son's father *Petrus Bundervoet*. He is not Petrus. The Gent baptism
register, the Gent notary who stood in the room with him, and his own signature all say
Egidius/Gillis. The Rotterdam clerk was wrong, and §76 recorded the conflict before it
could be resolved.

**THE FRONTIER, and it is the best one open on objective 3.** The godfather at his son's
baptism was **Livinus Bundervoet**, who *dixit se non posse scribere* — declared he could
not write. Godparents in a Flemish baptism register are almost always kin, so this is the
nearest approach yet to the generation above the Gent patriarchs. The Gent Sint-Pieters
register holds several candidate Livinus Bundervoets, of whom *Livinus Bundervoet ×
Petronilla De Smet, married Sint-Pieters 1757*, sits in the right generation to be his
father. **Not grafted** — a forename and a parish are one identifier. His own marriage act
of 1787 would name his parents outright and settle it.

Son of [[eduardus_vi]] and [[emma_vincke]], and [[joannes_vi]]'s brother — found
incidentally while sweeping the birth index for rows naming Emma Celesta Vincke as
mother (Oostende act nr. 501, `rab-bs-geboorten`). Not itemised on this couple's own
records before now: he has no marriage act in this sweep, unlike his five siblings
[[flavie_vi]], [[eduardus_vi2]], [[joannes_vi]], [[valentina_vi]] and [[maria_vi]], so his
own birth row is the only evidence for him.

**NOT INDEPENDENTLY RE-CHECKED.** This birth date was read off the same index whose
"Date" and "Certificate Date" columns are confirmed elsewhere in this family to be
conflated (see `rab-bs-overlijdens`'s note, and the corrections on [[irma_vincke]] and
[[jerome_vincke]], and on Albert Joseph Vincke, not itself a record here — see
[[emma_vincke]]'s frontier note). No second act or memorial card has confirmed this
date, so treat it as unverified until a second source is read. A second son of the same
names, [[camillushenricus_vi11]], was born six years later — possibly because this one
died in the interval, but no death act has been found for either.

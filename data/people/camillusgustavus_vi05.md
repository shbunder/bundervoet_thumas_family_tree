**HIS OWN DEATH ACT FOUND (2026-07-27), NOT "no death act for either brother" as first
framed** — he was mistakenly described in an earlier search as "a sixth child not in the
tree"; he was already held here. What is new is his death: Oostende, 29 November 1905
(Certificate Date 30/11/1905, a one-day offset), act nr. 810, RAB ID 500127. Sex M. The
Detail's own Birth Date field reads 25/04/1905, matching this record's birth date exactly
— **a second, independent identifier**, so this birth date is no longer single-sourced.
He lived about seven months, dying at Aartshertoginnestraat 20.

The mother is written **"VINCKE Emma Coleta"** on this act, against "Emma Celesta" on her
own 1907 marriage act and elsewhere in this tree. `data/forenames.json` folds
"celesta"~"celestina"/"celestine", but not "coleta"/"colette" — this pair is not covered
by that table. The identity does not depend on the fold: the father's rare full name
("Eduardus Ludovicus Rudolphus Van Iseghem") plus the exact birth-date match already give
two identifiers. Recorded as a variant, not resolved into `forenames.json`.

**Both parents are recorded "Ongehuwd" (unmarried), aged 25** — [[eduardus_vi]] (b.
29/11/1880) turning 25 on the very day of this death, [[emma_vincke]] (b. 23/01/1880)
already 25 — consistent with the couple's own 1907 marriage two years later, not a
contradiction. This **dates the relationship to at least 1905**, bearing on the open
legitimation frontier on [[joannes_vi]]'s record (born Lens 1903) and [[flavie_vi]]'s
(born Diksmuide 1898, under the surname Vincke) — see those records.

Father of Joanna (Janneken) van Hecke (~1610–1678), who married Segerius Bundervoet —
so a direct ancestor, and with a birth about 1580 one of the earliest people in the
tree.

The paulderidder chart gives him as her father, born about 1580, with the mother shown
only as "?". Joanna's record previously said her parents were "not recorded here";
they were.

WORTH NOTING, NOT YET CLAIMED: Livinus Bundervoet's second wife was a Catharina van
Hecke (~1620–~1670), while his son Segerius married this Joanna van Hecke (~1610).
Two van Hecke women marrying father and son suggests one van Hecke family, and
possibly sisters — but nothing seen so far states a relationship between them, so
none is recorded here.

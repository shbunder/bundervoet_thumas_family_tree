Father of Marie Catharina Joostens (1810–1857), who married Georges Thumas — so a direct
ancestor through their son Georges Carolus Josephus.

Named, with his wife, in his daughter's Grez-Doiceau death act of 10 June 1857. That act is
the whole of what is known about him here: no dates, no place, no occupation, because it
gave none. His daughter's birthplace is recorded there as Woluwe-Saint-Lambert, which is
where this couple were most likely living around 1810, but the act does not say so and it
is not recorded as a fact about him.

FRONTIER: the Joostens–Deconninck marriage, and his own birth. Woluwe-Saint-Lambert and
Grez-Doiceau are the two communes to try, roughly 1800–1810 for the marriage.

Father of the bride in his daughter [[haesaerts_anna_1810]]'s marriage act — Bertem nr. 8,
30 October 1838, read as the register image. A *landbouwer* of Berthem, present and
consenting in person, and recorded as unable to sign.

The image corrects AGATHA's transcription of his name: the ink reads **Joannes Baptista**,
not *Joannes Baptist*.

He is not called *wijlen* and is given no age, so this record carries no birth date and the
death entry says only that he was alive on the day.

`sup`, not `doc`: see [[coekelberghs_henricus]] for why an image-read act still yields a
`sup` person record here.

FRONTIER — his own marriage to [[vandenbroeck_elisabeth]], which the Bertem registers should
hold for roughly 1800–1810. The film is **DGS 004471767** (*Huwelijken 1796–1832*),
unindexed and bound in blocks by record type.

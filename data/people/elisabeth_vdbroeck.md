Mother of the bride in her daughter [[anna_haesaerts]]'s marriage act — Bertem nr. 8,
30 October 1838, read as the register image. A *landbouwster* of Berthem, present and
consenting in person, and recorded as unable to sign.

The image writes her surname as **three words**, *van den Broeck*, where AGATHA's
transcription runs them together. That is what this record follows, and it is worth
keeping: the spacing is what a name index will and will not match on.

No age in the act, so no birth date here; not called *wijlen*, so the death entry says only
that she was alive on the day.

`sup`, not `doc`: see [[henricus_coekelberghs]].

This is a different woman from [[mtheresia_vandenbroeck]], who is in this tree on another
line entirely — same surname, no established relation, and the two records are deliberately
not linked.

Son of [[antoine_vanald]] × [[brigitte_wyllie]], born 11 June 1803 (22 Prairial XI, the
Republican-calendar conversion checked by computation) at Diksmuide — **three days after
his parents' own 8 June wedding**. Checked rather than assumed to be a transcription slip:
8 June plus three days is 11 June exactly, and this reads as the ordinary pattern of a
couple marrying to legitimate a child already on the way, not an error.

Parentage rests on two independent civil acts, both naming Antonius (Josephus) × Brigitta
Wyllie in full: his own marriage, Diksmuide 20 April 1828, act nr. 76, RAB ID 85443, to
[[marietheresia_bouckaert]]; and his own death, Diksmuide act nr. 4, certificate 7 January
1869, RAB ID 2292938. Both are volunteer INDEX/Detail transcriptions (`rab-bs-huwelijken`,
`rab-bs-overlijdens`), `sup`, never `doc`.

His own children are visible in the same death index — sons Bernardus Franciscus (died
1842) and Emilius Jacobus (died 1846) — with further descendants marrying in 1854, 1858
and 1862. Noted here as a frontier for objective 2, not pursued this pass.

Married [[vandenberghe_paulina_1849]] at Hamme on 18 May 1882. He is here because she is: the act
that made her a graftable daughter of [[vanbergen_joannes_1815]] and [[vermandel_ida_1825]] is his
marriage act too, and a person named in this tree gets a record rather than living as a
string in someone else's prose.

Nothing else about him is recorded, because the pass that read this act was reading the
bride's half of it. His age, his trade, his birthplace and his parents are all on the same
image and simply have not been transcribed yet.

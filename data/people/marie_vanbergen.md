Mother of Louise Marie Bocklandt (1877, Hamme O-Vl). Full name Maria Louisa Van Bergen,
1842-1888. Her parents were Joannes Franciscus Van Bergen (1815-1898) × Ida Vermandel
(1825-1865).

CITATION REPAIRED (2026-07-26), replacing the §52 downgrade. Her own death act — Oostende
nr. 721, 23 November 1888 ([[agatha-oostende-1888-death-maria-louisa-vanbergen]]) — names
her father **Joannes Franciscus Vanbergen**, living at Hamme, *metser*, her mother **Ida
Vermandel**, "overleden te Hamme", and her partner **Eduardus Bocklandt**. CONFIDENCE STAYS
`sup`: AGATHA serves this act only as an "analyse van akte" (index transcription), not the
register image.

A BIRTHPLACE CORRECTION. The act states her *geboorteplaats* as **Waasmunster**, not Hamme.
The family prose elsewhere says Hamme throughout, but that was her father's residence at
the time of her death, not her own birth commune. Her own birth act, and her parents' own
marriage, therefore belong in the **Waasmunster** registers rather than Hamme's;
[[ida_vermandel]]'s record carries the same pointer, and is high on the research queue.

Death date and act date differ by one day: she died 22 November 1888, the act was drawn up
23 November — ordinary Belgian declare-the-day-after practice, not a conflict.

Occupation *werkvrouw* is from this 1888 act, at Oostende; the 1880 Hamme death act of her
son Ludovicus ([[fs-hamme-1880-death-ludovicus-bocklandt]]) calls her *spinster*, aged 39,
eight years earlier — the field keeps the later trade, this note keeps the earlier one.

A SISTER (2026-07-26): [[paulina_vdberghe]], born at Hamme about 1849, whose own 1882
marriage act names the same parents — and dates their mother's death to the day that
[[ida_vermandel]]'s own death act gives independently. Two more probable siblings, Josephus
(b. 1852) and Henricus (b. 1861), are named as frontiers on their mother's record and are
not grafted: they rest on a name index and their acts have not been read.

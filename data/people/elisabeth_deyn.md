Mother of the groom in her son [[guilielmus_bossin]]'s marriage act — Zaventem, act nr.
1, 24 February 1846 (S19), read as an image. She was present and consenting, and like her
husband [[arnoldus_bossin]] she signed with a mark, the act stating she could not write.

The act gives her no age and no occupation, so this record has neither. The death entry
records only that she was alive on the day.

FRONTIER — her marriage to Arnoldus Bossin, and her surname's spelling. *Deyn* as written
here is also found as De(y)n, Deijn and Deyns in Brabant registers, and only her own acts
will settle which this family used.

ACT READ (2026-07-26): named as mother of the bride in her daughter
[[anna_maria_bossin]]'s marriage act — Sint-Stevens-Woluwe nr. 9, 1 December 1853 (S22).

**And named as nothing more than a name.** Where the 1846 act had her present, consenting
and signing with a mark, the 1853 analysis gives her no residence, no trade and no remark,
while giving her husband all three. That is a difference worth recording and not worth
interpreting: an absent field in a transcription may mean she was absent, or dead, or
simply that the field was not filled. Her death bound therefore still rests on 1846, and
nothing is inferred from the silence. The register image would settle it.

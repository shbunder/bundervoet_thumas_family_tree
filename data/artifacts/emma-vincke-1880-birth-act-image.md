**THE ACT IMAGE WAS READ, not an index row.** This is the register page itself, at the
deepest resolution the viewer publishes, cropped to act nr. 14 and its margin heading. It
supersedes `emma-vincke-1880-birth-diksmuide`, which is the AGATHA volunteer index
transcription of the same act and remains `sup`.

**How to return to it.** FamilySearch image group **004833210**, **image 392 of 1269**,
ark `3:1:33SQ-GPD9-32V`, collection *Belgium, West Flanders, Civil Registration,
1582-1950* (cc=2139860); document custodian Rijksarchief te Brugge. AGATHA's own remark on
the act, **"SCAN 392 GSU"**, is exactly this frame number — the bridge AGATHA itself does
not publish, since its West-Flanders birth project exposes no scan link, no IIIF and no
Mirador for this act (re-checked while logged in, 2026-07-27: still none).

The register spread carries acts nr. 11–14; act nr. 14 is the lower entry on the right-hand
page. Margin heading: **Geboorte. / Vincke / Emma Celesta / N° 14.**

Transcription of the body, as written:

> In den Jare duist acht honderd tachentig, op den vier en twintigsten dag der maand van
> januari, ten negen ure en half 's morgens voor ons **Edmond Paret**, schepen, bedienende
> de plaats van Burgemeester, openbaren ambtenaar van den burgerstand der stad **Dixmude**,
> in Westvlaanderen is gecompareerd: **Lucien Julianus Vincke, metsersdiender, oud veertig
> jaren, geboren en woonende te Dixmude**, die ons een kind van het vrouwgeslacht vertoond
> heeft gebaren in zyn woonhuis staande op den **Kleinendyk** binnen deze stad, **gisteren
> den dry en twintigsten dezer maand, ten dry ure 's morgens**, van hem verklaarder en van
> zyne echtgenoote **Ludovica Maria Vanalderweireldt, kantwerkster, oud vyf en dertig
> jaren, ook geboren en woonende te Dixmude**; aan welk kind hy verklaard heeft te willen
> de voornamen geven van **Emma Celesta**. Bovengemelde vertooning en …

What the image adds over the index transcription:

- **The birth hour and the address** — 3 a.m. on 23 January 1880, in the father's own
  dwelling on the **Kleinendyk** at Diksmuide. Neither is in AGATHA.
- **The father's trade, in the register's own hand: *metsersdiender*.** The tree held
  "metserdiener" from a member tree; the act writes it, with the linking -s-.
- **Both parents' stated ages** — father 40, mother 35 — and that both were *born and
  living at Diksmuide*.
- **The mother's occupation, *kantwerkster* (lacemaker)** — not previously held anywhere.

**A one-year offset in both stated ages, in the same direction.** Against the dates this
tree holds — [[lucien_vincke]] b. 26 March 1840, [[ludovica_vanald]] b. 23 November 1845 —
on 24 January 1880 he was 39y 10m and she was 34y 2m, while the act says 40 and 35. Each
stated age is the age the person would *reach during 1880*, not the age completed at the
declaration. Both err by exactly one year in the same direction, which reads as one clerk's
consistent practice rather than as two independent contradictions; it is recorded here
rather than smoothed away, and it does not on its own unseat either held birth date.

**On the surname spelling.** The register writes the mother's surname in full as
*Vanalderweireldt* — with the R after `-ALD-` — so this act is *not* one of the
`VANALDEWEIRELDT` / `VANDERWEIRELT` variants that hid records elsewhere in this family.
Re-cropped at 6× zoom in three separate passes (2026-07-27): the **R after `-ALD-` is
clearly present**, and the final letter-cluster reads **`-eldt`** — both now read with
confidence, superseding this artifact's own earlier caveat that the last four characters
were "not letter-by-letter certain."

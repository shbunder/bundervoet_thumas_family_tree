The page itself, not a search result about it. Printed page 51 of a 19th-century inventory
of Flemish feudal records, with its FamilySearch transcription beside it. The **BUNDERVOET**
heading is highlighted, and under it four entries, all at **Saint-Pierre-Alost**:

> **Catherine Bundervoet**, veuve de Jean De Smet, fils de Balthazar, mère et tutrice de
> Balthazar De Smet, son fils — **3 mars 1687**
> **Liévin Bundervoet, fils de François** — **16 février 1700**
> **Liévin Bundervoet, fils de Pasquier** — **16 février 1700**
> **Pasquier Bundervoet, fils de Liévin** — **4 février 1701**

Each ends *"Le scel manque"* — the seal is missing.

**THIS CORRECTS THE EARLIER ARTIFACT.** Reading the same material through search-result
snippets, [[bundervoet-fulltext-flanders-fiefs]] recorded "Catherine Bundervoet, widow of
Jean De Smet — Meirelbeke, 1 April 1587". That was wrong in both date and place, and the
error is instructive: the entry immediately above BUNDERVOET on this page is a different
surname —

> **BUNDERE (VAN DEN)** — Nicolas Van den Bundere, tuteur de Jeanne Ser Lambrechts, fille
> d'Olivier, tient un fief situé à **Meirelbeke**. Le **1er avril 1587**.

The snippet had run the tail of the Van den Bundere entry into the head of the Bundervoet
one. Two more dates were also off: Liévin fils de François is 1700 and not 1687, and
Pasquier fils de Liévin is 4 February 1701 and not 16 February 1700.

**What the page actually supports.** A Bundervoet family holding fiefs at Saint-Pierre-Alost
across 1687–1701, with descent stated twice over: François → Liévin, and Liévin ⇄ Pasquier
in both directions, which is a father and son each holding in turn. Catherine is a widow
acting as guardian for her son.

**Still not our family.** Alost is in Oost-Vlaanderen but some 30 km from Evergem, and
nothing here ties these people to [[bundervoet_petrus_1560]] or [[bundervoet_livinus_1600]] beyond
the surname and the period. Recorded as the shape of a Bundervoet family in the right
century, and as a lead for objective 3 — not as a link.

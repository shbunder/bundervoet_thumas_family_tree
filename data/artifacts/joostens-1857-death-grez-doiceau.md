**What it settles.** Her death was **10 June 1857** — the tree held only the year. Her
birthplace, Woluwe-Saint-Lambert, is confirmed from the act rather than from an index. Her
occupation was *ménagère*. And her parents [[joostens_guillaume]] and [[deconninck_jeanne]],
who were added to this tree from an index entry a pass earlier, are now read at the archive.

**Her own age checks out, which is what makes the next paragraph interesting.** She is 47 in
June 1857, putting her birth in 1809–10; the tree says 1810. The act's arithmetic is sound
where it can be tested.

**And yet her husband's age does not fit.** Georges Thumas is given as **63**, which puts his
birth around 1793–94. [[thumas_georges_1804]] is recorded here as born **24 January 1804**, which
would have made him 53 — a ten-year gap, in an act that got his wife's age right.

Neither figure has been changed. Ten years is too much to wave away as the usual slippage,
and it cuts both ways: the 1804 date comes from a member tree and has never been documented,
while 63 was spoken by whoever reported the death. His trade, *menuisier*, matches the tree
exactly, so this is certainly the right man — only his age is in doubt.

FRONTIER: the Grez-Doiceau baptisms for 1793–94 and 1804, and his own 1864 death act, which
would give an age to compare. Note that his father [[thumas_georges_1772]] was born in 1772: a
son in 1794 makes him a father at 22, in 1804 at 32. Both are ordinary, so the parent link
does not decide it.

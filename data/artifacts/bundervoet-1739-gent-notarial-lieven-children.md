**The register page itself**, reconstructed from the deep-zoom tiles at level 12
(13 × 12 tiles, ~3300 px) and read at 2× on the text column. A transport of rents, drawn by
**Joannes Baptiste de Stobbeleere**, notary public at Gent, on a twelve-schelling stamp of
**1739**.

> Compareerde voor mij **Joan[nes] Bap[tis]te de Stobbeleere** notaris publicq tot Ghendt
> residerende … in persoone **Frans Ceuterick** ende met hem bij sijnen consente ende
> authoritaetie **Livine Bundervoet filia Lieven, verweckt bij Joanne de Smet syne
> huijsvrauwe** … gheceedeert ende ghetransporteert thebben aen ende ten proffijte van
> vrauwe **Susanne Marie de Smet, douariere van wijlent Remij du Laurij**, in syn leven
> Raedt van syne Majestijdts Grooten Raede, het derde deel deurgaende;
> dies ghelijck derde deel competeert aende voornoemde vrauwe douariere … by transporte
> ghepasseert voor mij nots voornoemt den vierden deser maendt van **Livinus Bundervoet
> filius Lieven voornoemt**, ende het resterende derde competeert aen **Catharine
> Bundervoet dochter van voorseyden Lieven**, eerst van eene obligatie van twee hondert
> vijftigh ponden grooten sterck wisselghelt capitaels, debiteur **Paschier Bundervoet
> filius Lieven, opsetenen der heerlijckhede van Sint Pieters Aelst**, loopende den
> penninck twintigh …

**What the image settles that the transcription could not.**

- **The mother.** *"verweckt bij Joanne de Smet syne huijsvrauwe"* — the machine
  transcription never produced her at all. **Joanna de Smet** is the wife of Lieven and the
  mother of these children, and she is the single most valuable word on the page.
- **Four children of one father, each stated separately**: Livina *filia Lieven*, Livinus
  *filius Lieven*, Catharina *dochter van voorseyden Lieven*, Paschier *filius Lieven*.
- **Livina's husband is Frans Ceuterick**, which matches the Geneanet index entry
  *"BUNDERVOET Livina × CEUTERICX Franciscus (1737), Gent Sint-Pieters"* — an independent
  second source for the same couple.

**And a correction to research-log §83, made from this image.** §83 read the family's
residence off the machine transcription as Gent's **Sint-Pieters parish**, and said so —
which mattered, because that is the parish Egidius Bundervoet came from. **The page says
`opsetenen der heerlijckhede van Sint Pieters Aelst`** — the seigneury of
**Sint-Pieters-Aalst**, roughly 30 km from Gent and a different place entirely. The act is
merely *drawn* at Gent, before a Gent notary, as such transports routinely were. The
apparent link to Egidius's parish does not exist.

Note also that the counterparty, **Susanne Marie de Smet**, shares the mother's surname.
The act does not state a relationship and none is assumed here.

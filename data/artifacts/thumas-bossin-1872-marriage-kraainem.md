**What it adds.** Antonia Bossin's birth date, which the tree held only as the year 1849, is
**10 January 1849** — and her birthplace, Sint-Stevens-Woluwe, the same commune Joanna
Vandenbemden would come from thirty years later. Three occupations arrive with it:
*fabriekwerkster* for the bride, *landbouwer* for her father, *huishoudster* for her mother.

**What it proves.** Four parent links: the bridegroom as son of Georges Thumas × Maria
Catharina Joostens, the bride as daughter of Guilielmus Bossin × Anna Catharina Peremans.

**A change of trade, not a contradiction.** The bridegroom is a *fabrieksgast* — a factory
hand — here in 1872, and a *landbouwer en herbergier* in his son's 1902 act. Thirty years
apart, that is one man's life, not two men; the record keeps both and says which act each
came from.

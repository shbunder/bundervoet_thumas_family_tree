The act that broke the De Keyser wall, read directly from the image rather than from
an index. Transcribed in the relevant part:

> Eduardus De Keyser, werkman, geboren te Hamme den twaalfden November 1876 …
> echtgescheiden van Louisa Maria Bocklandt (echtscheiding uitgesproken, Rechtbank
> van eersten aanleg Brugge, ~1923) … zoon van Desiderius en van Maria Theresia
> Van den Broeck, beiden overleden.

Four things come from this one document: Édouard's parents, his birthplace of Hamme
in Oost-Vlaanderen, the divorce from Louise Bocklandt that no tree recorded, and the
1946 remarriage itself. Every identifier on the act matches our Édouard — the
occupation *werkman*, the birth date, and the first wife's full name — which is what
made the identification safe rather than merely plausible.

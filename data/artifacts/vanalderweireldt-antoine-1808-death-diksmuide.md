**Read the two date columns apart.** The Date column is empty and 21/07/1808 sits in
Certificate Date, so this act establishes the day the death was *registered*, not the day
it happened. This tree carries 1808-07-21 as Antoine's death date, and that value is the
registration date — the same conflation already documented on this page for the Vincke
deaths and for `brigitte_wyllie` below.

The same distinction *confirms* Brigitte rather than unsettling her: her death act
(Diksmuide act nr. 198, RAB ID 2298609) has certificate date 10/11/1824, while both her
sons' 1828 marriage acts state her decease as 08/11/1824 — the tree's date, with a two-day
registration lag.

**A conflict this act does not resolve.** It names Antoine's father **François**, where the
1803 marriage act names him **Jacques Ignace** and the 1857 death act of Maria Joanna
Vanalderweireldt (Diksmuide act nr. 211, RAB ID 2294441) names him **Jacobus Ignatius**.
The mother is Verhaeghe Isabelle / Isabella Claire / Isabella Clara in all three. Two of
three say Ignace; none of the three was read as an image.

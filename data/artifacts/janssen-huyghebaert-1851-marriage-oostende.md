**This is the index analysis, not the act image.** The Rijksarchief Brugge/Kortrijk
volunteers transcribed the act into this Detail view; the register page itself sits on
FamilySearch microfilm 1358587 (waypoint QZ9J-PQZ), which returned HTTP 403
unauthenticated this pass. So this is `sup` evidence throughout, however precise the
transcription.

What the page states, in full:

> **Location/Microfilm** Oostende / 1358587 · **Date** 28/08/1851 · **Certificate** 090
>
> **Groom** JANSSEN Josephus Joannes · **Bride** HUYGHEBAERT Apollonia Johanna
> **Given name Father Groom** Joannes · **Mother of the Groom** DECLERCK Victoria
> **Given Name Father Bride** Hubertus · **Mother Bride** DERUDDER Johanna
>
> Birth Date: 11/08/1820 (groom) · 26/09/1830 (bride)
> Place of Birth: Oostende (groom) · Oudenburg (bride)
> Age: 31 (groom) · 20 (bride)
> Residence: Oostende (both) · Profession: kleermaker (groom) · dienstmeid (bride)
> Profession Father: [blank] (groom) · schoenmaker (bride)
> Location of Decease Father: Oostende (groom) · [blank] (bride, i.e. living)
> Age Mother: 64 (groom's mother) · 59 (bride's mother)
> Profession mother: werkvrouw (groom's mother) · zonder beroep (bride's mother)

No witnesses, registrar, marriage contract or legitimated children are recorded on this
row — every one of those tables is present on the page but empty.

This is the act the whole pass turned on: it names **both** spouses' parents in one
document, because a Belgian marriage act always does. It puts [[janssen_joannes_1778]] ×
[[declerck_victoria_1786]] and [[huyghebaert_hubertus_1791]] × [[derudder_joanna_1793]] into the tree
for the first time, and both spouses' own birth date and place match this tree's
pre-existing (tree-gverdievel) values exactly.

**The spelling is not a discrepancy.** "Apollonia Johanna" here against this tree's
"Appolonia Joanna" is exactly what `data/forenames.json` folds: `apollonia~appolonia` and
`joanna~johanna` are both registered variant groups, checked directly rather than
assumed.

**The age given for Victoria Declerck (64) does not match her own birth date.** Her 1819
marriage act (see [[janssen-declerck-1819-marriage-oostende]]) gives 30/04/1786, which
makes her 65 in August 1851, not 64. Recorded as a stated conflict on
[[declerck_victoria_1786]]'s own page, not smoothed over — it is ordinary index slippage on a
self-reported age thirty-two years after the fact, and does not veto an identification
that rests on both spouses' exact birth date and place agreement plus a second,
independent birth-act corroboration (see [[janssen-declerck-1819-marriage-oostende]] and
the 1820 birth act on [[janssen_josephus_1820]]'s own page).

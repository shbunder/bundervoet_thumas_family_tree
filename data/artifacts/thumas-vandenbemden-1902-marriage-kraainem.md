The richest kind of record there is, and the reason the charter says to prefer marriage
acts: one document naming both spouses, **all four parents**, and four witnesses, with
occupations and residences for nearly all of them.

What it states in full:

| role | person | detail |
|---|---|---|
| Bruid | Joanna Vandenbemden | b. Sint-Stevens-Woluwe 15/06/1882 · living Kraainem · *naaister* |
| Vader van de bruid | Henricus Augustinus Vandenbemden | **died Kraainem 12/08/1889** |
| Moeder van de bruid | Maria Theresia Coekelberghs | living Kraainem · *herbergierster* |
| Bruidegom | Joannes Baptista Georgius Thumas | b. Kraainem 26/09/1876 · living Sint-Stevens-Woluwe · *fabriekwerker* |
| Vader van de bruidegom | Georgius Carolus Josephus Thumas | living Kraainem · *landbouwer en herbergier* |
| Moeder van de bruidegom | Antonia Bossin | — |
| Getuige | Georgius Thumas | 25 · Sint-Stevens-Woluwe · *metser* |
| Getuige | Joannes Baptista Coppens | 31 · Sint-Stevens-Woluwe · *metser* |
| Getuige | Petrus Josephus Vandenbemden | 38 · Kraainem · *landbouwer* |
| Getuige | Felix Vandenbemden | 25 · Kraainem · *schaliedekker* |

**Four parent links confirmed at once**, each of which the tree had only on a Geneanet
tree's word: Joannes Baptista Georgius as the son of Georgius Carolus Josephus × Antonia
Bossin, and Joanna as the daughter of Henricus Augustinus × Maria Theresia Coekelberghs.
Two occupations already in the tree — *naaister* and *landbouwer en herbergier* — are
confirmed word for word, which is itself a check that these are the same people.

**It also corrects one fact.** Henricus Augustinus Vandenbemden's death is given here as
**Kraainem**, where the tree said Sint-Stevens-Woluwe. The date, 12 August 1889, agrees
exactly, so it is certainly the same man and the place was simply wrong.

And it settles a spelling: the mother is *Antonia* Bossin, not "Antoina" as the tree had.

**What this is, exactly.** AGATHA's "analyses van akten" are the Rijksarchief's own
structured transcriptions of the civil registers, made by its volunteer programme, with
the act number and register cited. It is the archive publishing its reading of act nr. 2,
not a photograph of the page. That is a much stronger thing than a member tree — it is
why the people on it move to `doc` — but the scan itself has still not been read, and
anything resting on this rests on the transcriber.

FRONTIER — the four witnesses. A Georgius Thumas of 25 and two Vandenbemden men, all at
the couple's own communes, are the shape of brothers and uncles. The act gives their
ages and trades but never states a relationship, so none is recorded as a link. Their
own birth acts would say.

**THIS IS THE REGISTER IMAGE, READ — not an index and not a transcription.** The act was
located from the year's alphabetical *tafel* (same film, image 107, "V" section:
"[Van] Bergen, Joannes Franciscus vrouw geboren **Vermandel Ida** … 123"), then read by
stitching the FamilySearch deep-zoom tiles at full resolution on the
`sg30p0.familysearch.org` origin (descriptor 6304 × 4224, tile 256, overlap 1, max
level 13). The crop saved here is the whole of act 123 at 1:1.

Transcribed as far as the hand is certain (uncertain readings flagged):

> In het jaar achttien honderd vyf en zestig, den **twee en twintigsten Juny** om elf uren
> voor middag. Voor ons **Jacques Johannes Vertongen**, Burgemeester, ambtenaer des
> burgerlyken stands der gemeente **Hamme**, provintie Oost-Vlaenderen, zyn gekomen
> **Norbertus Joannes Stabbaert**, oud acht en dertig jaren, *byzonderen*, en
> **Petrus Seraphinus Dieriex**, oud een en vyftig jaren, *veldwachter*, beide wonende te
> Hamme, kennissen des overledene, dewelke ons hebben verklaerd dat **gisteren, den een en

> **huisvrouw van Joannes Franciscus Van Bergen**, **dochter van nog levende
> [P]rimus Vermandel** en van **[D/J]u[s]phina Verplancken**, gehuisschene(?), overleden
> is in het **burgerhospitael van het vrouwelyk geslacht** gestaen alhier aen het
> **marktplein**. En hebben wy dezen act met beide declaranten geteekend na gedane
> voorlezing.

Signed: N. Stabbaert · P. S. Dieriex · Vertongen.

What it establishes, independently of any tree:

- **Ida Vermandel died at Hamme (Oost-Vlaanderen) on 21 June 1865**, in the women's
  *burgerhospitaal* on the Marktplein; the act is nr. **123** of 22 June 1865. The
  year-only `1865` already on her record is now day-exact, and the place is stated.
- She was **39**, so born about **1825–1826** — the `1825` already held is inside the
  slack an age-in-years carries, and is not exactly confirmed by it.
- She was **born at Zelzate** (*Zeltzaete*), not at Waasmunster or Hamme. Vermandel is a
  Meetjesland surname (Zelzate, Assenede, Bassevelde) and this is consistent.
- Her trade was ***arbeidster*** (day-labourer).
- She was **the wife of Joannes Franciscus Van Bergen** — the spelling in the Hamme
  register is **Van Bergen**, filed in the tafel under Van-a, Van-B(ergen), Van-Bochstael,
  Van-Bogaert…, so it is *not* Van den Berghe here.
- **HER PARENTS ARE NAMED**, and both were *still living* in June 1865:
  **[P]rimus Vermandel** and **[?]phina Verplancken**. The father's forename reads
  "Primus"; the mother's reads something like "Dyusphina"/"Jusphina" and is most probably
  **Josephina** or **Delphina** — the capital is genuinely ambiguous in this hand and is
  recorded here as read, not resolved. **Neither name should be grafted from this act
  alone**: the confirming document is the Zelzate birth act of Ida Vermandel, c. 1825-26
  (FamilySearch, Zelzate *Geboorten 1816-1840*, DGS 004670480, with the ten-year table
  1802-1870 at DGS 005089047 as the index into it).

Read with care: "Primus" is an unusual forename and the initial letter is written over a
column rule; "gehuisschene" (spouses) is a guess at a heavily abbreviated word. The
month column of the register reads *Juny* and the year is spelled out in the act itself,
so the 1865 dating does not depend on the film's title.

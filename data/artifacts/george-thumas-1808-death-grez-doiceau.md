than through an index.

**What it confirms.** The death date, 20 November 1808 at Grez-Doiceau, exactly as held. The
occupation *menuisier* — the word this tree already carried, which is a check in itself. And
three links that rested on the m2155 tree: his father [[jeanlambert_t]], his mother
[[leclercq_t]], and his wife [[noel_t]], who is written *Noé* here rather than Noël.

**One thing it disagrees with, and the disagreement is left standing.** The act gives his
age as **60**, which puts his birth around November 1747–1748. This tree records
**14 March 1744**, which would have made him 64. Both cannot be right.

The birth date has not been changed, for a reason: an age in a death act is hearsay — here
it was supplied by Jean Henry Lacourt, a 32-year-old day-labourer, about a man who died at
60, and ages in these registers are routinely out by a few years. A stated birth date, even
one from a member tree, is a claim about a specific record; a reported age is a
recollection. Neither is documented until the baptism is read.

FRONTIER: the Grez-Doiceau baptism register for 1744 and for 1747–48. Whichever year holds a
George Thumas son of Lambert and Marie Leclercq settles it, and would be the first properly
documented birth on this line.

**The act date is not the death date.** The register entry is dated 21 November; the act
itself states the death as the 20th. Searching AGATHA by year returns the registration date,
so an act can look a day off when it is not.

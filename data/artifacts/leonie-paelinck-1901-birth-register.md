**The register page, not the index entry about it.** Sint-Niklaas births for 1901, image
268 of 337, four acts to the opening; act **nr. 997** is the lower right, headed in the
margin *Philomena Leonia Paelinck*.

What can be read in the act's own hand:

- the child's names given at the end as **Philomena Leonia**;
- the father **Eduardus Franciscus Paelinck**, described as *geboortig te Belsele* — born at
  Belsele — and living in the Kreitstraat;
- the mother **Maria Magdalena Van Bogaert**, *huishoudster*;
- the officer **Michel De Smet, schepen**, and the same signature closing the neighbouring
  acts on the opening.

**The father's birthplace is the part worth noting.** [[eduardus_p]] is recorded here as
born at Belsele on 19 November 1858, on the stefpaelinck tree's word. The act says Belsele
independently, in 1901, from the man himself standing before the registrar. That is a second
identifier agreeing, and it is what makes this a safe identification rather than a name match.

**This supersedes [[leonie-paelinck-1901-birth-sint-niklaas]]**, which captured FamilySearch's
index card for the same act. The index gave the date and the two parents; the register gives
the act as written, the father's birthplace, the mother's occupation and the officiating
schepen. Three records move from `sup` to `doc` on the strength of it, because the image has
now actually been read.

Not everything on the page is legible at this resolution — the stated ages in particular are
not, and none is recorded. Reading them would need the zoomed tiles.

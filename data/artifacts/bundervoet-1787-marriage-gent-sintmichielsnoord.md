The register itself, not an index or an extract. Marginal heading **Bundervoet / peeters**.

> **Die 17 aprilis** praeviis tribus bannis matrimonium contraxerunt **Ægidius Bundervoet**
> natus in **B[eatae]: Mariae Gande** habitans in hac parochia et **Anna Maria Elisabetha
> Peeters** nata in **Horn prope Ruremundam** habitans in hac parochia, scribere non
> valens, coram me infrascripto et testibus **Livino Bundervoet** nato in **B: Mariae**
> habitante in hac parochia et **Carolina Gravelijn** nata in St[o] … habitante in hac
> parochia.
>
> Signed: **gillis bundervoet** — Signatura ✗ **anna maria elizabeth peeters** —
> **Lieven bundervoet** — **Carolinne Gravelijn** — J. J. De Witte, pastor S<sup>ti</sup>
> Michaelis.

What it gives:

- **The date: 17 April 1787**, after three banns. The Geneanet index had only the year.
- **Egidius's birth parish** — *natus in B. Mariae Gande*, the Onze-Lieve-Vrouw parish of
  Gent. His son's baptism the following year calls him *ex Sancti Petri*; these are the same
  parish (Onze-Lieve-Vrouw Sint-Pieters), so the two acts confirm each other independently.
- **The bride's birthplace, precisely: Horn, near Roermond.** The 1788 baptism says only
  *ruremondensis*. *Horn prope Ruremundam* is a village, and therefore a register to search.
- **She could not write** — *scribere non valens* — and signed with a cross, while he signed
  his own name.
- **A Bundervoet witness**: *Livino Bundervoet nato in B: Mariae habitante in hac parochia*
  — born in the groom's own birth parish, living in the groom's own parish, and godfather to
  the couple's first child a year later. See [[bundervoet_livinus_gent]] for why that is
  still not a link.

**What it does NOT give: the parents.** This older Flemish form records origins and
witnesses, and names a father only in a consent clause when a party is a minor — the act
immediately below this one, and the De Potter act on the facing page, both show that clause.
Egidius and his bride were of age, so no parents are named. The generation above the Gent
patriarchs therefore remains open, and the act that would close it is Egidius's own baptism
in Onze-Lieve-Vrouw / Sint-Pieters.

**The signature discrepancy is real and is recorded, not resolved.** *Lieven bundervoet*
signs here in a fluent hand; in the baptism register a year later a Livinus Bundervoet
*declared he could not write*. Either two men share the name in one parish — which the Gent
registers make entirely possible — or one of the two clerks was careless.

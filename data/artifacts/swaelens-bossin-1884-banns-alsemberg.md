**THE REGISTER IMAGE WAS READ.** The opening carries Alsemberg banns acts nrs. 6–10 on the
left page and nrs. 11–15 on the right; only 7 and 8 concern this couple. The printed
heading of every box on this page reads **AKTE VAN HUWELIJKSAFKONDIGING** — a *banns
publication*, not a marriage.

This is the page behind the two FamilySearch index rows that research-log §72 recorded as a
conflicting "marriage, 17 February 1884, Alsemberg". Both rows (`1:1:6TRK-Q4PF` and
`1:1:6TRK-HCJS`) point at this one image.

## Act nr. 7 — the first publication

> Op heden zondag den **tienden februarij** duizend achthonderd vier en tachtig, ten **tien**
> ure **vóór** middag, hebben Wij **Augustinus Guilielmus Winderickx, Burgemeester**,
> Ambtenaar van den Burgerlijken Stand der gemeente **Alsemberg**, Arrondissement
> **Brussel**, Provincie Brabant, ons voor de voornaamste deur des ingangs van ons
> gemeentehuis begeven hebbende, aldaar **voor de eerste maal afgekondigd** en bekend
> gemaakt, dat er belofte van huwelijk is tusschen **Joannes Baptista Julianus Swaelens**,
> *schrijnwerker*, wonende te **Alsemberg**, meerderjarige zoon van **Egidius Swaelens**,
> *hovenier*, wonende te **Braken-Eigen** [Eigenbrakel = Braine-l'Alleud], en van **Joanna
> Ackermans, overleden**. En **Ludovica Bossin**, zonder beroep, wonende te **Crainhem**,
> meerderjarige dochter van **Guilielmus Bossin** en van **Joanna Maria Peremans**,
> *landbouwers*, wonende buiten te **Crainhem**.

## Act nr. 8 — the second publication

Identical in every particular except the date and the ordinal: **zondag den zeventienden
februarij** 1884, **voor de tweede maal afgekondigd**; and the bride's mother is here
called "Joanna Maria Peremans, landbouwers, **zijne echtgenoote**".

## What this settles, and what it opens

**Settles:** the "17 February 1884, Alsemberg" record is the *second banns publication*, not
a marriage. The marriage is the Kraainem civil act of 25 February 1884
([[swaelens-bossin-1884-marriage-kraainem]]), which itself names both publication dates.
The two are not rival dates for one event; they are two different events in the ordinary
Belgian sequence — banns on two successive Sundays, act on the following Monday.

**Opens:** the bride's mother is written **Joanna Maria Peremans** here, against **Anna
Catharina Peremans** on the Kraainem marriage act eight days later and **Joanna Catharina
Peremans** on her son's 1887 Kraainem act. Three forms of the same woman's middle name in
three acts; the Alsemberg clerk had the couple's details second-hand, which is the obvious
explanation, but it is recorded rather than resolved here.

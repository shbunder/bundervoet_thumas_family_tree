**The register image, read** — the open spread of the Hamme 1880 death register, zoomed
to the lower half. The act that matters is the left-hand column, entry **nr. 126,
"Bocklandt Ludovicus"**. Transcribed as far as the hand is certain:

> In 't jaar achttien honderd tachtig, den negensten Juni … voor middag. Voor ons
> Jacques Johannes Vertongen, Burgemeester, ambtenaar des burgerlijken stands van
> **Hamme**, zijn verschenen **Eduardus Bocklandt**, oud **veertig** jaren, *zeeldraaier*,
> en Petrus Joannes Van Goe[y]e, oud vijftig jaren, zeeldraaier, beide wonende te Hamme,
> respectievelijk **vader** en gebuur des overleden kind, dewelke ons hebben verklaard dat
> gisteren om elf ure 's morgends overleden is in zijne ouders woning
> **Ludovicus Bocklandt**, oud omtrent **vijf jaren**, geboren en wonende te Hamme, zoon
> van den eersten declarant **Eduardus Bocklandt** en van **Maria Louisa Van Bergen**,
> oud **negen en dertig** jaren, *spinster*, … wonende alhier in 't Kleinsmiske. …

What it establishes, independently of any tree or index:

- **Eduardus Bocklandt and Maria Louisa Van Bergen were a married couple living at Hamme
  (Oost-Vlaanderen) in 1880**, with children born there. Louise Marie Bocklandt was born
  at Hamme on 31 December 1877, between this child and the family's move to Oostende.
- **Eduardus was 40** in June 1880, so born about 1839–1840 — the record holds 1839.
- **Maria Louisa was 39**, so born about 1840–1841 — the record holds 1842. Within the
  slack an age-in-years statement carries, but not an exact confirmation.
- His trade at Hamme was *zeeldraaier* (ropemaker); at Oostende in 1888 the death act of
  his wife calls him *lijndraaiersknecht*, the same trade. That continuity is itself an
  identifier.
- A further child of the couple: **Ludovicus Bocklandt**, born Hamme about 1875, died in
  his parents' house at Hamme on 8 June 1880, aged about five.

The two ages are read from a nineteenth-century clerical hand and should be treated as
read-with-care rather than as certain: "veertig" and "negen en dertig" are legible but
the surrounding numerals in this register are cramped.

**The most useful thing here may be the film, not the act.** FamilySearch holds the
Hamme (O-Vl) civil registers as browsable images — this film alone runs to 1,082 images
and covers deaths 1880-1888, marriages 1881-1888 and births 1887-1888. Neither Open
Archives nor AGATHA's volunteer index has any Hamme civil registration at all, which is
why every earlier search for this family died. The Hamme birth register for 1877 and the
marriage register for the 1860s are the neighbouring films.

**This is the index analysis, not the act image.**

Found while sweeping `naam=Wyllie` for the 1803 act, and it turns out to matter more than a
by-catch. `brigitte_wyllie` did not stay a widow: ten years after Antoine's death she
married again, and the act says so in its own Previous-Partners table.

| | Groom | Bride |
|---|---|---|
| name | **LOOTEN François Joseph** | **WYLLIE Brigitte Josephine Jeanne** |
| birth date | 17/08/1785 | 22/10/1779 |
| place of birth | Rexpoede, Dép. du Nord | Diksmuide |
| age | 33 | 38 |
| residence | Diksmuide | Diksmuide |
| profession | *timmerman* | — |
| civil status | — | **weduwe** |
| father | Jeremie Jacques Looten, d. 20/08/1804 (2 Fructidor XII) Stavele | François Antoine Wyllie, **d. 30/09/1811 Diksmuide** |
| mother | Anne Therese Deprez, d. 12/04/1792 Rexpoede | Catharine Deman, **d. 31/12/1800 (10 Nivôse IX) Diksmuide** |

**Previous partners of the bride, as transcribed:**

| Name | Given name | Place of decease | Date of decease |
|---|---|---|---|
| VANALDERWEIRELDT | Antoine | Diksmuide | **06/07/1808** |

That row is what makes the identification, rather than the name agreeing. The bride of 1818
is named as the widow of an Antoine Vanalderweireldt who died at Diksmuide in 1808, and her
parents are the same François Antoine Wyllie × Deman as in the 1803 act.

Both Republican conversions check out by computation: 2 Fructidor XII = 20 Aug 1804,
10 Nivôse IX = 31 Dec 1800.

**It also puts a third date on Antoine's death.** 06/07/1808 here; 16/07/1808 in his son
Petrus Antonius's 1828 act; 21/07/1808 in his son Carolus's 1828 act, which is also the
*certificate* date of the death act itself. Three transcriptions, three dates, and no image
read — so the death day is open, not settled.

She had at least four children by Looten: Catharina (d. Diksmuide 1823), Renatus Norbertus
(d. Diksmuide 1825, weeks after his mother), Ludovica Antonia (m. 1842) and Maria Ludovica
(m. 1843) — all naming Looten Franciscus Josephus × Wyllie Brigitta Josephina as parents.

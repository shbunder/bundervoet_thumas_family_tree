The record page as AGATHA presents it. What it states:

- **Kind:** Jérôme Josephus Dekeyser, born Oostende, 14/06/1897
- **Vader:** Eduardus Dekeyser · **Moeder:** Louisa Maria Bocklandt
- **Opmerking:** *Gewettigd 4 5 1901*

That marginal note is the whole value of this document. Legitimization on 4 May 1901
is what dates Édouard and Louise's marriage, which no act of the marriage itself has
ever been found for — the Oostende register that would hold it is at the
Stadsarchief, not the Rijksarchief, and is not digitised.

**This is the archive's index page, not the act image.** AGATHA's transcription is
authoritative enough to cite, but the scan itself was not reachable, so anything
resting on this is only as good as the transcription. It also carries the spelling
"Dekeyser" as one word, which is how the Oostende line appears throughout.

**Not the parish register itself, but a certified extract from it** — issued by the Mairie
de Gand and filed as a supplement to the Rotterdam civil marriage of 25 November 1818,
because a Dutch marriage required the groom's birth certificate and the groom was born
abroad. The heading is printed and French; the act body is the parish clerk's Latin.

> Extrait du registre de Naissances de **L'Église de St. Martin à Gand** de l'an
> Mil sept cent quatre vingt huit
>
> **Vigesima secunda aprilis 1788 Baptizavi Livinum Cornelium filium Ægidii Bundervoet
> ex S[anc]ti Petri et annæ mariæ Elizabethæ Peeters ruremondensis conjugum, natum heri
> vesperi quadrante post undecimam. Susceperunt Livinus Bundervoet qui dixit se non posse
> scribere et Angela Van der Schuren.**
>
> signés C.J.J. de Rousse Vice-p[astor], **Gillis Bundervoet**, d'un marque pour signature
> du parrain, et Angelique Van der Schuren.
>
> Pour extrait conforme délivré à la Mairie de Gand.

Read at 725×1024, the largest the Rotterdam viewer serves, with the Latin body re-read at
3× on an upscaled crop. Four things it settles:

- **The date. Baptised 22 April 1788, born the evening before** — *natum heri vesperi
  quadrante post undecimam*, at a quarter past eleven on **21 April 1788**. Four Rotterdam
  population-register entries give his birth as *22 May 1788*; they are wrong by a month.
  The Rotterdam death act of 1872 independently agrees with the act rather than the
  registers: it gives his age as 83 years and 10 months on 28 February 1872, which computes
  to April 1788.
- **The father is Egidius, not Petrus.** Both Rotterdam civil records name the groom's
  father *Petrus Bundervoet*. The baptism act names him *Ægidius*, and he signs the
  register in the Flemish form, **Gillis Bundervoet**. The Rotterdam clerk was wrong.
- **Both parents' origins.** *Ægidii Bundervoet **ex Sancti Petri*** — of Gent's
  Sint-Pieters parish, which is where the Bundervoet surname is thickest in the Gent
  registers. *Annæ Mariæ Elizabethæ Peeters **ruremondensis*** — of **Roermond**, in
  Limburg, which is why her surname is not a Gent one.
- **A godfather who is kin.** *Susceperunt **Livinus Bundervoet** qui dixit se non posse
  scribere* — Livinus Bundervoet stood godfather and declared he could not write, so the
  register carries a mark for him instead of a signature. Godparents in a Flemish baptism
  register are almost always family, so this is the nearest thing yet to the generation
  above the Gent patriarch Egidius.

The vice-pastor signs C.J.J. de Rousse. The godmother is written *Angela* in the Latin and
*Angelique* in the French subscription; neither form is preferred here over the other.

The date is given in both calendars — *08/06/1803 (19 Prairial XI)* — and the conversion
checks out by computation: 1 Prairial XI = 21 May 1803, so 19 Prairial XI = 8 June 1803.

**The groom's birth date matches this tree exactly** (`antoine_vanald`, 1780-05-09).

**The bride's does not.** This tree carries 1779-02-22 from a member tree; the act says
22/10/1779, and her 1818 remarriage act says 22/10/1779 as well. Against that, the age
stated here — 24 on 8 June 1803 — fits a February 1779 birth and *not* an October one,
where she would still have been 23. The two acts agree with each other and disagree with
their own stated age, so this is left as a conflict, not a correction.

Both fathers are recorded as born at **Langemark** — an oddity worth stating plainly rather
than smoothing over, since the field may in practice hold a residence.

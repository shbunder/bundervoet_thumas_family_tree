The richest act found in this run: it carries **four ancestors this tree did not have**,
two of them with their own death dates.

| role | person | detail |
|---|---|---|
| Bruidegom | **Guillielmus Bossin** | b. Sint-Stevens-Woluwe · age **21** · *arbeider* · "heeft voldaan aan zijn militieverplichtingen" |
| Vader van de bruidegom | **Arnoldus Bossin** | Sint-Stevens-Woluwe · *arbeider* · present and consenting · **cannot write** |
| Moeder van de bruidegom | **Elisabeth Deyn** | Sint-Stevens-Woluwe · *arbeidster* · present and consenting · **cannot write** |
| Bruid | **Joanna Catharina Jacoba Peremans** | b. Zaventem · age **21** · *arbeidster* |
| Vader van de bruid | **Egidius Peremans** | died Zaventem **6 March 1837** |
| Moeder van de bruid | **Joanna Theresia Ver Elst** | died Zaventem **19 December 1843** |

Banns were read at Zaventem and Sint-Stevens-Woluwe on 8 and 15 February 1846. The first
and second witnesses could not write either.

**It settles a name dispute.** The 1872 Kraainem act ([[S11]]) called the bride's mother
*Anna* Catharina Peremans, while this tree carried *Joanna Catharina Jacoba*. Her own
marriage act, twenty-six years earlier, gives the full form — so the tree was right and
the 1872 act was abbreviating, which is what these acts routinely do to a parent's name.

**Both ages check out.** Bride and groom are each 21 in February 1846, putting both births
in 1824–25; the tree records 1824 for both, from a member tree. Two independent
confirmations at once.

**A line noted, because it is the kind of thing a register rarely says.** Of the bride:
*"Ze verklaart samen met de getuigen dat zij haar grootouders niet heeft gekend noch de
plaats van hun overlijden"* — she declared, with the witnesses, that she had never known
her grandparents nor where they died. Her own parents were already dead, in 1837 and 1843.
An orphan of twenty-one marrying a labourer's son whose parents came and signed with a mark.

**What is not here.** Neither Arnoldus Bossin nor Elisabeth Deyn is given a date, and
Egidius Peremans and Joanna Theresia Ver Elst are given only their deaths. The act was read
as AGATHA's transcription, not as the register image.
